# 📖 Crossing Borders: Immigrant Ancestors and Revolutionary Heroes

A chronologically-organized book telling the stories of ancestors who immigrated to North America and those who played significant roles in shaping its history.

## Project Overview

### Goal
Create a comprehensive, narrative-driven book documenting the immigrant ancestors who came to Quebec, with historical context and engaging storytelling.

### Status
- **Phase**: Planning & Initial Research
- **Started**: 2025-12-05
- **Target Completion**: TBD
- **Current Focus**: Setting up structure, researching first ancestors

### Scope
- **Organization**: Organized into parts (Immigrant Era, Revolutionary Era, etc.)
- **Detail Level**: Mixed approach (full chapters for major figures, profiles for secondary)
- **Historical Context**: Dedicated chapters + woven into narratives + appendices
- **Geographic Coverage**: Quebec, New England, and connections between them
- **Estimated Length**: 300-400 pages
