# Immigrant Ancestor Tracking

Master list of all ancestors who immigrated to Quebec, organized chronologically for book planning.

---

## Decision Criteria

### Full Chapter (8-15 pages)
- ✅ Significant historical importance
- ✅ Rich documentation available
- ✅ Compelling personal story
- ✅ Major family line (direct ancestor)
- ✅ Key to understanding family history

### Profile (2-4 pages)
- Limited documentation
- Collateral line or indirect connection
- Interesting but not central to narrative
- Worth including but not emphasizing
