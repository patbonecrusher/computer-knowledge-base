## Decision Criteria

### Full Chapter vs Profile

**Full Chapter (8-15 pages)**:
- Major historical significance
- Rich documentation available
- Compelling personal story
- Direct ancestor (not collateral)
- Key to understanding family history
- Founding ancestor of family line

**Profile (2-4 pages)**:
- Less documentation
- Collateral line
- Interesting but not central
- Worth including but not emphasizing

### All five current ancestors warrant full chapters because:
- Matthias I: Founding immigrant, original proprietor
- Louis Badaillac: Carignan-Salières soldier, father of Detroit founder
- Matthias II: Tragic death shapes son's story
- Claude-Mathias: Sole ancestor of all Phaneufs (unique!)
- Clément: Extraordinary Revolutionary War service, spy for Washington
