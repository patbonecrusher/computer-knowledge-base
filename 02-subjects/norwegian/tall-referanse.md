## Mønster og Regler (Patterns and Rules)

### How Numbers Are Formed

1. **0-12**: Unique words (must be memorized)
2. **13-19**: Base number + "ten" sound
   - Example: tre + tten = tretten (13)

3. **20, 30, 40...**: Unique base words for each ten
   - 20: tjue
   - 30: tretti
   - 40: førti
   - etc.

4. **21-99**: Combine tens + ones (no "and")
   - Pattern: [tens] + [ones]
   - Example: tjue + fire = tjuefire (24)
   - Example: førti + seks = førtiseks (46)

### Important Notes

**"En" vs "Ett":**
- **en** is used with common gender nouns: en bil (one car)
- **ett** is used with neuter nouns: ett hus (one house)
- When counting, "en" is typically used

**"Sju" vs "Syv":**
- Both mean "seven"
- "Sju" is more common in speech
- "Syv" is more formal/written
- Both are correct!

**"Sytti" vs "Søtti":**
- Both mean "seventy"
- "Sytti" is more common
- "Søtti" is a regional variant
- Both are correct!

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

### Asking About Numbers

- **Hvor mange?** - How many?
- **Hvilket nummer?** - Which number?
- **Hva er nummeret ditt?** - What is your number?
- **Kan du telle?** - Can you count?

### Using Numbers

- **Jeg er tjuefem år gammel** - I am 25 years old
- **Det koster femti kroner** - It costs 50 kroner
- **Jeg bor på nummer førtito** - I live at number 42
- **Vi er tretti personer** - We are 30 people

## Tips for Memorisering (Memorization Tips)

1. **Master 0-20 first** - These are the building blocks
2. **Learn the tens** (20, 30, 40...) - Only 9 words to memorize
3. **Practice the pattern** - Once you know tens + ones, you can make any number
4. **Count daily activities** - Count stairs, items, minutes
5. **Practice out loud** - Helps with pronunciation and memory
