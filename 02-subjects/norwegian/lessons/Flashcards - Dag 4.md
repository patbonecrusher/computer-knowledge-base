# Flashcards - Dag 4: Modale verb og muligheter

## Modale Verb (Modal Verbs)

kan
?
can (ability/permission)
**Example**: Jeg **kan** snakke norsk (I can speak Norwegian)

må
?
must / have to (obligation)
**Example**: Jeg **må** gå nå (I must go now)

skal
?
shall / going to (future plans)
**Example**: Jeg **skal** bo i Oslo (I'm going to live in Oslo)

vil
?
will / want to (desire/willingness)
**Example**: Jeg **vil** ha kaffe (I want coffee)

bør
?
should (advice/recommendation)
**Example**: Du **bør** snakke norsk (You should speak Norwegian)

## Modal Verb Usage

jeg kan
?
I can
**Example**: Jeg kan hjelpe deg (I can help you)
**Note**: Same form for all persons!

jeg må
?
I must / I have to
**Example**: Jeg må gjøre lekser (I must do homework)

jeg skal
?
I'm going to / I shall
**Example**: Jeg skal gå i morgen (I'm going to go tomorrow)

jeg vil
?
I want to / I will
**Example**: Jeg vil lære norsk (I want to learn Norwegian)

jeg bør
?
I should
**Example**: Jeg bør øve (I should practice)

## Nyttige Verb - Infinitiv (Useful Verbs - Infinitive)

å hjelpe
?
to help
**Example**: Jeg kan **hjelpe** deg (I can help you)

å lære
?

**Example**: Jeg vil **lære** norsk (I want to learn Norwegian)

å arbeide
?
to work
**Example**: Jeg må **arbeide** i dag (I have to work today)

å spise
?
to eat
**Example**: Vi skal **spise** mat (We're going to eat food)

å drikke
?
to drink
**Example**: Vil du **drikke** kaffe? (Do you want to drink coffee?)

å lese
?

**Example**: Du bør **lese** boken (You should read the book)

å skrive
?
to write
**Example**: Jeg må **skrive** lekser (I have to write homework)

å forstå
?

**Example**: Kan du **forstå** norsk? (Can you understand Norwegian?)

## Setninger med Modale Verb (Sentences with Modal Verbs)

Jeg kan snakke norsk
?
I can speak Norwegian
**Pattern**: kan + infinitive (without å)

Du må gjøre lekser
?
You must do homework / You have to do homework
**Pattern**: må + infinitive

Vi skal gå i morgen
?
We're going to go tomorrow / We shall go tomorrow
**Pattern**: skal + infinitive

Vil du ha kaffe?
?
Do you want coffee?
**Pattern**: vil + ha (to have/want)

Hun bør lære norsk
?
She should learn Norwegian
**Pattern**: bør + infinitive

Kan jeg hjelpe deg?
?
Can I help you?
**Pattern**: Question = modal + subject + infinitive

Jeg kan ikke snakke norsk
?
I can't speak Norwegian / I cannot speak Norwegian
**Pattern**: modal + ikke + infinitive

Skal vi gå?
?
Shall we go?
**Usage**: Common way to make suggestions

Jeg må gå nå
?
I must go now / I have to go now
**Common phrase for leaving**

Vi skal spise mat
?
We're going to eat food
**Pattern**: skal + spise

## Viktig Forskjell (Important Difference)

må ikke
?
must not (prohibition)
**Example**: Du **må ikke** gå der! (You must not go there!)
**Note**: This is a prohibition, not "don't have to"!

trenger ikke å
?
don't have to (no obligation)
**Example**: Du **trenger ikke å** gå (You don't have to go)
**Note**: This means it's optional, not forbidden

## Spørsmål med Modale Verb (Questions with Modal Verbs)

Kan du snakke norsk?
?
Can you speak Norwegian?
**Pattern**: Modal + subject + infinitive?

Må du gå nå?
?
Do you have to go now? / Must you go now?
**Usage**: Asking about obligation

Skal du arbeide i morgen?
?
Are you going to work tomorrow?
**Usage**: Asking about future plans

Vil du ha kaffe?
?
Do you want coffee?
**Very common question at cafés**

Hva skal du gjøre?
?
What are you going to do?
**Common question about plans**

Når må du gå?
?
When do you have to go?
**Asking about timing of obligation**

Hvor kan jeg bo?
?
Where can I live?
**Asking about possibility**

## Nektelse (Negation)

Jeg kan ikke forstå
?
I can't understand / I cannot understand
**Pattern**: modal + ikke + infinitive

Vi skal ikke komme
?
We're not going to come
**Pattern**: skal + ikke + infinitive

Hun vil ikke ha te
?
She doesn't want tea / She won't have tea
**Pattern**: vil + ikke + infinitive

Du bør ikke gjøre det
?
You shouldn't do that
**Pattern**: bør + ikke + infinitive

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

?

?

**Asking if it's necessary**

Vi skal se
?
We shall see / We'll see
**Common expression for "we'll find out"**

Vil du ha mer?
?

**Offering more of something**

Du bør øve

**Checking comprehension**

## Kombinasjoner (Combinations)

Jeg vil kunne snakke norsk
?
I want to be able to speak Norwegian
**Pattern**: vil + kunne (infinitive of kan)
**Note**: Can't use two modals together - use infinitive form of second!

Jeg må lære å forstå
?
I have to learn to understand
**Pattern**: må + lære + å + infinitive
**Note**: "lære" is not modal, so it takes "å"!
