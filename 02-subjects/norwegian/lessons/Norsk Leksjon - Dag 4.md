# Norsk Leksjon - Dag 4

**Tema: Modale verb og muligheter**

---

## Del 1: Ordforråd (5 minutter)

### Modale verb (Modal verbs)

- **kan** - can (ability/permission)
- **må** - must / have to (obligation)
- **skal** - shall / going to (future plans)
- **vil** - will / want to (desire/willingness)
- **bør** - should (advice)

### Nyttige verb (infinitiv) (Useful verbs - infinitive)

- **å hjelpe** - to help
- **å lære** - to learn
- **å arbeide** - to work
- **å spise** - to eat
- **å drikke** - to drink
- **å lese** - to read
- **å skrive** - to write
- **å forstå** - to understand

### Nyttige ord (Useful words)

- **en kaffe** (m) - a coffee
- **en te** (m) - a tea
- **en mat** (m) - food
- **en bok** (m/f) - a book
- **leksene** (plural) - homework (singular: **en lekse** - m)
- **en hjelp** (m/f) - help
- **en tid** (m) - time
- **et pass** (n) - a passport
- **nå** - now (adverb)
- **senere** - later (adverb)
- **alltid** - always (adverb)
- **aldri** - never (adverb)
- **ofte** - often (adverb)

## Del 2: Grammatikk (7 minutter)

### Modale verb + infinitiv

Modal verbs are followed by the infinitive **without "å"**:

**Pattern**: Subject + modal verb + infinitive

**Eksempel**: Jeg **kan snakke** norsk. (I can speak Norwegian.)

### kan (can - ability/permission)

All persons use the same form: **kan**

- Jeg **kan** snakke norsk. (I can speak Norwegian.)
- Du **kan** hjelpe meg. (You can help me.)
- Han **kan** lære norsk. (He can learn Norwegian.)
- **Kan jeg** gå hjem? (Can I go home?)

**Uses:**
- Ability: Jeg kan snakke engelsk. (I can speak English.)
- Permission: Kan jeg gå nå? (Can I go now?)
- Possibility: Det kan være sant. (It can be true.)

### må (must / have to - obligation)

All persons use the same form: **må**

- Jeg **må** gå nå. (I must go now / I have to go now.)
- Du **må** gjøre lekser. (You must do homework.)
- Vi **må** snakke norsk. (We must speak Norwegian.)

**Uses:**
- Obligation: Jeg må arbeide. (I have to work.)
- Necessity: Du må ha pass. (You must have a passport.)

### skal (shall / going to - future plans)

All persons use the same form: **skal**

- Jeg **skal** gå til skolen i morgen. (I'm going to go to school tomorrow.)
- Vi **skal** spise mat. (We're going to eat food.)
- **Skal vi** gå? (Shall we go?)

**Uses:**
- Future plans: Jeg skal bo i Oslo. (I'm going to live in Oslo.)
- Suggestions: Skal vi snakke norsk? (Shall we speak Norwegian?)

### vil (will / want to - desire/willingness)

All persons use the same form: **vil**

- Jeg **vil** snakke norsk. (I want to speak Norwegian.)
- **Vil du ha** kaffe? (Do you want coffee?)
- Vi **vil** lære norsk. (We want to learn Norwegian.)

**Uses:**
- Wanting: Jeg vil ha kaffe. (I want coffee.)
- Willingness: Jeg vil hjelpe deg. (I'll help you.)

### bør (should - advice)

All persons use the same form: **bør**

- Du **bør** snakke norsk. (You should speak Norwegian.)
- Jeg **bør** gjøre lekser. (I should do homework.)
- Vi **bør** gå nå. (We should go now.)

**Uses:**
- Advice: Du bør lese boken. (You should read the book.)
- Recommendations: Vi bør arbeide. (We should work.)

### Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the modal verb:

- Jeg **kan ikke** snakke norsk. (I can't speak Norwegian.)
- Du **må ikke** gå! (You must not go!)
- Vi **skal ikke** komme. (We're not going to come.)
- Hun **vil ikke** ha kaffe. (She doesn't want coffee.)
- Jeg **bør ikke** gjøre det. (I shouldn't do that.)

**Viktig!** (Important!)
- **må ikke** = must not (prohibition)
- **trenger ikke å** = don't have to (no obligation)

**Eksempler:**

- Du **må ikke** gå. (You must not go. - forbidden!)
- Du **trenger ikke å** gå. (You don't have to go. - optional)
- Jeg vil ha **en kaffe** (m). (I want a coffee.)
- Du bør lese **boken** (the book - definite form of **en bok**).

## Del 3: Øvelser (8 minutter)

### Øvelse 1: Fyll inn riktig modalt verb (kan, må, skal, vil, bør)

1. Jeg _____ snakke engelsk. (I can speak English.)
2. Du _____ gjøre lekser nå. (You must do homework now.)
3. Vi _____ gå til skolen i morgen. (We're going to go to school tomorrow.)
4. _____ du ha kaffe? (Do you want coffee?)
5. Han _____ lære norsk. (He should learn Norwegian.)

### Øvelse 2: Oversett til norsk

1. I can help you.
2. We must speak Norwegian.
3. Are you going to work tomorrow? (use "skal")
4. She wants to live in Oslo.
5. You should read the book.

### Øvelse 3: Nektelse - Gjør setningene negative

1. Jeg kan snakke norsk. → _____
2. Du må gå nå. → _____
3. Vi skal komme i morgen. → _____
4. Hun vil ha kaffe. → _____
5. De bør gjøre det. → _____

### Øvelse 4: Velg riktig verb (må ikke / trenger ikke å)

1. Du _____ gå hvis du ikke vil. (You don't have to go if you don't want to.)
2. Du _____ gå der! Det er farlig! (You must not go there! It's dangerous!)
3. Vi _____ snakke hvis vi ikke har lyst. (We don't have to speak if we don't feel like it.)
4. Dere _____ gjøre det! (You must not do that!)

### Øvelse 5: Svar på spørsmålene

1. Kan du snakke norsk?
2. Hva skal du gjøre i morgen?
3. Vil du ha kaffe eller te?
4. Hva må du gjøre i dag?

## Kulturnotat (Cultural note)

Norwegians are generally quite direct in their communication. When using modal verbs like **må** (must) and **skal** (going to), they are stating facts rather than being pushy. This directness is considered honest and efficient, not rude. Similarly, saying **"Jeg vil ha kaffe"** (I want coffee) at a café is perfectly polite - you don't need to soften it with "Jeg vil gjerne ha" (I would like to have) unless you want to be extra polite.

## Grammatikknotat (Grammar note)

### Modal verb vs regular verb with "å"

**Modal verbs** (kan, må, skal, vil, bør) are followed by infinitive **without "å"**:
- Jeg kan **snakke** norsk. ✅
- Jeg kan **å snakke** norsk. ❌

**Other verbs** that take infinitives use **"å"**:
- Jeg liker **å snakke** norsk. ✅ (I like to speak Norwegian.)
- Jeg trenger **å gå**. ✅ (I need to go.)
- Jeg begynner **å forstå**. ✅ (I'm beginning to understand.)

**Quick test**: If it's one of the 5 main modals (kan, må, skal, vil, bør), drop the "å"!

## Lekse til i morgen:

1. Memorize the 5 modal verbs and what they mean
2. Practice making sentences with each modal verb
3. Remember: modals + infinitive WITHOUT "å"
4. Practice the difference between "må ikke" (must not) and "trenger ikke å" (don't have to)

**Eksempel:**
- Jeg **kan** snakke norsk. (I can speak Norwegian.)
- Jeg **må** gjøre lekser. (I must do homework.)
- Jeg **skal** gå til skolen i morgen. (I'm going to go to school tomorrow.)
- Jeg **vil** lære mer. (I want to learn more.)
- Jeg **bør** øve hver dag. (I should practice every day.)
