# Norsk Leksjon - Dag 5

**Tema: Familie og eierord**

---

## Del 1: Ordforråd (5 minutter)

### Familie (Family)

- **en familie** (m) - a family
- **en mor** (m/f) / **en mamma** (m) - a mother / a mom
- **en far** (m) / **en pappa** (m) - a father / a dad
- **foreldre** (plural) - parents
- **en søster** (m/f) - a sister
- **en bror** (m) - a brother
- **søsken** (plural/n) - siblings
- **et barn** (n) - a child / children (can be both singular and plural)
- **en sønn** (m) - a son
- **en datter** (m/f) - a daughter
- **en kone** (m/f) - a wife
- **en mann** (m) - a husband / a man
- **en bestemor** (m/f) - a grandmother
- **en bestefar** (m) - a grandfather
- **besteforeldre** (plural) - grandparents

### Nyttige verb (Useful verbs)

- **å finne** - to find
- **å se** - to see
- **å si** - to say
- **å høre** - to hear
- **å trenge** - to need
- **å mene** - to mean / to think (opinion)
- **å vite** - to know (facts)
- **å kjenne** - to know (people/places)

## Del 2: Grammatikk (7 minutter)

### Eierord (Possessive Pronouns)

Possessive pronouns show ownership (my, your, his, her, etc.)

**In Norwegian, possessives agree with the noun they describe!**

#### Min/Mi/Mitt (my) - Common/Feminine/Neuter

| Gender | Singular | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Common (en) | min | min bil (my car) |
| Feminine (ei) | mi | mi bok (my book) |
| Neuter (et) | mitt | mitt hus (my house) |
| Plural | mine | mine barn (my children) |

#### Din/Di/Ditt (your - singular)

| Gender | Singular | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Common (en) | din | din bil (your car) |
| Feminine (ei) | di | di bok (your book) |
| Neuter (et) | ditt | ditt hus (your house) |
| Plural | dine | dine barn (your children) |

#### Hans (his) / Hennes (her) / Sitt (reflexive)

**Hans** and **hennes** do NOT change form:
- hans bil (his car)
- hans hus (his house)
- hennes bil (her car)
- hennes hus (her house)

**Sitt/si/sine** (reflexive - when referring back to subject):
- Han ser **sin** bil. (He sees his [own] car.)
- Hun ser **sitt** hus. (She sees her [own] house.)

#### Vår/Vår/Vårt (our)

| Gender | Singular | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Common (en) | vår | vår bil (our car) |
| Feminine (ei) | vår | vår bok (our book) |
| Neuter (et) | vårt | vårt hus (our house) |
| Plural | våre | våre barn (our children) |

#### Deres (your - plural / their)

**Deres** does NOT change form:
- deres bil (your/their car)
- deres hus (your/their house)
- deres barn (your/their children)

### Sammendrag (Summary)

| Person | Common (en) | Feminine (ei) | Neuter (et) | Plural |
|--------|-------------|---------------|-------------|---------|
| min (my) | min | mi | mitt | mine |
| din (your) | din | di | ditt | dine |
| hans (his) | hans | hans | hans | hans |
| hennes (her) | hennes | hennes | hennes | hennes |
| vår (our) | vår | vår | vårt | våre |
| deres (your/their) | deres | deres | deres | deres |

**Eksempler:**

- Dette er **min bil** (m). (This is my car.)
- Hvor er **ditt hus** (n)? (Where is your house?)
- **Hans mor** (m/f) er lærer. (His mother is a teacher.)
- **Hennes familie** (m) bor i Oslo. (Her family lives in Oslo.)
- **Vårt barn** (n) er fem år. (Our child is five years old.)
- Hvor bor **deres foreldre** (plural)? (Where do your/their parents live?)

## Del 3: Øvelser (8 minutter)

### Øvelse 1: Fyll inn riktig form av "min"

1. Dette er _____ mor. (en mor - This is my mother.)
2. _____ hus er stort. (et hus - My house is big.)
3. Jeg liker _____ bil. (en bil - I like my car.)
4. Hvor er _____ barn? (plural - Where are my children?)

### Øvelse 2: Velg riktig eierord (min, din, hans, hennes, vår, deres)

1. Hun ser _____ søster. (her sister)
2. Vi liker _____ hjem. (our home - et hjem)
3. Hvor bor _____ foreldre? (your parents - du)
4. _____ bror arbeider i Oslo. (his brother)
5. De elsker _____ barn. (their children)

### Øvelse 3: Oversett til norsk

1. This is my family.
2. Where is your house? (ditt)
3. Her mother speaks Norwegian.
4. Our children go to school.
5. I know his sister.

### Øvelse 4: Familie - Svar på spørsmålene

1. Har du søsken? (Do you have siblings?)
2. Hvor bor familien din? (Where does your family live?)
3. Hva heter moren din? (What is your mother's name?)
4. Har du barn? (Do you have children?)

### Øvelse 5: Fyll inn riktig verb (finne, se, si, høre, trenge, vite)

1. Jeg kan ikke _____ bilen min. (I can't find my car.)
2. Kan du _____ meg? (Can you hear me?)
3. Hva _____ du? (What do you say?)
4. Vi _____ hjelp. (We need help.)
5. _____ du hvor han bor? (Do you know where he lives?)

## Kulturnotat (Cultural note)

Norwegian families are generally quite close-knit but also value independence. It's common for extended families to gather for holidays and special occasions. Norwegians typically use first names even with older family members and in-laws, reflecting the egalitarian nature of Norwegian society. The concept of **"koselig"** (cozy/nice) is important in family life, especially gathering together for meals or activities.

## Grammatikknotat (Grammar note)

### Word order with possessives

In Norwegian, you can place possessive pronouns in two positions:

**Before the noun** (more formal):
- **Min** bil er rød. (My car is red.)
- **Vårt** hus er stort. (Our house is big.)

**After the noun with definite form** (more common):
- Bilen **min** er rød. (My car is red - literally "the car mine")
- Huset **vårt** er stort. (Our house is big - literally "the house our")

**Note**: When possessive comes after, the noun takes the definite form!
- en bil → bilen min (NOT: bil min)
- et hus → huset vårt (NOT: hus vårt)

**Both forms are correct!** The second form (after the noun) is more common in everyday speech.

## Lekse til i morgen:

1. Memorize family vocabulary
2. Practice possessive pronouns with different genders (min/mitt/mine)
3. Learn the new verbs: å finne, å se, å si, å høre, å trenge, å vite
4. Practice describing your family in Norwegian

**Eksempel:**
- **Familien min** (the family my - definite form of **en familie**) bor i Canada. (My family lives in Canada.)
- Jeg har **en søster** (m/f) og **en bror** (m). (I have a sister and a brother.)
- **Moren min** (the mother my - definite form of **en mor**) er lærer. (My mother is a teacher.)
- **Faren min** (the father my - definite form of **en far**) arbeider på **et kontor** (n - an office). (My father works at an office.)
