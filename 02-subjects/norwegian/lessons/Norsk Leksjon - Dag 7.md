**Tema: Vær og årstider**

## Del 1: Ordforråd (5 minutter)

### Årstider (Seasons)

- **en vår** (m) - spring
- **en sommer** (m) - summer
- **en høst** (m) - autumn/fall
- **en vinter** (m) - winter
- **et år** (n) - year
- **en sesong** (m) - season

### Været (Weather)

- **et vær** (n) - weather
- **en sol** (m/f) - sun
- **en regn** (m) - rain
- **en snø** (m) - snow
- **en vind** (m) - wind
- **en sky** (m/f) - cloud
- **en tåke** (m/f) - fog
- **et tordenvær** (n) - thunderstorm
- **en temperatur** (m) - temperature
- **en grad** (m) - degree

### Værbeskrivelser (Weather descriptions)

- **varmt** - warm/hot
- **kaldt** - cold
- **fint** - nice (weather)
- **dårlig** - bad
- **solfylt** - sunny
- **overskyet** - cloudy
- **regnfylt** - rainy
- **snøfylt** - snowy
- **vått** - wet
- **tørt** - dry
- **vindfullt** - windy
- **mildt** - mild

### Klær (Clothes)

- **et skjerf** (n) - scarf
- **en lue** (m/f) - hat/beanie

## Del 2: Grammatikk (7 minutter)

### Verbet "å bli" (to become) - IRREGULAR

| Person | Presens | Preteritum |
|--------|---------|------------|
| jeg | blir | ble |
| du | blir | ble |
| han/hun/det | blir | ble |
| vi | blir | ble |
| dere | blir | ble |
| de | blir | ble |

**Important**: "bli" is used for weather and changes:
- Det **blir** kaldt. (It's getting cold.)
- Været **blir** bedre. (The weather is getting better.)

### Verbet "å føle" (to feel) - Regular

- jeg **føler** - I feel
- du **føler** - you feel
- han/hun/det **føler** - he/she/it feels

### Verbet "å reise" (to travel) - Regular

- jeg **reiser** - I travel
- du **reiser** - you travel
- vi **reiser** - we travel

### Væruttrykk (Weather expressions)

**Describing current weather:**
- **Hvordan er været?** - How is the weather?
- **Det er fint vær.** - The weather is nice.
- **Det regner.** - It's raining.
- **Det snør.** - It's snowing.
- **Det blåser.** - It's windy.
- **Solen skinner.** - The sun is shining.

**Temperature:**
- **Det er varmt.** - It's warm/hot.
- **Det er kaldt.** - It's cold.
- **Det er ti grader.** - It's ten degrees.
- **Det er minus fem grader.** - It's minus five degrees.

**Future weather:**
- **Det blir kaldere.** - It will get colder.
- **Det blir varmere.** - It will get warmer.
- **Skal det regne i morgen?** - Will it rain tomorrow?

**Eksempler:**

- **Hvordan er været** i dag? (How is the weather today?)
- Det **er fint vær**. **Solen skinner**. (The weather is nice. The sun is shining.)
- **Det regner** mye om høsten. (It rains a lot in autumn.)
- Om vinteren **snør det** ofte. (In winter it often snows.)
- Jeg **føler** meg kald. (I feel cold.)
- Vi **reiser** til Norge om sommeren. (We travel to Norway in summer.)
- Det **blir** varmere i morgen. (It will get warmer tomorrow.)

## Kulturnotat (Cultural note)

Norway experiences dramatic seasonal changes. **Vinteren** (winter) can be long and dark, especially in northern Norway where the **mørketid** (polar night) occurs. However, Norwegians embrace winter with activities like skiing and **koselig** time indoors. **Sommeren** (summer) brings the **midnattssol** (midnight sun) in the north, with long, bright days. Norwegians make the most of summer weather, spending time outdoors hiking, swimming, and enjoying nature.

The weather is a common conversation topic in Norway! People often discuss **været** and the changing seasons. Don't be surprised if someone starts a conversation with **"Hvordan er været?"**

**Important phrases:**
- **Det er ikke dårlig vær, bare dårlige klær** - "There's no bad weather, only bad clothes" (common Norwegian saying)

## Grammatikknotat (Grammar note)

### Using "det" for weather

In Norwegian, weather expressions use **"det"** as the subject:

- **Det regner.** (It's raining.)
- **Det snør.** (It's snowing.)
- **Det er fint vær.** (The weather is nice.)
- **Det er kaldt.** (It's cold.)

This is similar to English using "it" for weather.

### "bli" for changes and weather

**å bli** is a very important verb meaning "to become" or "to get":

**For weather changes:**
- Det **blir** kaldt. (It's getting cold.)
- Det **blir** varmt. (It's getting warm.)
- Været **blir** bedre. (The weather is getting better.)

**For other changes:**
- Jeg **blir** glad. (I become/get happy.)
- Hun **blir** lærer. (She's becoming a teacher.)
- Det **blir** mørkt. (It's getting dark.)

### Seasons with "om"

Use **"om"** (about/in) with seasons:
- **om våren** - in spring
- **om sommeren** - in summer
- **om høsten** - in autumn
- **om vinteren** - in winter

**Examples:**
- Vi reiser **om sommeren**. (We travel in summer.)
- Det snør mye **om vinteren**. (It snows a lot in winter.)

## Lekse til i morgen:

1. Memorize all four seasons and basic weather vocabulary
2. Practice describing today's weather in Norwegian
3. Learn the conjugations of "å bli" (irregular!)
4. Practice saying how you feel: "Jeg føler meg..." + adjective
5. Learn to say what the weather will be like: "Det blir..."

**Eksempel:**

- I dag er det **overskyet** (overcast). Det er **15 grader**.
- Det **blir** kanskje **regn** (rain) senere.
- **Om sommeren** (in summer) reiser jeg til Norge.
- Jeg **føler** meg **kald** når det snør.
- I **vinter** (this winter) skal jeg lære å gå på ski.
