# Flashcards - Dag 5: Familie og eierord

## Familie (Family)

en familie
?
a family (m)
**Example**: Min familie bor i Norge (My family lives in Norway)

en mor / en mamma
?
a mother / a mom (m/f) / (m)
**Example**: Moren min er en lærer (My mother is a teacher)

en far / en pappa
?
a father / a dad (m) / (m)
**Example**: Faren min arbeider mye (My father works a lot)

foreldre
?
parents (plural)
**Example**: Foreldrene mine bor i Oslo (My parents live in Oslo)

en søster
?
a sister (m/f)
**Example**: Jeg har en søster (I have a sister)

en bror
?
a brother (m)
**Example**: Broren min er en student (My brother is a student)

søsken
?
siblings (plural/n)
**Example**: Har du søsken? (Do you have siblings?)

et barn
?
a child / children (n)
**Example**: Vi har to barn (We have two children)

en sønn
?
a son (m)
**Example**: Sønnen min er fem år (My son is five years old)

en datter
?
a daughter (m/f)
**Example**: Datteren hennes går til skolen (Her daughter goes to school)

en kone
?
a wife (m/f)
**Example**: Dette er konen min (This is my wife)

en mann
?
a husband / a man (m)
**Example**: Mannen hennes er norsk (Her husband is Norwegian)

en bestemor
?
a grandmother (m/f)
**Example**: Bestemoren min bor i Bergen (My grandmother lives in Bergen)

en bestefar
?
a grandfather (m)
**Example**: Bestefaren min er 80 år (My grandfather is 80 years old)

besteforeldre
?
grandparents (plural)
**Example**: Besteforeldrene mine er snille (My grandparents are kind)

## Eierord - Min (My)

min (en)
?
my (common gender)
**Example**: min bil (my car)

mi (ei)
?
my (feminine)
**Example**: mi bok (my book)

mitt (et)
?
my (neuter)
**Example**: mitt hus (my house)

mine
?
my (plural)
**Example**: mine barn (my children)

## Eierord - Din (Your - singular)

din (en)
?
your (common gender - singular you)
**Example**: din bil (your car)

di (ei)
?
your (feminine - singular you)
**Example**: di bok (your book)

ditt (et)
?
your (neuter - singular you)
**Example**: ditt hus (your house)

dine
?
your (plural - singular you)
**Example**: dine barn (your children)

## Eierord - Hans/Hennes (His/Her)

hans
?
his
**Example**: hans bil (his car)
**Note**: Does not change with gender!

hennes
?
her
**Example**: hennes hus (her house)
**Note**: Does not change with gender!

## Eierord - Vår (Our)

vår (en/ei)
?
our (common/feminine)
**Example**: vår bil (our car)

vårt (et)
?
our (neuter)
**Example**: vårt hus (our house)

våre
?
our (plural)
**Example**: våre barn (our children)

## Eierord - Deres (Your plural/Their)

deres
?
your (plural) / their
**Example**: deres bil (your/their car)
**Note**: Does not change with gender!

## Nyttige Verb - Infinitiv (Useful Verbs - Infinitive)

å finne
?
to find
**Example**: Jeg kan ikke finne bilen min (I can't find my car)

å se
?
to see
**Example**: Jeg ser familien min (I see my family)

å si
?
to say
**Example**: Hva sier du? (What do you say?)

å høre
?
to hear
**Example**: Kan du høre meg? (Can you hear me?)

å trenge
?
to need
**Example**: Vi trenger hjelp (We need help)

å mene
?
to mean / to think (opinion)
**Example**: Hva mener du? (What do you think?)

å vite
?
to know (facts)
**Example**: Jeg vet det (I know that)

å kjenne
?
to know (people/places)
**Example**: Jeg kjenner henne (I know her)

## Verb Konjugasjon (Verb Conjugation)

jeg finner
?
I find
**Example**: Jeg finner bilen min (I find my car)

jeg ser
?
I see
**Example**: Jeg ser familien min (I see my family)

jeg sier
?
I say
**Example**: Hva sier jeg? (What do I say?)

jeg hører
?
I hear
**Example**: Jeg hører deg (I hear you)

jeg trenger
?
I need
**Example**: Jeg trenger kaffe (I need coffee)

jeg mener
?
I mean / I think
**Example**: Jeg mener det (I mean it)

jeg vet
?
I know (fact)
**Example**: Jeg vet hvor han bor (I know where he lives)

jeg kjenner
?
I know (person/place)
**Example**: Jeg kjenner Oslo godt (I know Oslo well)

## Setninger med Eierord (Sentences with Possessives)

Dette er min familie
?
This is my family
**Pattern**: dette er + min/mitt/mine + noun

Hvor er ditt hus?
?
Where is your house?
**Pattern**: possessive agrees with noun gender

Hans mor er lærer
?
His mother is a teacher
**Pattern**: hans/hennes + noun (no change)

Hennes familie bor i Oslo
?
Her family lives in Oslo
**Pattern**: hennes + noun

Vårt barn er fem år
?
Our child is five years old
**Pattern**: vårt (neuter) + et barn

Barna våre går til skolen
?
Our children go to school
**Pattern**: noun (definite) + possessive

Familien min bor i Norge
?
My family lives in Norway
**Pattern**: noun (definite) + possessive (more common)

## Viktig Forskjell (Important Difference)

å vite
?
to know (facts/information)
**Example**: Jeg vet hvor han bor (I know where he lives)
**Note**: Facts, information, that something is true

å kjenne
?
to know (people/places/by familiarity)
**Example**: Jeg kjenner henne (I know her)
**Note**: People, places, being familiar with

## Spørsmål om Familie (Questions about Family)

Har du søsken?
?
Do you have siblings?
**Common question about family**

Hvor bor familien din?
?
Where does your family live?
**Pattern**: familien + din (possessive after noun)

Hva heter moren din?
?
What is your mother's name?
**Pattern**: moren + din

Har du barn?
?
Do you have children?
**Common question**

Hvor mange søsken har du?
?
How many siblings do you have?
**Using "hvor mange" (how many)**

## Ord-plassering (Word Order with Possessives)

Min bil er rød
?
My car is red
**Pattern**: Possessive BEFORE noun (more formal)

Bilen min er rød
?
My car is red
**Pattern**: Noun (definite) + possessive AFTER (more common)
**Note**: Both are correct!

Vårt hus er stort
?
Our house is big
**Pattern**: Possessive BEFORE noun

Huset vårt er stort
?
Our house is big
**Pattern**: Noun (definite) + possessive AFTER (more common)

## Setninger med Nye Verb (Sentences with New Verbs)

Jeg kan ikke finne bilen min
?
I can't find my car
**Pattern**: kan ikke + infinitive

Kan du høre meg?
?
Can you hear me?
**Common question for checking if someone hears you**

Hva sier du?
?
What do you say? / What are you saying?
**Very common question**

Vi trenger hjelp
?
We need help
**Example**: trenger + noun

Vet du hvor han bor?
?
Do you know where he lives?
**Pattern**: vite + indirect question

Jeg kjenner søsteren hans
?
I know his sister
**Pattern**: kjenne + person

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

Jeg elsker familien min
?
I love my family
**Pattern**: elske + noun

Mor og far
?
Mom and dad / Mother and father
**Common way to refer to parents**

Store søster / Lille søster
?
Big sister / Little sister
**Adjective + noun**

Store bror / Lille bror
?
Big brother / Little brother
**Common way to describe siblings**
