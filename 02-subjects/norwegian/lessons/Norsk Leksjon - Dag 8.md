# Norsk Leksjon - Dag 8

**Tema: Tid og Daglige Rutiner**

---

## Del 1: Ordforråd (5 minutter)

### Tid (Time)

- **en tid** (m/f) - time
- **et klokkeslett** (n) - time (of day), o'clock
- **en time** (m) - hour
- **et minutt** (n) - minute
- **et sekund** (n) - second
- **en morgen** (m) - morning
- **en formiddag** (m) - forenoon/late morning
- **en ettermiddag** (m) - afternoon
- **en kveld** (m) - evening
- **en natt** (m/f) - night
- **en dag** (m) - day
- **en uke** (m/f) - week

### Klokkeslett (Times of day)

- **Hva er klokken?** - What time is it?
- **Klokken er...** - It's... o'clock
- **klokken ett** - 1 o'clock
- **klokken to** - 2 o'clock
- **halv to** - half past one (1:30)
- **kvart over** - quarter past
- **kvart på** - quarter to

### Daglige aktiviteter (Daily activities)

- **en rutine** (m) - routine
- **en vane** (m) - habit
- **frokost** (m) - breakfast
- **lunsj** (m) - lunch
- **middag** (m) - dinner
- **kvelds** (m) - supper/evening meal
- **en pause** (m) - break/pause
- **søvn** (m) - sleep
- **arbeid** (n) - work

### Tidsuttrykk (Time expressions)

- **tidlig** - early
- **sent** - late
- **nå** - now
- **snart** - soon
- **senere** - later
- **alltid** - always
- **ofte** - often
- **noen ganger** - sometimes
- **sjelden** - rarely
- **aldri** - never
- **vanligvis** - usually
- **hver dag** - every day

## Del 2: Grammatikk (7 minutter)

### Verbet "å våkne" (to wake up) - Regular

| Person | Presens | Preteritum |
|--------|---------|------------|
| jeg | våkner | våknet |
| du | våkner | våknet |
| han/hun/det | våkner | våknet |
| vi | våkner | våknet |
| dere | våkner | våknet |
| de | våkner | våknet |

### Verbet "å sove" (to sleep) - Regular

| Person | Presens | Preteritum |
|--------|---------|------------|
| jeg | sover | sov |
| du | sover | sov |
| han/hun/det | sover | sov |
| vi | sover | sov |
| dere | sover | sov |
| de | sover | sov |

### Verbet "å begynne" (to begin/start) - Regular

- jeg **begynner** - I begin
- du **begynner** - you begin
- Preteritum: **begynte** - began

### Verbet "å slutte" (to end/finish) - Regular

- jeg **slutter** - I finish/end
- du **slutter** - you finish/end
- Preteritum: **sluttet** - finished

### Separable verbs with "opp" and "seg"

**å stå opp** (to get up - literally "to stand up"):
- Jeg **står opp** klokken sju. (I get up at 7 o'clock.)

**å legge seg** (to go to bed - literally "to lay oneself"):
- Jeg **legger meg** klokken elleve. (I go to bed at 11 o'clock.)

### Si klokken (Telling time)

**Full hours:**
- Klokken er **ett**. (It's 1 o'clock.)
- Klokken er **ti**. (It's 10 o'clock.)

**Half hours (note: Norwegian counts toward the next hour!):**
- Klokken er **halv to**. (It's 1:30 - literally "half to two")
- Klokken er **halv seks**. (It's 5:30 - literally "half to six")

**Quarter hours:**
- Klokken er **kvart over to**. (It's 2:15 - quarter past two)
- Klokken er **kvart på tre**. (It's 2:45 - quarter to three)

**Minutes:**
- Klokken er **fem over tre**. (It's 3:05)
- Klokken er **ti på fire**. (It's 3:50 - ten to four)

### Expressing when (time prepositions)

**klokken + time** (at + time):
- Jeg våkner **klokken sju**. (I wake up at 7 o'clock.)

**om + part of day**:
- **om morgenen** - in the morning
- **om formiddagen** - in the late morning
- **om ettermiddagen** - in the afternoon
- **om kvelden** - in the evening
- **om natten** - at night

**på + day of week**:
- **på mandag** - on Monday
- **på lørdag** - on Saturday

**Eksempler:**

- Jeg **våkner** klokken sju **om morgenen**. (I wake up at 7 in the morning.)
- Jeg **spiser** frokost klokken halv åtte. (I eat breakfast at 7:30.)
- Jeg **begynner** å arbeide klokken ni. (I start working at 9.)
- Jeg **spiser** lunsj klokken tolv. (I eat lunch at 12.)
- Jeg **slutter** å arbeide klokken fem. (I finish working at 5.)
- Jeg **spiser** middag **om kvelden**. (I eat dinner in the evening.)
- Jeg **legger meg** klokken elleve. (I go to bed at 11.)
- Jeg **sover** åtte timer hver natt. (I sleep eight hours every night.)

## Kulturnotat (Cultural note)

### Norwegian Daily Routines

Norwegians tend to have structured daily routines:

**Work hours**: Most offices work **8-16** or **9-17** (8am-4pm or 9am-5pm). The workday is shorter than in many countries, but Norwegians are very productive during work hours.

**Meals**:
- **Frokost** (breakfast): Usually simple - bread, cheese, jam, yogurt. Eaten between 7-9am.
- **Lunsj** (lunch): Often a packed lunch (**matpakke**) brought from home, typically open-faced sandwiches. Eaten around 12-1pm.
- **Middag** (dinner): The main hot meal, eaten early, usually between 4-6pm.
- **Kvelds** (evening snack): Light meal or snack later in the evening.

**Punctuality**: Being on time is very important in Norwegian culture! If you say you'll meet someone "klokken tre" (at 3 o'clock), you should be there at 3, not 3:15.

**Important phrases:**
- **God morgen!** - Good morning!
- **God natt!** - Good night!
- **Ha en fin dag!** - Have a nice day!

## Grammatikknotat (Grammar note)

### "halv" - The tricky half hour

In Norwegian, **half hours are counted toward the NEXT hour**, not the previous hour like in English:

- **halv to** = 1:30 (half to two, not half past one)
- **halv fire** = 3:30 (half to four)
- **halv tolv** = 11:30 (half to twelve)

This can be confusing for English speakers! Remember: "halv" means you're halfway TO the next hour.

### "på" vs "om" for time

**om** is used for parts of the day:
- **om morgenen** (in the morning)
- **om kvelden** (in the evening)

**på** is used for days:
- **på mandag** (on Monday)
- **på lørdag** (on Saturday)

**klokken** is used for specific times:
- **klokken åtte** (at 8 o'clock)

### Reflexive verbs for daily routines

Many daily routine verbs are reflexive (use "seg"):

- **å legge seg** (to go to bed - literally "lay oneself down")
- **å kle seg** (to get dressed - Day 7)
- **å vaske seg** (to wash oneself)

The reflexive pronoun changes with the subject:
- jeg legger **meg** (I go to bed)
- du legger **deg** (you go to bed)
- han/hun legger **seg** (he/she goes to bed)
- vi legger **oss** (we go to bed)

## Lekse til i morgen:

1. Memorize how to tell time in Norwegian (especially "halv"!)
2. Practice describing your daily routine using time expressions
3. Learn the conjugations of å våkne, å sove, å begynne, å slutte
4. Practice the separable verbs: å stå opp, å legge seg
5. Write out your daily schedule in Norwegian

**Eksempel:**

- Jeg **våkner** klokken **sju** om morgenen.
- Jeg **står opp** og spiser frokost.
- Jeg **begynner** å arbeide klokken **ni**.
- Jeg **spiser** lunsj klokken **tolv**.
- Jeg **slutter** å arbeide klokken **fem** om ettermiddagen.
- **Om kvelden** leser jeg eller ser på TV.
- Jeg **legger meg** **vanligvis** klokken **elleve**.
- Jeg **sover** omtrent åtte timer.
