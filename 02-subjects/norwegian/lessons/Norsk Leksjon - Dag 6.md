# Norsk Leksjon - Dag 6

**Tema: Mat og måltider**

---

## Del 1: Ordforråd (5 minutter)

### Måltider (Meals)

- **en frokost** (m) - breakfast
- **en lunsj** (m) - lunch
- **en middag** (m) - dinner
- **et måltid** (n) - a meal
- **et mellommåltid** (n) - a snack

### Mat (Food)

- **et brød** (n) - bread
- **en smørbrød** (n) - open sandwich
- **en ost** (m) - cheese
- **en skinke** (m/f) - ham
- **et egg** (n) - egg
- **en fisk** (m) - fish
- **et kjøtt** (n) - meat
- **en kylling** (m/f) - chicken
- **en frukt** (m/f) - fruit
- **en grønnsak** (m) - vegetable
- **en salat** (m) - salad
- **en suppe** (m/f) - soup
- **en pizza** (m) - pizza
- **en pasta** (m) - pasta
- **en ris** (m) - rice

### Drikke (Drinks)

- **et vann** (n) - water
- **en melk** (m/f) - milk
- **en juice** (m) - juice
- **en øl** (n) - beer
- **et glass** (n) - glass

### Smak (Taste)

- **god** - good/tasty
- **deilig** - delicious
- **søt** - sweet
- **salt** - salty
- **sur** - sour
- **bitter** - bitter

## Del 2: Grammatikk (7 minutter)

### Verbet "å lage" (to make/cook) i presens

- jeg **lager** - I make
- du **lager** - you make
- han/hun **lager** - he/she makes
- vi **lager** - we make
- dere **lager** - you make
- de **lager** - they make

### Verbet "å kjøpe" (to buy) i presens

- jeg **kjøper** - I buy
- du **kjøper** - you buy
- han/hun **kjøper** - he/she buys
- vi **kjøper** - we buy
- dere **kjøper** - you buy
- de **kjøper** - they buy

### Verbet "å elske" (to love) i presens

- jeg **elsker** - I love
- du **elsker** - you love
- han/hun **elsker** - he/she loves
- vi **elsker** - we love
- dere **elsker** - you love
- de **elsker** - they love

### Verbet "å smake" (to taste) i presens

- jeg **smaker** - I taste
- du **smaker** - you taste
- han/hun **smaker** - he/she tastes
- vi **smaker** - we taste
- dere **smaker** - you taste
- de **smaker** - they taste

### Spørsmål om mat (Questions about food)

- **Hva liker du å spise?** - What do you like to eat?
- **Hva skal vi lage til middag?** - What should we make for dinner?
- **Vil du ha mer?** - Do you want more?
- **Hva smaker best?** - What tastes best?
- **Er du sulten?** - Are you hungry?
- **Er du tørst?** - Are you thirsty?

### Nyttige uttrykk (Useful expressions)

- **Jeg er sulten** (m) / **Jeg er sulten** - I am hungry
- **Jeg er tørst** (m) / **Jeg er tørst** - I am thirsty
- **Jeg er mett** (m) / **Jeg er mett** - I am full
- **God appetitt!** - Enjoy your meal! (like "Bon appétit")
- **Takk for maten** - Thanks for the food (said after meals)
- **Vær så god** - Here you go / Help yourself

**Eksempler:**

- Jeg **lager** mat til middag. (I'm making food for dinner.)
- Vi **kjøper** brød og melk. (We buy bread and milk.)
- Hun **elsker** pizza! (She loves pizza!)
- **Smaker** det godt? (Does it taste good?)
- Jeg er **sulten**. Skal vi spise nå? (I'm hungry. Should we eat now?)

**Common food combinations:**
- **en brød med ost** - bread with cheese (en brød - n, en ost - m)
- **et glass melk** - a glass of milk (et glass - n, en melk - m/f)
- **en kopp kaffe** (m/f) - a cup of coffee

## Del 3: Øvelser (8 minutter)

### Øvelse 1: Fyll inn riktig form av verbet

1. Jeg _____ middag hver dag. (lage - I make dinner every day.)
2. Hva _____ du til frokost? (spise - What do you eat for breakfast?)
3. Vi _____ mat i butikken. (kjøpe - We buy food at the store.)
4. Hun _____ fisk og ris. (elske - She loves fish and rice.)
5. _____ det godt? (smake - Does it taste good?)

### Øvelse 2: Oversett til norsk

1. I am hungry.
2. What do you want for lunch?
3. We are making pizza for dinner.
4. Do you like cheese?
5. She buys bread and milk.

### Øvelse 3: Velg riktig substantiv (med kjønn)

1. Jeg drikker _____ vann. (et/en)
2. Vi spiser _____ frokost. (en/et)
3. Hun lager _____ pasta. (en/et)
4. De kjøper _____ brød. (et/en)
5. Jeg vil ha _____ glass melk. (et/en)

### Øvelse 4: Svar på spørsmålene

1. Hva liker du å spise til frokost?
2. Er du sulten nå?
3. Hva smaker best - pizza eller pasta?
4. Hva drikker du til middag?
5. Kan du lage mat?

### Øvelse 5: Match the food with category

3. Grønnsaker: _____
4. Meieriprodukter (Dairy): _____

## Kulturnotat (Cultural note)

Norwegian food culture has some distinctive features. **Matpakke** (packed lunch) is very common - Norwegians often bring sandwiches from home rather than buying lunch. **Fredagstaco** (Friday tacos) is a popular tradition where families eat tacos together on Friday evenings. Norwegian breakfast typically includes **brød** (bread), **brunost** (brown cheese - a unique Norwegian sweet cheese), and coffee. Dinner (**middag**) is usually eaten early, around 4-6 PM, and is the main hot meal of the day.

**Takk for maten** (thanks for the food) is always said after a meal as a polite gesture, whether at home or as a guest.

## Grammatikknotat (Grammar note)

### Definite and indefinite forms with food

When talking about food in general, Norwegian often uses the definite form:

- **Jeg liker pizza.** (I like pizza.) - general, no article
- **Jeg spiser pizzaen.** (I'm eating the pizza.) - specific pizza

With quantities:

- **et glass vann** (a glass of water)
- **en kopp kaffe** (a cup of coffee)
- **et stykke brød** (a piece of bread)

### Regular verb pattern review

All the new verbs today follow the regular pattern:
- Infinitive: **å lage** → Present: **lager**
- Infinitive: **å kjøpe** → Present: **kjøper**
- Infinitive: **å elske** → Present: **elsker**

The pattern: Remove **å**, add **-r** or **-er**

## Lekse til i morgen:

1. Memorize food vocabulary - at least 10 food words
2. Practice the new verbs: å lage, å kjøpe, å elske, å smake
3. Learn to say what you like to eat and drink
4. Practice saying "Jeg er sulten/tørst/mett"

**Eksempel:**

- Til frokost liker jeg å spise **brød med ost** (et brød - n, en ost - m). (For breakfast I like to eat bread with cheese.)
- Jeg drikker **kaffe** (en kaffe - m) hver morgen. (I drink coffee every morning.)
- Jeg **lager** ofte **pasta** (en pasta - m) til middag. (I often make pasta for dinner.)
- **Takk for maten!** (Thanks for the food!)

**Lykke til!** (Good luck!)
