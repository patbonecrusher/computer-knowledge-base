# å se (to see)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å se

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | ser | I see |
| du | ser | you see |
| han/hun/det | ser | he/she/it sees |
| vi | ser | we see |
| dere | ser | you see (plural) |
| de | ser | they see |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **ser** deg. (I see you.)
- **Ser du** huset? (Do you see the house?)
- Hun **ser** familien sin. (She sees her family.)
- Vi **ser** på TV. (We watch TV / We're watching TV.)
- De **ser** ikke bilen. (They don't see the car.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | så | I saw |
| du | så | you saw |
| han/hun/det | så | he/she/it saw |
| vi | så | we saw |
| dere | så | you saw (plural) |
| de | så | they saw |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **så** deg i går. (I saw you yesterday.)
- **Så du** filmen? (Did you see the movie?)
- Vi **så** huset. (We saw the house.)
- Hun **så** ikke bilen. (She didn't see the car.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + sett

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har sett | I have seen |
| du | har sett | you have seen |
| han/hun/det | har sett | he/she/it has seen |
| vi | har sett | we have seen |
| dere | har sett | you have seen (plural) |
| de | har sett | they have seen |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har sett** denne filmen. (I have seen this movie.)
- **Har du sett** Oslo? (Have you seen Oslo?)
- Vi **har sett** familien hans. (We have seen his family.)
- Hun **har ikke sett** det. (She hasn't seen it.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + se

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal se / vil se | I will see / I'm going to see |
| du | skal se / vil se | you will see |
| han/hun/det | skal se / vil se | he/she/it will see |
| vi | skal se / vil se | we will see |
| dere | skal se / vil se | you will see (plural) |
| de | skal se / vil see | they will see |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal se** filmen. (I'm going to see the movie.)
- **Vil du se** på TV? (Do you want to watch TV?)
- Vi **skal se**! (We'll see!)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**se** - see! / look!

### Eksempler:
- **Se** her! (Look here!)
- **Se** på dette! (Look at this!)
- **Se**! (Look!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **ser ikke** det. (I don't see it.)
- Vi **så ikke** henne. (We didn't see her.)
- Hun **har ikke sett** filmen. (She hasn't seen the movie.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Ser du?** - Do you see? / You see?
- **Vi får se** - We'll see
- **Jeg ser** - I see (I understand)
- **se på** - to look at / to watch
  - Jeg **ser på** TV. (I watch TV / I'm watching TV.)
- **se ut** - to look (appear)
  - Du **ser ut** som om du er sliten. (You look like you're tired.)
- **se etter** - to look for / to look after
  - Jeg **ser etter** bilen min. (I'm looking for my car.)

---

## Common Uses

**Watching/Viewing:**
- **se på TV** - watch TV
- **se en film** - watch a movie / see a movie
- **se fotball** - watch football/soccer

**Looking:**
- **se her** - look here
- **se der** - look there
- **se opp** - look up
- **se ned** - look down

**Seeing people:**
- **se familien** - see the family
- **se venner** - see friends
- **Jeg ser deg** - I see you

---

## Special Expressions

**Vi får se** = We'll see / We'll have to wait and see
- **Vi får se** hva som skjer. (We'll see what happens.)

**Se her!** = Look here!
- **Se her**! Dette er viktig. (Look here! This is important.)

**Se ut** = look (appear)
- Det **ser ut** til å regne. (It looks like it's going to rain.)
- Du **ser** god **ut**! (You look good!)

**Se etter** = look for / look after
- Kan du **se etter** barna? (Can you look after the children?)
- Jeg **ser etter** nøklene. (I'm looking for the keys.)

---

## Special Note: Conjugation

**å se** is irregular and very short!:
- Present: ser
- Past: **så** (irregular!)
- Past participle: **sett** (irregular!)

Pattern: se → så → sett

---

## Related Verbs

**å se** = to see (visual perception)
- Jeg **ser** bilen. (I see the car.)

**å se på** = to watch / to look at
- Jeg **ser på** TV. (I watch TV.)

**å kikke** = to peek / to look
- Jeg **kikker** på deg. (I'm peeking at you.)
