# å trives (to enjoy / to thrive)

**Type**: Regular verb (deponent - always reflexive)
**Infinitive**: å trives

**Meaning**: To enjoy (being somewhere), to thrive, to feel comfortable

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | trives | I enjoy / I thrive |
| du | trives | you enjoy / you thrive |
| han/hun/det | trives | he/she/it enjoys / thrives |
| vi | trives | we enjoy / we thrive |
| dere | trives | you enjoy / you thrive (plural) |
| de | trives | they enjoy / they thrive |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **trives** i Norge. (I enjoy it in Norway / I'm thriving in Norway.)
- **Trives du** her? (Do you enjoy it here? / Are you happy here?)
- Hun **trives** på jobben. (She enjoys her job / She's thriving at work.)
- Vi **trives** godt. (We're doing well / We're enjoying ourselves.)
- De **trives** med været. (They enjoy the weather.)

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**triv deg** - enjoy yourself!

### Eksempler:
- **Triv deg!** (Enjoy yourself!)
- **Triv dere godt!** (Enjoy yourselves!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **trives ikke** her. (I don't enjoy it here.)
- Vi **trivdes ikke** der. (We didn't enjoy it there.)
- Hun **har ikke trivdes** på jobben. (She hasn't enjoyed her job.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **trives godt** - to enjoy oneself / to be doing well
  - Jeg **trives godt** her. (I'm doing well here.)
- **trives med** - to enjoy (something specific)
  - Jeg **trives med** været. (I enjoy the weather.)
- **trives i** - to enjoy (being in a place)
  - Hun **trives i** Norge. (She enjoys it in Norway.)
- **trives på** - to enjoy (at a place/job)
  - Vi **trives på** jobben. (We enjoy our jobs.)

---

## Important: Special Deponent Verb

**å trives** is a special type of verb called "deponent"

This means:
- It ALWAYS has reflexive form (ends in -s)
- You NEVER say "jeg triver meg" - it's ALWAYS "jeg trives"
- The -s is built into the verb, not a separate reflexive pronoun

This is different from "føle seg" or "kle seg" where you conjugate the reflexive pronoun!

## Common Uses

### 1. Enjoying a place (most common)

- Jeg **trives** i Norge. (I enjoy it in Norway / I'm happy in Norway.)
- **Trives du** her? (Do you like it here? / Are you happy here?)
- Hun **trives** i Oslo. (She enjoys it in Oslo.)
- Vi **trives** godt her. (We're doing well here.)

### 2. Enjoying work/school

- Jeg **trives** på jobben. (I enjoy my job / I'm happy at work.)
- **Trives du** på skolen? (Do you enjoy school?)
- Han **trives** ikke på universitetet. (He doesn't enjoy university.)

### 3. Enjoying weather/climate

- Jeg **trives** med kaldt vær. (I enjoy cold weather.)
- **Trives du** med vinteren? (Do you enjoy winter?)
- Vi **trives** med norsk klima. (We enjoy the Norwegian climate.)

### 4. General well-being

- **Trives du** godt? (Are you doing well? / Are you happy?)
- Jeg **trives** veldig godt. (I'm doing very well / I'm very happy.)

---

## trives vs føle seg

**å trives** = long-term enjoyment/comfort (being happy somewhere)
- Jeg **trives** i Norge. (I enjoy living in Norway / I'm happy in Norway.)

**å føle seg** = temporary feeling (how you feel now)
- Jeg **føler meg** glad. (I feel happy right now.)

**trives** is about general well-being and enjoying your situation!

## Common Questions

**Trives du her?** - Do you enjoy it here? / Are you happy here?

**Trives du i Norge?** - Do you enjoy it in Norway?

**Trives du på jobben?** - Do you enjoy your job?

**Trives du godt?** - Are you doing well? / Are you happy?

**Hvordan trives du?** - How are you doing? / How are you enjoying it?

---

## Special Expressions

**Trives godt** - Be doing well / Enjoy oneself
- Jeg **trives godt** her. (I'm doing well here / I really enjoy it here.)

**Trives ikke** - Not enjoy / Not be happy
- Han **trives ikke** på skolen. (He doesn't enjoy school.)

**Trives med** - Enjoy (something specific)
- Jeg **trives med** kaldt vær. (I enjoy cold weather.)
- **Trives du med** norsk mat? (Do you enjoy Norwegian food?)

**Trives sammen** - Enjoy being together
- Vi **trives** veldig godt sammen. (We really enjoy being together.)

---

## Weather and Climate Uses

Very relevant for talking about Norwegian weather!

- Jeg **trives** med kaldt vær. (I enjoy cold weather.)
- **Trives du** med vinteren? (Do you enjoy winter?)
- Hun **trives** med snø. (She enjoys the snow.)
- Vi **trives** med norsk klima. (We enjoy the Norwegian climate.)
- Jeg **trives ikke** med regn. (I don't enjoy rain.)

---

## Place Expressions with "trives"

- **trives i Norge** - enjoy it in Norway
- **trives her** - enjoy it here
- **trives der** - enjoy it there
- **trives i Oslo** - enjoy it in Oslo
- **trives hjemme** - enjoy being at home
- **trives på jobben** - enjoy work
- **trives på skolen** - enjoy school

---

## Important Note

**"Trives du?"** is a very common and friendly question in Norwegian!

It's warmer and more personal than just asking "Hvordan har du det?" (How are you?)

When someone asks **"Trives du godt?"** they're genuinely asking if you're happy and comfortable in your situation.

Good answers:
- Ja, jeg **trives** veldig godt! (Yes, I'm doing very well!)
- Jeg **trives** kjempebra! (I'm doing great!)
- Jeg **trives** godt, takk! (I'm doing well, thanks!)
