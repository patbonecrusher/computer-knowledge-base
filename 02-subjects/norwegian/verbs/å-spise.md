# å spise (to eat)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å spise

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | spiser | I eat |
| du | spiser | you eat |
| han/hun/det | spiser | he/she/it eats |
| vi | spiser | we eat |
| dere | spiser | you eat (plural) |
| de | spiser | they eat |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **spiser** frokost. (I eat breakfast / I'm eating breakfast.)
- **Hva spiser du?** (What are you eating? / What do you eat?)
- Hun **spiser** middag nå. (She's eating dinner now.)
- Vi **spiser** mat. (We eat food / We're eating food.)
- De **spiser** ofte ute. (They often eat out.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |

| han/hun/det | spiste | he/she/it ate |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **spiste** frokost i går. (I ate breakfast yesterday.)
- **Hva spiste du?** (What did you eat?)
- Vi **spiste** middag sammen. (We ate dinner together.)
- De **spiste** pizza. (They ate pizza.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + spist

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har spist | I have eaten |
| du | har spist | you have eaten |
| han/hun/det | har spist | he/she/it has eaten |
| vi | har spist | we have eaten |
| dere | har spist | you have eaten (plural) |
| de | har spist | they have eaten |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har spist**. (I have eaten / I've already eaten.)
- **Har du spist** frokost? (Have you eaten breakfast?)
- Vi **har spist** for mye. (We have eaten too much.)
- Hun **har ikke spist** ennå. (She hasn't eaten yet.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + spise

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal spise / vil spise | I will eat / I'm going to eat |
| du | skal spise / vil spise | you will eat |
| han/hun/det | skal spise / vil spise | he/she/it will eat |
| vi | skal spise / vil spise | we will eat |
| dere | skal spise / vil spise | you will eat (plural) |
| de | skal spise / vil spise | they will eat |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal spise** snart. (I'm going to eat soon.)
- **Hva skal vi spise**? (What are we going to eat?)
- Vi **vil spise** middag sammen. (We want to eat dinner together.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**spis** - eat!

### Eksempler:
- **Spis** maten din! (Eat your food!)
- **Spis** frokost! (Eat breakfast!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **spiser ikke** kjøtt. (I don't eat meat.)
- Vi **spiste ikke** middag. (We didn't eat dinner.)
- Hun **har ikke spist**. (She hasn't eaten.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **spise frokost** - to eat breakfast
- **spise lunsj** - to eat lunch
- **spise middag** - to eat dinner
- **spise kvelds** - to eat supper/evening meal
- **spise ute** - to eat out (at a restaurant)
- **spise hjemme** - to eat at home
- **God appetitt!** - Enjoy your meal! (Bon appétit!)
- **Skal vi spise?** - Shall we eat?

---

## Meals in Norwegian

**frokost** = breakfast
- Jeg **spiser frokost** klokken åtte. (I eat breakfast at 8 o'clock.)

**lunsj** = lunch
- Vi **spiser lunsj** sammen. (We eat lunch together.)

**middag** = dinner (main meal, usually evening)
- Hva **skal vi spise til middag**? (What are we going to eat for dinner?)

**kvelds** = supper/evening meal (light meal)
- Jeg **spiser kvelds** før jeg går til sengs. (I eat supper before I go to bed.)

---

## Common Questions

**Hva spiser du?** - What are you eating? / What do you eat?

**Har du spist?** - Have you eaten?

**Skal vi spise?** - Shall we eat?

**Hva skal vi spise til middag?** - What are we going to eat for dinner?

**Spiser du kjøtt?** - Do you eat meat?

---

## Prepositions

**spise til** = eat for (a meal)
- Hva skal vi **spise til middag**? (What are we going to eat for dinner?)

**spise ute** = eat out
- Vi **spiser ute** i kveld. (We're eating out tonight.)

**spise hjemme** = eat at home
- Jeg **spiser hjemme**. (I eat at home.)

---

---

## Special Note: Dietary Preferences

**Jeg spiser ikke...** = I don't eat...
- Jeg **spiser ikke kjøtt**. (I don't eat meat.) - vegetarian
- Jeg **spiser ikke animalsk mat**. (I don't eat animal products.) - vegan
