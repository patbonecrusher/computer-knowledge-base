# å hete (to be called / to be named)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å hete

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | heter | I am called / my name is |
| du | heter | you are called / your name is |
| han/hun/det | heter | he/she/it is called |
| vi | heter | we are called |
| dere | heter | you are called (plural) |
| de | heter | they are called |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **heter** Patrick. (My name is Patrick / I am called Patrick.)
- **Hva heter du?** (What's your name? / What are you called?)
- Hun **heter** Maria. (Her name is Maria.)
- Vi **heter** ikke det. (We are not called that / That's not our name.)
- **Hva heter det på norsk?** (What's that called in Norwegian?)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | het / hette | I was called |
| du | het / hette | you were called |
| han/hun/det | het / hette | he/she/it was called |
| vi | het / hette | we were called |
| dere | het / hette | you were called (plural) |
| de | het / hette | they were called |

**Note**: Both "het" and "hette" are correct. "Het" is more common.

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **het** Anna før. (I was called Anna before / My name was Anna before.)
- Han **het** ikke det. (He wasn't called that.)
- Hva **het** hun? (What was she called? / What was her name?)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + hett

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har hett | I have been called |
| du | har hett | you have been called |
| han/hun/det | har hett | he/she/it has been called |
| vi | har hett | we have been called |
| dere | har hett | you have been called (plural) |
| de | har hett | they have been called |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har hett** det hele livet. (I have been called that my whole life.)
- Hun **har alltid hett** Maria. (She has always been called Maria.)

**Note**: This tense is rarely used with "å hete" since names don't change often!

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + hete

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal hete / vil hete | I will be called |
| du | skal hete / vil hete | you will be called |
| han/hun/det | skal hete / vil hete | he/she/it will be called |
| vi | skal hete / vil hete | we will be called |
| dere | skal hete / vil hete | you will be called (plural) |
| de | skal hete / vil hete | they will be called |

### Eksempler:
- Barnet **skal hete** Emma. (The baby will be called Emma.)
- Hva **skal det hete**? (What will it be called?)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**het** - be called! (very rarely used)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **heter ikke** Anna. (My name is not Anna / I'm not called Anna.)
- Han **het ikke** det. (He wasn't called that.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Hva heter du?** - What's your name? / What are you called?
- **Jeg heter...** - My name is... / I am called...
- **Hva heter det på norsk?** - What's that called in Norwegian?
- **Det heter... på norsk** - It's called... in Norwegian
- **Hva heter denne...?** - What is this... called?
- **Han/Hun heter...** - His/Her name is...

---

## Special Note: Usage

**å hete** is used for:
1. **Personal names**:
   - Jeg heter Patrick. (My name is Patrick.)

2. **What things are called**:
   - Hva heter det på norsk? (What is that called in Norwegian?)
   - Det heter "bok" på norsk. (It's called "bok" in Norwegian.)

3. **Names of places/things**:
   - Byen heter Oslo. (The city is called Oslo.)

---

## Common Questions and Answers

**Q: Hva heter du?** (What's your name?)
**A: Jeg heter [name].** (My name is [name].)

**Q: Hva heter han/hun?** (What's his/her name?)
**A: Han/Hun heter [name].** (His/Her name is [name].)

**Q: Hva heter det på norsk?** (What's that called in Norwegian?)
**A: Det heter [word] på norsk.** (It's called [word] in Norwegian.)

---

## Related Verbs

**å hete** - to be called (for names)
- Jeg **heter** Patrick.

**å kalle** - to call (active: to name someone/something)
- De **kaller** meg Pat. (They call me Pat.)
