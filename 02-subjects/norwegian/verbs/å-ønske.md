# å ønske (to wish / to want)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å ønske

**Meaning**: To wish or to want (more formal than "vil ha")

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | ønsker | I wish / I want |
| du | ønsker | you wish / you want |
| han/hun/det | ønsker | he/she/it wishes / wants |
| vi | ønsker | we wish / we want |
| dere | ønsker | you wish / you want (plural) |
| de | ønsker | they wish / they want |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **ønsker** deg lykke til! (I wish you good luck!)
- **Ønsker du** noe å drikke? (Do you want something to drink?)
- Hun **ønsker** å lære norsk. (She wants to learn Norwegian.)
- Vi **ønsker** dere velkommen! (We wish you welcome!)
- De **ønsker** ikke pizza. (They don't want pizza.)

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | ønsket | I wished / I wanted |

| han/hun/det | ønsket | he/she/it wished / wanted |
| vi | ønsket | we wished / we wanted |
| dere | ønsket | you wished / you wanted (plural) |
| de | ønsket | they wished / they wanted |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **ønsket** ham god bedring. (I wished him a speedy recovery.)
- **Ønsket du** å komme? (Did you want to come?)
- Vi **ønsket** å hjelpe. (We wanted to help.)
- Hun **ønsket** ham god jul. (She wished him Merry Christmas.)

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + ønsket

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har ønsket | I have wished / I have wanted |
| du | har ønsket | you have wished / you have wanted |
| han/hun/det | har ønsket | he/she/it has wished / has wanted |
| vi | har ønsket | we have wished / we have wanted |
| dere | har ønsket | you have wished / you have wanted (plural) |
| de | har ønsket | they have wished / they have wanted |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har alltid ønsket** å reise til Norge. (I have always wanted to travel to Norway.)
- **Har du ønsket** deg noe? (Have you wished for anything?)
- Vi **har ønsket** dette lenge. (We have wanted this for a long time.)

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + ønske

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal ønske / vil ønske | I will wish / I'm going to wish |
| du | skal ønske / vil ønske | you will wish |
| han/hun/det | skal ønske / vil ønske | he/she/it will wish |
| vi | skal ønske / vil ønske | we will wish |
| dere | skal ønske / vil ønske | you will wish (plural) |
| de | skal ønske / vil ønske | they will wish |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **vil ønske** deg god jul. (I will wish you Merry Christmas.)
- Vi **skal ønske** dem lykke til. (We're going to wish them good luck.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**ønsk** - wish!

### Eksempler:
- **Ønsk** ham lykke til! (Wish him good luck!)
- **Ønsk** deg noe! (Wish for something!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **ønsker ikke** å gå. (I don't wish to go / I don't want to go.)
- Vi **ønsket ikke** å forstyrre. (We didn't want to disturb.)
- Hun **har ikke ønsket** seg noe. (She hasn't wished for anything.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **ønske seg** - to wish for (for oneself)
  - Hva **ønsker du deg** til jul? (What do you want for Christmas?)
- **ønske velkommen** - to welcome / wish welcome
  - Vi **ønsker** dere **velkommen**! (We welcome you!)
- **ønske lykke til** - to wish good luck
  - Jeg **ønsker** deg **lykke til**! (I wish you good luck!)
- **ønske god bedring** - to wish a speedy recovery
  - Jeg **ønsker** deg **god bedring**. (I wish you a speedy recovery / Get well soon.)

---

## Common Uses

**Wishing greetings:**
- Jeg **ønsker** deg god jul. (I wish you Merry Christmas.)
- Vi **ønsker** deg godt nyttår. (We wish you Happy New Year.)
- **Ønsker** du god påske. (Wishing you Happy Easter.)

**Wanting something:**
- Jeg **ønsker** å lære norsk. (I want to learn Norwegian.)
- **Ønsker du** kaffe? (Do you want coffee?)
- Hun **ønsker** å bo i Norge. (She wants to live in Norway.)

**Wishing for things:**
- Hva **ønsker du deg**? (What do you want? / What do you wish for?)
- Jeg **ønsker meg** en ny bil. (I want a new car / I wish for a new car.)

---

## Important: ønske vs ville ha

**å ønske** = to wish / to want (more formal, polite)
- Jeg **ønsker** å spise nå. (I wish to eat now.) - formal/polite
- **Ønsker du** noe å drikke? (Do you want something to drink?) - polite

**vil ha** = to want (direct, common)
- Jeg **vil ha** mat nå. (I want food now.) - direct
- **Vil du ha** noe å drikke? (Do you want something to drink?) - common

Both are correct, but **ønske** is more polite and formal!

## Common Holiday Wishes

  - Jeg **ønsker** deg **god jul**. (I wish you Merry Christmas.)
- **Godt nyttår!** - Happy New Year!
  - Vi **ønsker** deg **godt nyttår**. (We wish you Happy New Year.)
- **God påske!** - Happy Easter!
  - **Ønsker** dere **god påske**. (Wishing you Happy Easter.)
- **Gratulerer med dagen!** - Happy birthday!
  - Jeg **ønsker** deg **gratulerer med dagen**! (I wish you happy birthday!)

## Other Common Wishes

- **God bedring** - Get well soon / Speedy recovery
  - Jeg **ønsker** deg **god bedring**. (I wish you a speedy recovery.)
- **Lykke til** - Good luck
  - Vi **ønsker** deg **lykke til**! (We wish you good luck!)
- **Velkommen** - Welcome
  - Vi **ønsker** deg **velkommen**! (We wish you welcome / We welcome you!)
- **God tur** - Have a good trip
  - **Ønsker** deg **god tur**! (Wishing you a good trip!)

---

## Pattern: ønske å + infinitive

**ønske å** = to wish to / to want to (do something)

Always use **å** before the next verb:
- Jeg **ønsker å** snakke med deg. ✅ (I wish to speak with you.)
- Jeg **ønsker** snakke med deg. ❌

---

## Common Questions

**Hva ønsker du?** - What do you want / wish?

**Hva ønsker du deg?** - What do you want (for yourself)?

**Ønsker du noe å drikke?** - Do you want something to drink?

**Kan jeg ønske deg lykke til?** - Can I wish you good luck?
