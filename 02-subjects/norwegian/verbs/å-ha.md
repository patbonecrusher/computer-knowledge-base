# å ha (to have)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å ha

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har | I have |
| du | har | you have |
| han/hun/det | har | he/she/it has |
| vi | har | we have |
| dere | har | you have (plural) |
| de | har | they have |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **har** en bil. (I have a car.)
- Du **har** et telefonnummer. (You have a phone number.)
- Hun **har** to barn. (She has two children.)
- Vi **har** en hund. (We have a dog.)
- De **har** tid. (They have time.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | hadde | I had |
| du | hadde | you had |
| han/hun/det | hadde | he/she/it had |
| vi | hadde | we had |
| dere | hadde | you had (plural) |
| de | hadde | they had |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **hadde** en bil. (I had a car.)
- Du **hadde** rett. (You were right / You had right.)
- Vi **hadde** det gøy. (We had fun.)
- De **hadde** tid. (They had time.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + hatt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har hatt | I have had |
| du | har hatt | you have had |
| han/hun/det | har hatt | he/she/it has had |
| vi | har hatt | we have had |
| dere | har hatt | you have had (plural) |
| de | har hatt | they have had |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har hatt** en fin dag. (I have had a nice day.)
- Hun **har hatt** mange biler. (She has had many cars.)
- Vi **har hatt** det bra. (We have had it good / We've been doing well.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + ha

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal ha / vil ha | I will have / I want to have |
| du | skal ha / vil ha | you will have |
| han/hun/det | skal ha / vil ha | he/she/it will have |
| vi | skal ha / vil ha | we will have |
| dere | skal ha / vil ha | you will have (plural) |
| de | skal ha / vil ha | they will have |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal ha** møte i morgen. (I will have a meeting tomorrow.)
- **Vil du ha** kaffe? (Do you want coffee? / Will you have coffee?)
- Vi **skal ha** det gøy! (We will have fun!)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**ha** - have!

### Eksempler:
- **Ha** det bra! (Have it good! / Take care! / Goodbye!)
- **Ha** en fin dag! (Have a nice day!)
- **Ha** det gøy! (Have fun!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **har ikke** bil. (I don't have a car.)
- Vi **hadde ikke** tid. (We didn't have time.)
- Hun **har ikke hatt** ferie. (She has not had vacation.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Har du det bra?** - Are you doing well? (literally: Do you have it good?)
- **Ha det!** - Bye! (literally: Have it!)
- **Jeg har lyst til...** - I feel like... / I want to...
- **Har du tid?** - Do you have time?
- **Ha en fin helg!** - Have a nice weekend!
- **Jeg har rett** - I am right (literally: I have right)
- **Du har feil** - You are wrong (literally: You have wrong)
