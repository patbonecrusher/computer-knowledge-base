# å smake (to taste)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å smake

**Meaning**: To taste (experience flavor) or to have a taste

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | smaker | I taste |
| du | smaker | you taste |
| han/hun/det | smaker | he/she/it tastes |
| vi | smaker | we taste |
| dere | smaker | you taste (plural) |
| de | smaker | they taste |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **smaker** på maten. (I taste the food.)
- **Smaker du** forskjell? (Do you taste the difference?)
- Det **smaker** godt! (It tastes good!)
- Maten **smaker** deilig. (The food tastes delicious.)
- De **smaker** ikke fisk. (They don't taste fish / don't like the taste.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | smakte | I tasted |
| du | smakte | you tasted |
| han/hun/det | smakte | he/she/it tasted |
| vi | smakte | we tasted |
| dere | smakte | you tasted (plural) |
| de | smakte | they tasted |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **smakte** på retten. (I tasted the dish.)
- **Smakte du** på osten? (Did you taste the cheese?)
- Det **smakte** ikke godt. (It didn't taste good.)
- Maten **smakte** fantastisk. (The food tasted fantastic.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + smakt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har smakt | I have tasted |
| du | har smakt | you have tasted |
| han/hun/det | har smakt | he/she/it has tasted |
| vi | har smakt | we have tasted |
| dere | har smakt | you have tasted (plural) |
| de | har smakt | they have tasted |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har smakt** på alle rettene. (I have tasted all the dishes.)
- **Har du smakt** norsk brunost? (Have you tasted Norwegian brown cheese?)
- Vi **har aldri smakt** dette før. (We have never tasted this before.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + smake

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal smake / vil smake | I will taste / I'm going to taste |
| du | skal smake / vil smake | you will taste |

| vi | skal smake / vil smake | we will taste |

| de | skal smake / vil smake | they will taste |

### Eksempler:
- Du **vil smake** forskjellen. (You will taste the difference.)
- Jeg **skal smake** på alt. (I'm going to taste everything.)
- Det **vil smake** godt! (It will taste good!)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**smak** - taste!

### Eksempler:
- **Smak** på dette! (Taste this!)
- **Smak** selv! (Taste it yourself!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **smaker ikke** forskjell. (I don't taste the difference.)
- Det **smakte ikke** godt. (It didn't taste good.)
- Hun **har ikke smakt** på det. (She hasn't tasted it.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **smake på** - to taste / to try (the taste of)
  - Kan jeg **smake på** dette? (Can I taste this?)
- **smake godt** - to taste good
  - Det **smaker** veldig **godt**! (It tastes very good!)
- **smake deilig** - to taste delicious
  - Maten **smaker deilig**. (The food tastes delicious.)
- **smake av** - to taste of (have flavor of)
  - Dette **smaker av** sitron. (This tastes of lemon.)

---

## Common Uses

**Tasting food:**
- **Smak** på maten! (Taste the food!)
- Jeg må **smake** på dette. (I must taste this.)
- Kan jeg **smake** litt? (Can I taste a little?)

**Describing taste:**
- Det **smaker** godt. (It tastes good.)
- Maten **smaker** deilig. (The food tastes delicious.)
- **Smaker** det salt? (Does it taste salty?)

**Tasting difference:**
- **Smaker du** forskjell? (Do you taste the difference?)
- Jeg **smaker** ikke noe. (I don't taste anything.)

---

## How Things Taste

- **smaker godt** - tastes good
- **smaker deilig** - tastes delicious
- **smaker søtt** - tastes sweet
- **smaker salt** - tastes salty
- **smaker surt** - tastes sour
- **smaker bittert** - tastes bitter
- **smaker rart** - tastes weird
- **smaker ikke noe** - doesn't taste of anything

---

## Prepositions with "smake"

- **smake på** - to taste (sample)
  - Jeg **smaker på** maten. (I taste the food.)
- **smake av** - to taste of
  - Det **smaker av** vanilje. (It tastes of vanilla.)
- **smake som** - to taste like
  - Det **smaker som** sitron. (It tastes like lemon.)

---

## Special Note

**å smake** can mean two things:

1. **To taste (action)** - when someone tastes something
   - Jeg **smaker** på maten. (I taste the food.)

2. **To taste (quality)** - when something has a taste
   - Det **smaker** godt. (It tastes good.)

The context usually makes it clear which meaning is intended!

---

## Common Questions

**Smaker det godt?** - Does it taste good?

**Hva smaker det som?** - What does it taste like?

**Kan jeg smake på det?** - Can I taste that?

**Smaker du forskjell?** - Do you taste the difference?
