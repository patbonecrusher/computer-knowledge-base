# å bestille (to order)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å bestille

**Meaning**: To order (food, goods, services)

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | bestiller | I order |
| du | bestiller | you order |
| han/hun/det | bestiller | he/she/it orders |
| vi | bestiller | we order |
| dere | bestiller | you order (plural) |
| de | bestiller | they order |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **bestiller** pizza. (I order pizza.)
- **Bestiller du** mat? (Are you ordering food?)
- Hun **bestiller** alltid kaffe. (She always orders coffee.)
- Vi **bestiller** mat på nettet. (We order food online.)
- De **bestiller** ikke kjøtt. (They don't order meat.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | bestilte | I ordered |
| du | bestilte | you ordered |
| han/hun/det | bestilte | he/she/it ordered |
| vi | bestilte | we ordered |
| dere | bestilte | you ordered (plural) |
| de | bestilte | they ordered |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **bestilte** pizza i går. (I ordered pizza yesterday.)
- **Bestilte du** noe? (Did you order anything?)
- Vi **bestilte** mat til lunsj. (We ordered food for lunch.)
- Hun **bestilte** en salat. (She ordered a salad.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + bestilt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har bestilt | I have ordered |
| du | har bestilt | you have ordered |
| han/hun/det | har bestilt | he/she/it has ordered |
| vi | har bestilt | we have ordered |
| dere | har bestilt | you have ordered (plural) |
| de | har bestilt | they have ordered |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har allerede bestilt** mat. (I have already ordered food.)
- **Har du bestilt** bord? (Have you ordered a table / made a reservation?)
- Vi **har bestilt** pizza til middag. (We have ordered pizza for dinner.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + bestille

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal bestille / vil bestille | I will order / I'm going to order |
| du | skal bestille / vil bestille | you will order |
| han/hun/det | skal bestille / vil bestille | he/she/it will order |
| vi | skal bestille / vil bestille | we will order |
| dere | skal bestille / vil bestille | you will order (plural) |
| de | skal bestille / vil bestille | they will order |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal bestille** mat nå. (I'm going to order food now.)
- **Vil du bestille** pizza? (Will you order pizza?)
- Vi **skal bestille** bord til åtte. (We're going to order a table for eight / make a reservation for 8pm.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**bestill** - order!

### Eksempler:
- **Bestill** pizza til oss! (Order pizza for us!)
- **Bestill** bord! (Make a reservation! / Order a table!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **bestiller ikke** mat i dag. (I'm not ordering food today.)
- Vi **bestilte ikke** noe. (We didn't order anything.)
- Hun **har ikke bestilt** ennå. (She hasn't ordered yet.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **bestille mat** - to order food
  - Skal vi **bestille mat**? (Should we order food?)
- **bestille bord** - to reserve a table / make a reservation
  - Jeg må **bestille bord**. (I need to reserve a table.)
- **bestille hjem** - to order for home delivery
  - Vi **bestiller** pizza **hjem**. (We order pizza for home delivery.)
- **bestille på nettet** - to order online
  - Hun **bestiller** alltid **på nettet**. (She always orders online.)

---

## Common Uses

**Ordering food:**
- Skal vi **bestille** pizza? (Should we order pizza?)
- Jeg **bestiller** ofte mat hjem. (I often order food for delivery.)
- **Bestiller du** eller skal jeg? (Are you ordering or should I?)

**Making reservations:**
- Jeg må **bestille** bord på restauranten. (I need to reserve a table at the restaurant.)
- **Har du bestilt** bord? (Have you made a reservation?)

**Ordering online:**
- Vi **bestiller** mat på nettet. (We order food online.)
- Hun **bestiller** alltid fra denne siden. (She always orders from this site.)

---

## What to Order

- **bestille pizza** - order pizza
- **bestille pasta** - order pasta
- **bestille sushi** - order sushi
- **bestille kaffe** - order coffee
- **bestille mat** - order food
- **bestille drikke** - order drinks
- **bestille dessert** - order dessert
- **bestille takeaway** - order takeout

---

## Prepositions with "bestille"

- **bestille fra** - to order from
  - Jeg **bestiller** mat **fra** den restauranten. (I order food from that restaurant.)
- **bestille til** - to order for (delivery)
  - Vi **bestiller** pizza **til** hjemmet. (We order pizza for home.)
- **bestille på** - to order at/on
  - Hun **bestiller** **på** nettet. (She orders online.)

---

## Special Note

**bestille bord** = to reserve a table (NOT to order a table as furniture!)

This is a very common expression in Norwegian:
- Jeg vil **bestille bord** til fire personer. (I want to reserve a table for four people.)
- **Har du bestilt bord**? (Have you made a reservation?)

---

## Common Questions

**Skal vi bestille?** - Should we order?

**Hva skal du bestille?** - What are you going to order?

**Har du bestilt bord?** - Have you made a reservation?

**Kan jeg bestille mat?** - Can I order food?

---

## At a Restaurant

**Formal ordering:**
- Jeg vil gjerne **bestille**... (I would like to order...)
- Kan jeg **bestille**...? (Can I order...?)

**Asking someone:**
- Er dere klar til å **bestille**? (Are you ready to order?)
- Hva vil du **bestille**? (What do you want to order?)
