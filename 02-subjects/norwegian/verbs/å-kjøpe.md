# å kjøpe (to buy)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å kjøpe

**Meaning**: To buy or to purchase

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | kjøper | I buy |
| du | kjøper | you buy |
| han/hun/det | kjøper | he/she/it buys |
| vi | kjøper | we buy |
| dere | kjøper | you buy (plural) |
| de | kjøper | they buy |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **kjøper** mat i butikken. (I buy food at the store.)
- **Kjøper du** brød hver dag? (Do you buy bread every day?)
- Hun **kjøper** frukt og grønnsaker. (She buys fruit and vegetables.)
- Vi **kjøper** alltid fersk fisk. (We always buy fresh fish.)
- De **kjøper** ikke kjøtt. (They don't buy meat.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | kjøpte | I bought |
| du | kjøpte | you bought |
| han/hun/det | kjøpte | he/she/it bought |
| vi | kjøpte | we bought |
| dere | kjøpte | you bought (plural) |
| de | kjøpte | they bought |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **kjøpte** melk i går. (I bought milk yesterday.)
- **Kjøpte du** noe? (Did you buy anything?)
- Vi **kjøpte** pizza til middag. (We bought pizza for dinner.)
- Hun **kjøpte** mange grønnsaker. (She bought many vegetables.)

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + kjøpt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har kjøpt | I have bought |
| du | har kjøpt | you have bought |
| han/hun/det | har kjøpt | he/she/it has bought |
| vi | har kjøpt | we have bought |
| dere | har kjøpt | you have bought (plural) |
| de | har kjøpt | they have bought |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har kjøpt** all maten vi trenger. (I have bought all the food we need.)
- **Har du kjøpt** brød? (Have you bought bread?)
- Vi **har allerede kjøpt** mat for uka. (We have already bought food for the week.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**kjøp** - buy!

### Eksempler:
- **Kjøp** melk når du er i butikken! (Buy milk when you're at the store!)
- **Kjøp** ikke for mye. (Don't buy too much.)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **kjøper ikke** kjøtt. (I don't buy meat.)
- Vi **kjøpte ikke** noe i dag. (We didn't buy anything today.)
- Hun **har ikke kjøpt** mat ennå. (She hasn't bought food yet.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **kjøpe mat** - to buy food / to go grocery shopping
  - Jeg må **kjøpe mat**. (I need to buy food / go grocery shopping.)
- **kjøpe inn** - to stock up
  - Vi **kjøper inn** mat for uka. (We stock up on food for the week.)
- **kjøpe med seg** - to buy to bring along
  - Kan du **kjøpe med deg** brød? (Can you buy bread to bring?)
- **kjøpe seg** - to buy for oneself
  - Jeg **kjøper meg** en kaffe. (I'm buying myself a coffee.)

---

## Common Uses

**Shopping for food:**
- Jeg **kjøper** mat hver onsdag. (I buy food every Wednesday.)
- Hvor **kjøper du** mat? (Where do you buy food?)
- Vi **kjøper** alltid økologisk mat. (We always buy organic food.)

**What to buy:**
- Hva skal vi **kjøpe** til middag? (What should we buy for dinner?)
- Jeg må **kjøpe** brød og melk. (I need to buy bread and milk.)

**Where to buy:**
- Vi **kjøper** fisk på torget. (We buy fish at the market.)
- Hun **kjøper** alltid i samme butikk. (She always shops at the same store.)

---

## Prepositions with "kjøpe"

- **kjøpe i** - to buy at/in (location)
  - Jeg **kjøper** mat **i** butikken. (I buy food at the store.)
- **kjøpe hos** - to buy from (person/company)
  - Vi **kjøper** fisk **hos** fiskeren. (We buy fish from the fisherman.)
- **kjøpe på** - to buy at (market, online)
  - Hun **kjøper** ting **på** nettet. (She buys things online.)
- **kjøpe til** - to buy for (occasion/person)
  - Jeg **kjøper** mat **til** middagen. (I buy food for dinner.)

---
