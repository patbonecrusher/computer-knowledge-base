# å være (to be)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å være

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | er | I am |
| du | er | you are |
| han/hun/det | er | he/she/it is |
| vi | er | we are |
| dere | er | you are (plural) |
| de | er | they are |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **er** student. (I am a student.)
- Du **er** norsk. (You are Norwegian.)
- Hun **er** lærer. (She is a teacher.)
- Vi **er** fra Canada. (We are from Canada.)
- De **er** hjemme. (They are at home.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | var | I was |
| du | var | you were |
| han/hun/det | var | he/she/it was |
| vi | var | we were |
| dere | var | you were (plural) |
| de | var | they were |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **var** i Oslo i går. (I was in Oslo yesterday.)
- Du **var** syk. (You were sick.)
- Vi **var** på skolen. (We were at school.)
- De **var** her. (They were here.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + vært

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har vært | I have been |
| du | har vært | you have been |
| han/hun/det | har vært | he/she/it has been |
| vi | har vært | we have been |
| dere | har vært | you have been (plural) |
| de | har vært | they have been |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har vært** i Norge. (I have been to Norway.)
- Hun **har vært** syk. (She has been sick.)
- Vi **har vært** her før. (We have been here before.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + være

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal være / vil være | I will be / I'm going to be |
| du | skal være / vil være | you will be |
| han/hun/det | skal være / vil være | he/she/it will be |
| vi | skal være / vil være | we will be |
| dere | skal være / vil være | you will be (plural) |
| de | skal være / vil være | they will be |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal være** der i morgen. (I will be there tomorrow.)
- Vi **vil være** hjemme i kveld. (We will be home tonight.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**vær** - be!

### Eksempler:
- **Vær** snill! (Be nice!)
- **Vær** rolig! (Be calm!)
- **Vær** forsiktig! (Be careful!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **er ikke** norsk. (I am not Norwegian.)
- Vi **var ikke** der. (We were not there.)
- Hun **har ikke vært** her. (She has not been here.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Hvordan er det?** - How is it?
- **Det er bra** - That's good / It's good
- **Jeg er enig** - I agree (literally: I am in agreement)
- **Vær så god** - Here you are / You're welcome
- **Er du klar?** - Are you ready?
