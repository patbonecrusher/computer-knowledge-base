# å lære (to learn / to teach)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å lære

**Important**: This verb means both "to learn" AND "to teach" depending on context!

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | lærer | I learn / I teach |
| du | lærer | you learn / you teach |
| han/hun/det | lærer | he/she/it learns / teaches |
| vi | lærer | we learn / we teach |
| dere | lærer | you learn / you teach (plural) |
| de | lærer | they learn / they teach |

### Eksempler (Examples):

**Learning:**
- Jeg **lærer** norsk. (I'm learning Norwegian / I learn Norwegian.)
- Vi **lærer** nye ord hver dag. (We learn new words every day.)
- Hun **lærer** fort. (She learns quickly.)

**Teaching:**
- Jeg **lærer** norsk til studenter. (I teach Norwegian to students.)
- Hun **lærer** barn. (She teaches children.)
- Han **lærer** oss norsk. (He teaches us Norwegian.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | lærte | I learned / I taught |
| du | lærte | you learned / you taught |
| han/hun/det | lærte | he/she/it learned / taught |
| vi | lærte | we learned / we taught |
| dere | lærte | you learned / you taught (plural) |
| de | lærte | they learned / they taught |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **lærte** norsk i fjor. (I learned Norwegian last year.)
- Hun **lærte** meg å snakke norsk. (She taught me to speak Norwegian.)
- Vi **lærte** mye. (We learned a lot.)
- De **lærte** ikke noe. (They didn't learn anything.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + lært

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har lært | I have learned / I have taught |
| du | har lært | you have learned / you have taught |
| han/hun/det | har lært | he/she/it has learned / has taught |
| vi | har lært | we have learned / we have taught |
| dere | har lært | you have learned / you have taught (plural) |
| de | har lært | they have learned / they have taught |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har lært** mye norsk. (I have learned a lot of Norwegian.)
- Hun **har lært** norsk i tre år. (She has learned Norwegian for three years.)
- Vi **har lært** dem å snakke norsk. (We have taught them to speak Norwegian.)

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + lære

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal lære / vil lære | I will learn / I'm going to learn |
| du | skal lære / vil lære | you will learn |
| han/hun/det | skal lære / vil lære | he/she/it will learn |
| vi | skal lære / vil lære | we will learn |
| dere | skal lære / vil lære | you will learn (plural) |
| de | skal lære / vil lære | they will learn |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal lære** norsk. (I'm going to learn Norwegian.)
- **Vil du lære** mer? (Do you want to learn more?)
- Vi **skal lære** dem norsk. (We're going to teach them Norwegian.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**lær** - learn! / teach!

### Eksempler:
- **Lær** norsk! (Learn Norwegian!)
- **Lær** dem dette! (Teach them this!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **lærer ikke** norsk. (I'm not learning Norwegian.)
- Vi **lærte ikke** det. (We didn't learn that.)
- Hun **har ikke lært** norsk. (She has not learned Norwegian.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Jeg vil lære norsk** - I want to learn Norwegian
- **lære seg** - to learn (reflexive - emphasizes self-learning)
  - Jeg **lærer meg** norsk. (I'm teaching myself Norwegian.)
- **lære bort** - to teach (specifically)
  - Hun **lærer bort** norsk. (She teaches Norwegian.)
- **lære å...** - to learn to... (+ verb)
  - Jeg vil **lære å snakke** norsk. (I want to learn to speak Norwegian.)

---

## Special Note: Learn vs Teach

**Context determines meaning!**

### Learning (most common):
- Jeg **lærer** norsk. (I'm learning Norwegian.)
- Du **lærer** fort! (You learn quickly!)

### Teaching (with object):
- Jeg **lærer** deg norsk. (I teach you Norwegian.)
- Hun **lærer** barn. (She teaches children.)

### Clarifying:

**To emphasize learning**: Use **lære seg**
- Jeg **lærer meg** norsk. (I'm learning Norwegian / teaching myself Norwegian.)

**To emphasize teaching**: Use **lære bort**
- Jeg **lærer bort** norsk. (I teach Norwegian.)

---

## Patterns with "å"

**lære å** + infinitive = learn to (do something)
- Jeg vil **lære å snakke** norsk. (I want to learn to speak Norwegian.)
- Hun **lærer å lese**. (She's learning to read.)
- Vi må **lære å forstå**. (We must learn to understand.)

**Note**: "lære" is NOT a modal verb, so it takes "å" before infinitives!

---

## Common Questions

**Hva lærer du?** - What are you learning?
- Jeg **lærer** norsk. (I'm learning Norwegian.)

**Hvor lærer du norsk?** - Where do you learn Norwegian?
- Jeg **lærer** norsk på skolen. (I learn Norwegian at school.)

**Hvem lærte deg det?** - Who taught you that?
- Læreren **lærte** meg det. (The teacher taught me that.)
