# å kjenne (to know)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å kjenne

**Meaning**: To know people, places, or be familiar with something

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | kjenner | I know |
| du | kjenner | you know |
| han/hun/det | kjenner | he/she/it knows |
| vi | kjenner | we know |
| dere | kjenner | you know (plural) |
| de | kjenner | they know |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **kjenner** henne. (I know her.)
- **Kjenner du** Oslo? (Do you know Oslo?)
- Hun **kjenner** familien min. (She knows my family.)
- Vi **kjenner** hverandre. (We know each other.)
- De **kjenner** ikke stedet. (They don't know the place.)

**Pattern**: har + kjent

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|

| han/hun/det | har kjent | he/she/it has known |
| vi | har kjent | we have known |
| dere | har kjent | you have known (plural) |
| de | har kjent | they have known |

### Eksempler:

- **Har du kjent** ham lenge? (Have you known him long?)
- Vi **har kjent** hverandre siden barndommen. (We have known each other since childhood.)

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**kjenn** - know! (rarely used as command)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **kjenner ikke** ham. (I don't know him.)
- Vi **kjente ikke** stedet. (We didn't know the place.)
- Hun **har ikke kjent** dem lenge. (She hasn't known them long.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Kjenner du...?** - Do you know...? (person/place)
- **Jeg kjenner** - I know (person/place)
- **kjenne til** - to know of / to be aware of
  - Jeg **kjenner til** problemet. (I'm aware of the problem / I know of the problem.)
- **kjenne igjen** - to recognize
  - Jeg **kjenner** deg **igjen**. (I recognize you.)
- **kjenne på** - to feel (emotion/sensation)
  - Jeg **kjenner på** tristhet. (I feel sadness.)
- **kjenne seg** - to feel (oneself)
  - Jeg **kjenner meg** trøtt. (I feel tired.)

---

## Important: kjenne vs vite

**å kjenne** = to know (people, places, be familiar with)
- Jeg **kjenner** henne. (I know her.) - person
- Jeg **kjenner** Oslo godt. (I know Oslo well.) - place
- **Kjenner du** denne sangen? (Do you know this song?) - familiarity

**å vite** = to know (facts, information)
- Jeg **vet** hvor hun bor. (I know where she lives.) - fact
- **Vet du** svaret? (Do you know the answer?) - information

---

## Common Uses

**Knowing people:**
- **Kjenner du** ham? (Do you know him?)
- Jeg **kjenner** familien hans. (I know his family.)
- Vi **kjenner** hverandre godt. (We know each other well.)

**Knowing places:**
- **Kjenner du** Oslo? (Do you know Oslo?)
- Hun **kjenner** veien. (She knows the way.)
- Jeg **kjenner** dette stedet. (I know this place.)

**Being familiar with:**
- **Kjenner du** denne boken? (Do you know this book? / Are you familiar with this book?)
- Jeg **kjenner** ikke denne musikken. (I don't know this music / I'm not familiar with this music.)

---

## Special Expressions

**kjenne til** = know of / be aware of
- Jeg **kjenner til** problemet. (I'm aware of the problem.)
- **Kjenner du til** denne historien? (Do you know of this story?)

**kjenne igjen** = recognize
- Jeg **kjenner** deg **igjen**! (I recognize you!)
- **Kjenner du igjen** stedet? (Do you recognize the place?)

**kjenne seg** = feel (oneself)
- Jeg **kjenner meg** glad. (I feel happy.)
- Hun **kjenner seg** trøtt. (She feels tired.)

**kjenne på** = feel (emotion)
- Jeg **kjenner på** tristhet. (I feel sadness.)
- Vi **kjenner på** glede. (We feel joy.)

---

## Common Questions

**Kjenner du...?** - Do you know...? (person/place)
- **Kjenner du** ham? (Do you know him?)
- **Kjenner du** Oslo? (Do you know Oslo?)

**Kjenner du til...?** - Do you know of...?
- **Kjenner du til** dette problemet? (Do you know of this problem?)

**Kjente du...?** - Did you know...?
- **Kjente du** henne før? (Did you know her before?)

---

## Time Expressions with kjenne

- **Vi har kjent hverandre lenge** - We have known each other for a long time
- **Hun kjente stedet godt** - She knew the place well
