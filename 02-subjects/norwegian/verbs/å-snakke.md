# å snakke (to speak / to talk)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å snakke

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | snakker | I speak / I talk |
| du | snakker | you speak / you talk |
| han/hun/det | snakker | he/she/it speaks / talks |
| vi | snakker | we speak / we talk |
| dere | snakker | you speak / you talk (plural) |
| de | snakker | they speak / they talk |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **snakker** norsk. (I speak Norwegian.)
- **Snakker du** engelsk? (Do you speak English?)
- Hun **snakker** tre språk. (She speaks three languages.)
- Vi **snakker** sammen. (We talk together.)
- De **snakker** på telefonen. (They are talking on the phone.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | snakket | I spoke / I talked |
| du | snakket | you spoke / you talked |
| han/hun/det | snakket | he/she/it spoke / talked |
| vi | snakket | we spoke / we talked |
| dere | snakket | you spoke / you talked (plural) |
| de | snakket | they spoke / they talked |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **snakket** med henne i går. (I talked with her yesterday.)
- Vi **snakket** norsk. (We spoke Norwegian.)
- De **snakket** om deg. (They talked about you.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + snakket

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har snakket | I have spoken / I have talked |
| du | har snakket | you have spoken / you have talked |
| han/hun/det | har snakket | he/she/it has spoken / has talked |
| vi | har snakket | we have spoken / we have talked |
| dere | har snakket | you have spoken / you have talked (plural) |
| de | har snakket | they have spoken / they have talked |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har snakket** med læreren. (I have spoken with the teacher.)
- Vi **har snakket** om dette før. (We have talked about this before.)
- Hun **har snakket** norsk i to år. (She has spoken Norwegian for two years.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + snakke

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal snakke / vil snakke | I will speak / I'm going to speak |
| du | skal snakke / vil snakke | you will speak |
| han/hun/det | skal snakke / vil snakke | he/she/it will speak |
| vi | skal snakke / vil snakke | we will speak |
| dere | skal snakke / vil snakke | you will speak (plural) |
| de | skal snakke / vil snakke | they will speak |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal snakke** med sjefen i morgen. (I will speak with the boss tomorrow.)
- Vi **skal snakke** senere. (We'll talk later.)
- Hun **vil snakke** norsk. (She wants to speak Norwegian.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**snakk** - speak! / talk!

### Eksempler:
- **Snakk** norsk! (Speak Norwegian!)
- **Snakk** høyere! (Speak louder!)
- **Snakk** sakte! (Speak slowly!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **snakker ikke** norsk. (I don't speak Norwegian.)
- Vi **snakket ikke** sammen. (We didn't talk together.)
- Hun **har ikke snakket** med ham. (She has not spoken with him.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Snakker du norsk?** - Do you speak Norwegian?
- **Vi snakkes!** - We'll talk! / Talk to you later!
- **Kan du snakke saktere?** - Can you speak more slowly?
- **Snakk for deg selv** - Speak for yourself
- **Vi må snakke** - We need to talk
- **Snakke om...** - To talk about...

---

## Special Note: "snakke" vs "si" vs "fortelle"

**å snakke** = to speak/talk (general conversation)
- Jeg **snakker** norsk. (I speak Norwegian.)

**å si** = to say (specific words/statements)
- Hva **sier** du? (What are you saying?)

**å fortelle** = to tell (stories, information)
- Jeg **forteller** en historie. (I'm telling a story.)
