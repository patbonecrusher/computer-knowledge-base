# å arbeide (to work)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å arbeide

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | arbeider | I work |
| du | arbeider | you work |
| han/hun/det | arbeider | he/she/it works |
| vi | arbeider | we work |
| dere | arbeider | you work (plural) |
| de | arbeider | they work |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **arbeider** på et kontor. (I work at an office.)
- **Hvor arbeider du?** (Where do you work?)
- Hun **arbeider** som lærer. (She works as a teacher.)
- Vi **arbeider** sammen. (We work together.)
- De **arbeider** hardt. (They work hard.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|

| han/hun/det | arbeidet | he/she/it worked |
| vi | arbeidet | we worked |
| dere | arbeidet | you worked (plural) |
| de | arbeidet | they worked |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **arbeidet** i går. (I worked yesterday.)
- Du **arbeidet** for mye. (You worked too much.)

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

| han/hun/det | har arbeidet | he/she/it has worked |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har arbeidet** her i fem år. (I have worked here for five years.)
- Hun **har arbeidet** som lærer. (She has worked as a teacher.)
- Vi **har arbeidet** hardt. (We have worked hard.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**arbeid** - work!

### Eksempler:
- **Arbeid** hardt! (Work hard!)
- **Arbeid** sammen! (Work together!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **arbeider ikke** i dag. (I'm not working today.)
- Vi **arbeidet ikke** i går. (We didn't work yesterday.)
- Hun **har ikke arbeidet** her. (She has not worked here.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Hvor arbeider du?** - Where do you work?
- **Hva arbeider du med?** - What do you work with?
- **arbeide som** - to work as
  - Jeg **arbeider som** lærer. (I work as a teacher.)
- **arbeide på** - to work at/on
  - Jeg **arbeider på** et kontor. (I work at an office.)
- **arbeide med** - to work with
  - Jeg **arbeider med** data. (I work with computers/data.)
- **arbeide sammen** - to work together
- **arbeide hardt** - to work hard
- **gå på arbeid** - to go to work
  - Jeg **går på arbeid** nå. (I'm going to work now.)

---

## Prepositions with "arbeide"

**arbeide på** = work at (location)
- Jeg **arbeider på** et sykehus. (I work at a hospital.)
- Hun **arbeider på** skolen. (She works at the school.)

**arbeide som** = work as (profession)
- Jeg **arbeider som** lærer. (I work as a teacher.)
- Han **arbeider som** lege. (He works as a doctor.)

**arbeide med** = work with (tasks/tools/people)
- Jeg **arbeider med** barn. (I work with children.)
- Vi **arbeider med** data. (We work with data.)
- De **arbeider med** norsk. (They're working on Norwegian.)

---

## Related Vocabulary

**arbeid** (noun) = work (the noun)
- Jeg har mye **arbeid**. (I have a lot of work.)
- **Arbeid**et er vanskelig. (The work is difficult.)

**på arbeid** = at work
- Jeg er **på arbeid** nå. (I'm at work now.)

**gå til arbeid** / **gå på arbeid** = go to work
- Jeg **går til arbeid** hver dag. (I go to work every day.)

---

## Special Note: arbeide vs jobbe

**å arbeide** = to work (formal/general)
- Jeg **arbeider** som lærer.

**å jobbe** = to work (informal/colloquial)
- Jeg **jobber** som lærer.

Both are correct! **Jobbe** is more common in casual speech.
