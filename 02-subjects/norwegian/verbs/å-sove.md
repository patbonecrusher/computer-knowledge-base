# å sove (to sleep)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å sove

**Meaning**: To sleep

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | sover | I sleep |
| du | sover | you sleep |
| han/hun/det | sover | he/she/it sleeps |
| vi | sover | we sleep |
| dere | sover | you sleep (plural) |
| de | sover | they sleep |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **sover** åtte timer hver natt. (I sleep eight hours every night.)
- **Sover du** godt? (Do you sleep well?)
- Hun **sover** alltid tidlig. (She always sleeps early.)
- Vi **sover** i soverommet. (We sleep in the bedroom.)
- De **sover** lenge på helgen. (They sleep long on the weekend.)

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**sov** - sleep!

### Eksempler:
- **Sov** godt! (Sleep well!)
- **Sov** lenge! (Sleep long!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **sover ikke** godt. (I don't sleep well.)
- Vi **sov ikke** nok i går. (We didn't sleep enough yesterday.)
- Hun **har ikke sovet** i natt. (She hasn't slept tonight.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **sove godt** - to sleep well
  - Jeg **sover godt** hver natt. (I sleep well every night.)
- **sove dårlig** - to sleep poorly
  - Han **sov dårlig** i går. (He slept poorly yesterday.)
- **sove lenge** - to sleep long
  - Jeg **sover lenge** på søndag. (I sleep long on Sunday.)
- **sove nok** - to sleep enough
  - **Sover du nok?** (Do you sleep enough?)
- **sove fast** - to sleep soundly/deeply
  - Babyen **sover fast**. (The baby sleeps soundly.)
- **sove ute** - to sleep outside
  - Vi **sover ute** i telt. (We sleep outside in a tent.)

---

## Common Uses

**Duration of sleep:**
- Jeg **sover** åtte timer hver natt. (I sleep eight hours every night.)
- Hun **sover** bare fem timer. (She sleeps only five hours.)
- Vi **sov** ti timer i går. (We slept ten hours yesterday.)

**Quality of sleep:**
- Jeg **sover** godt. (I sleep well.)
- **Sov** du dårlig? (Did you sleep poorly?)
- Vi **sov** rolig. (We slept peacefully.)

**Location:**
- Jeg **sover** i soverommet. (I sleep in the bedroom.)
- Vi **sov** på hotellet. (We slept at the hotel.)
- De **sover** ute i telt. (They sleep outside in a tent.)

## Time Expressions with "sove"

- **sove om natten** - sleep at night
  - Jeg **sover** alltid **om natten**. (I always sleep at night.)
- **sove på dagen** - sleep during the day
  - Babyen **sover på dagen**. (The baby sleeps during the day.)
- **sove hver natt** - sleep every night
  - Vi **sover** åtte timer **hver natt**. (We sleep eight hours every night.)
- **sove i natt** - sleep tonight
  - Jeg skal **sove** godt **i natt**. (I'm going to sleep well tonight.)

## Common Questions

**Hvor mange timer sover du?** - How many hours do you sleep?

**Når skal du sove?** - When are you going to sleep?

**Sov du godt i natt?** - Did you sleep well last night?

**Hvor sover du?** - Where do you sleep?

## Related Verbs

**å legge seg** - to go to bed (action before sleeping)
- Jeg **legger meg** klokken elleve og **sover** til klokken syv. (I go to bed at 11 and sleep until 7.)

**å våkne** - to wake up (opposite action)
- Jeg **sover** om natten og **våkner** om morgenen. (I sleep at night and wake up in the morning.)

**å hvile** - to rest
- Jeg trenger å **hvile**, ikke å **sove**. (I need to rest, not to sleep.)

---

## Duration with "sove"

**sove i [duration]** - sleep for [duration]
- Jeg **sov i** åtte timer. (I slept for eight hours.)
- Hun **sover i** ti timer hver natt. (She sleeps for ten hours every night.)

**sove til [time]** - sleep until [time]
- Jeg **sover til** klokken åtte. (I sleep until 8 o'clock.)
- Vi **sov til** klokken ti i dag. (We slept until 10 today.)

**sove fra [time] til [time]** - sleep from [time] to [time]
- Jeg **sover fra** elleve **til** syv. (I sleep from 11 to 7.)

---

## Frequency Adverbs with "sove"

- **alltid sove** - always sleep
  - Jeg **sover alltid** godt. (I always sleep well.)
- **vanligvis sove** - usually sleep
  - Vi **sover vanligvis** åtte timer. (We usually sleep eight hours.)
- **ofte sove** - often sleep
  - Hun **sover ofte** lenge. (She often sleeps long.)
- **noen ganger sove** - sometimes sleep
  - Jeg **sover noen ganger** dårlig. (I sometimes sleep poorly.)
- **sjelden sove** - rarely sleep
  - Han **sover sjelden** nok. (He rarely sleeps enough.)
- **aldri sove** - never sleep
  - Jeg **sover aldri** på dagen. (I never sleep during the day.)

---

## Special Expressions

**Sove som en stein** - Sleep like a rock
- Jeg **sov som en stein** i natt. (I slept like a rock last night.)

**Sove søtt** - Sleep sweetly (for children)
- **Sov søtt**, lille venn! (Sleep sweetly, little friend!)

**Ikke få sove** - Can't sleep / Not get sleep
- Jeg **fikk ikke sove** i går. (I couldn't sleep yesterday.)

**Gå og legge seg** vs **Sove**
- Jeg **går og legger meg** klokken elleve. (I go to bed at 11.)
- Jeg **sover** fra elleve til syv. (I sleep from 11 to 7.)
- Note: "legge seg" = going to bed, "sove" = the act of sleeping

**Sov godt!** - Sleep well!
- Common farewell before bedtime

## Nouns Related to Sleep

- **søvn** (m) - sleep (noun)
  - Jeg trenger mer **søvn**. (I need more sleep.)
- **en middagslur** (m) - a nap
  - Jeg tar en **middagslur**. (I take a nap.)
- **en blund** (m) - a snooze
  - Jeg skal ta en **blund**. (I'm going to take a snooze.)
