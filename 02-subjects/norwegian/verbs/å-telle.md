# å telle (to count)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å telle

**Meaning**: To count or to number

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | teller | I count |
| du | teller | you count |
| han/hun/det | teller | he/she/it counts |
| vi | teller | we count |
| dere | teller | you count (plural) |
| de | teller | they count |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **teller** til ti. (I count to ten.)
- **Teller du** pengene? (Are you counting the money?)
- Hun **teller** alltid fort. (She always counts fast.)
- Vi **teller** sammen. (We count together.)
- De **teller** sauene. (They count the sheep.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | talte | I counted |
| du | talte | you counted |
| han/hun/det | talte | he/she/it counted |
| vi | talte | we counted |
| dere | talte | you counted (plural) |
| de | talte | they counted |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **talte** pengene mine. (I counted my money.)
- **Talte du** til hundre? (Did you count to one hundred?)
- Vi **talte** alle stemmene. (We counted all the votes.)
- Hun **talte** sauene før hun sov. (She counted the sheep before she slept.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + talt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har talt | I have counted |
| du | har talt | you have counted |
| han/hun/det | har talt | he/she/it has counted |
| vi | har talt | we have counted |
| dere | har talt | you have counted (plural) |
| de | har talt | they have counted |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har talt** pengene to ganger. (I have counted the money twice.)
- **Har du talt** alle? (Have you counted everyone?)
- Vi **har allerede talt** stemmene. (We have already counted the votes.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + telle

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal telle / vil telle | I will count / I'm going to count |
| du | skal telle / vil telle | you will count |
| han/hun/det | skal telle / vil telle | he/she/it will count |
| vi | skal telle / vil telle | we will count |
| dere | skal telle / vil telle | you will count (plural) |
| de | skal telle / vil telle | they will count |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal telle** pengene i morgen. (I'm going to count the money tomorrow.)
- **Vil du telle** for meg? (Will you count for me?)
- Vi **skal telle** alle sammen. (We're going to count everyone.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**tell** - count!

### Eksempler:
- **Tell** til ti! (Count to ten!)
- **Tell** pengene dine! (Count your money!)
- **Tell** ikke høyt. (Don't count out loud.)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **teller ikke** så fort. (I don't count that fast.)
- Vi **talte ikke** alle. (We didn't count everyone.)
- Hun **har ikke talt** pengene ennå. (She hasn't counted the money yet.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **telle til** - to count to
  - Jeg kan **telle til** hundre. (I can count to one hundred.)
- **telle opp** - to count up
  - Vi må **telle opp** alle pengene. (We need to count up all the money.)
- **telle ned** - to count down
  - Vi **teller ned** fra ti. (We count down from ten.)
- **telle sauer** - to count sheep (to fall asleep)
  - Jeg **teller sauer** når jeg ikke kan sove. (I count sheep when I can't sleep.)
- **telle med** - to count in / to include
  - **Teller du meg med**? (Are you counting me in?)

---

## Common Uses

**Counting numbers:**
- Jeg **teller** fra én til ti. (I count from one to ten.)
- Kan du **telle** til hundre? (Can you count to one hundred?)
- Hun **teller** fort. (She counts fast.)

**Counting objects:**
- Jeg må **telle** pengene mine. (I need to count my money.)
- **Tell** hvor mange epler det er. (Count how many apples there are.)
- Vi **talte** alle bøkene. (We counted all the books.)

**Counting people:**
- **Tell** hvor mange som er her. (Count how many are here.)
- Læreren **teller** elevene. (The teacher counts the students.)

---

## Prepositions with "telle"

- **telle til** - to count to (a number)
  - Jeg **teller til** tjue. (I count to twenty.)
- **telle fra** - to count from
  - Vi **teller fra** null. (We count from zero.)
- **telle i** - to count in (a language/way)
  - Kan du **telle i** norsk? (Can you count in Norwegian?)

---

## Related Words

- **et tall** (n) - a number
- **telling** (m/f) - counting, census
- **teller** (m) - counter (person who counts)
- **en teller** (m) - a counter (device)
- **antall** (n) - number, quantity

---

## Common Phrases

- Jeg **teller** til ti. (I count to ten.)
- **Tell** pengene! (Count the money!)
- Vi **teller** ned fra ti. (We count down from ten.)
- Hun **teller** sauer. (She counts sheep.)
- **Teller** det? (Does it count?)
- Hver stemme **teller**. (Every vote counts.)

---

## Special Meanings

**"å telle" can also mean "to matter" or "to count" (be important):**

- Det **teller** ikke. (It doesn't count / It doesn't matter.)
- Hver dag **teller**. (Every day counts.)
- Din mening **teller**. (Your opinion counts / matters.)
