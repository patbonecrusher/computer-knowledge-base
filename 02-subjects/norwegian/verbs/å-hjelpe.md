# å hjelpe (to help)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å hjelpe

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | hjelper | I help |
| du | hjelper | you help |
| han/hun/det | hjelper | he/she/it helps |
| vi | hjelper | we help |
| dere | hjelper | you help (plural) |
| de | hjelper | they help |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **hjelper** deg. (I help you.)
- **Kan jeg hjelpe** deg? (Can I help you?)
- Hun **hjelper** meg med leksene. (She helps me with the homework.)
- Vi **hjelper** hverandre. (We help each other.)
- De **hjelper** til. (They help out.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | hjalp | I helped |
| du | hjalp | you helped |
| han/hun/det | hjalp | he/she/it helped |
| vi | hjalp | we helped |

| de | hjalp | they helped |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **hjalp** henne i går. (I helped her yesterday.)
- Du **hjalp** meg mye. (You helped me a lot.)
- Vi **hjalp** til med arbeidet. (We helped with the work.)
- De **hjalp** ikke. (They didn't help.)

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + hjulpet

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har hjulpet | I have helped |
| du | har hjulpet | you have helped |
| han/hun/det | har hjulpet | he/she/it has helped |
| vi | har hjulpet | we have helped |
| dere | har hjulpet | you have helped (plural) |
| de | har hjulpet | they have helped |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har hjulpet** mange mennesker. (I have helped many people.)
- Hun **har hjulpet** meg før. (She has helped me before.)
- Vi **har hjulpet** hverandre lenge. (We have helped each other for a long time.)

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + hjelpe

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal hjelpe / vil hjelpe | I will help / I'm going to help |
| du | skal hjelpe / vil hjelpe | you will help |
| han/hun/det | skal hjelpe / vil hjelpe | he/she/it will help |
| vi | skal hjelpe / vil hjelpe | we will help |
| dere | skal hjelpe / vil hjelpe | you will help (plural) |
| de | skal hjelpe / vil hjelpe | they will help |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal hjelpe** deg i morgen. (I'm going to help you tomorrow.)
- **Vil du hjelpe** meg? (Will you help me?)
- Vi **skal hjelpe** til. (We're going to help out.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**hjelp** - help!

### Eksempler:
- **Hjelp** meg! (Help me!)
- **Hjelp** til! (Help out!)
- **Hjelp** hverandre! (Help each other!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **hjelper ikke**. (I don't help / I'm not helping.)
- Vi **hjalp ikke**. (We didn't help.)
- Hun **har ikke hjulpet**. (She has not helped.)
- Jeg **kan ikke hjelpe** deg nå. (I can't help you now.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Kan jeg hjelpe deg?** - Can I help you?
- **Trenger du hjelp?** - Do you need help?
- **Takk for hjelpen!** - Thanks for the help!
- **Jeg vil gjerne hjelpe** - I'd like to help / I'd be happy to help
- **hjelpe til** - to help out (with something)
- **hjelpe med** - to help with (something specific)

---

## Prepositions with "hjelpe"

**hjelpe** + **med** = help with (something)
- Kan du **hjelpe meg med** leksene? (Can you help me with the homework?)
- Hun **hjelper med** maten. (She helps with the food.)

**hjelpe til** = help out (general)
- Vi **hjelper til** på jobben. (We help out at work.)
- Kan du **hjelpe til**? (Can you help out?)

---

## Common Patterns

**Asking for help:**
- Kan du hjelpe meg? (Can you help me?)
- Vil du hjelpe meg? (Will you help me?)
- Kan jeg få hjelp? (Can I get help?)

**Offering help:**
- Jeg kan hjelpe deg. (I can help you.)
- Skal jeg hjelpe deg? (Shall I help you?)
- Trenger du hjelp? (Do you need help?)
