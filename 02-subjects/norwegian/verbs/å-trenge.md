# å trenge (to need)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å trenge

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | trenger | I need |
| du | trenger | you need |
| han/hun/det | trenger | he/she/it needs |
| vi | trenger | we need |
| dere | trenger | you need (plural) |
| de | trenger | they need |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **trenger** hjelp. (I need help.)
- **Trenger du** noe? (Do you need anything?)
- Hun **trenger** en pause. (She needs a break.)
- Vi **trenger** mat. (We need food.)
- De **trenger** ikke å komme. (They don't need to come.)

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + trengt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har trengt | I have needed |
| du | har trengt | you have needed |
| han/hun/det | har trengt | he/she/it has needed |
| vi | har trengt | we have needed |
| dere | har trengt | you have needed (plural) |
| de | har trengt | they have needed |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har trengt** dette lenge. (I have needed this for a long time.)
- **Har du trengt** hjelp? (Have you needed help?)
- Vi **har alltid trengt** mer tid. (We have always needed more time.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**treng** - need! (rarely used as command)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **trenger ikke** hjelp. (I don't need help.)
- Vi **trengte ikke** det. (We didn't need it.)
- Hun **har ikke trengt** hjelp. (She hasn't needed help.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Trenger du hjelp?** - Do you need help?
- **Jeg trenger...** - I need...
- **trenge ikke å** - don't need to / don't have to
  - Du **trenger ikke å** gå. (You don't need to go / You don't have to go.)
- **trenge noe** - need something
- **trenge å** - need to (+ verb)
  - Jeg **trenger å** hvile. (I need to rest.)

---

## Important: trenge vs må

**må** = must / have to (obligation)
- Jeg **må** gå nå. (I must go now / I have to go now.)

**trenger** = need (necessity, but softer)
- Jeg **trenger** å gå nå. (I need to go now.)

**må ikke** = must not (prohibition!)
- Du **må ikke** gå! (You must not go!)

**trenger ikke å** = don't need to (no obligation)
- Du **trenger ikke å** gå. (You don't need to go.)

## Common Things to Need

- **trenge hjelp** - need help
- **trenge mat** - need food
- **trenge vann** - need water
- **trenge tid** - need time
- **trenge penger** - need money
- **trenge en pause** - need a break
- **trenge å hvile** - need to rest
- **trenge å snakke** - need to talk

---

## Pattern: trenge å + verb

**trenge å** = need to (+ infinitive)

**Note**: "trenge" is NOT a modal verb, so it takes **"å"** before the infinitive!

- Jeg **trenger å** spise. (I need to eat.)
- Du **trenger å** sove. (You need to sleep.)
- Vi **trenger å** snakke. (We need to talk.)

---

## Common Questions

**Hva trenger du?** - What do you need?

**Trenger du noe?** - Do you need anything?

**Trenger jeg å komme?** - Do I need to come?
