# å betale (to pay)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å betale

**Meaning**: To pay

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | betaler | I pay |
| du | betaler | you pay |
| han/hun/det | betaler | he/she/it pays |
| vi | betaler | we pay |
| dere | betaler | you pay (plural) |
| de | betaler | they pay |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **betaler** med kort. (I pay with card.)
- **Betaler du** kontant? (Do you pay cash?)
- Hun **betaler** alltid med Vipps. (She always pays with Vipps.)
- Vi **betaler** regningen. (We pay the bill.)
- De **betaler** for middagen. (They pay for the dinner.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | betalte | I paid |
| du | betalte | you paid |
| han/hun/det | betalte | he/she/it paid |
| vi | betalte | we paid |
| dere | betalte | you paid (plural) |
| de | betalte | they paid |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **betalte** tusen kroner. (I paid 1000 crowns.)
- **Betalte du** med kort? (Did you pay with card?)
- Vi **betalte** regningen i går. (We paid the bill yesterday.)
- Hun **betalte** for kaffen. (She paid for the coffee.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + betalt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har betalt | I have paid |
| du | har betalt | you have paid |
| han/hun/det | har betalt | he/she/it has paid |
| vi | har betalt | we have paid |
| dere | har betalt | you have paid (plural) |
| de | har betalt | they have paid |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har betalt** regningen. (I have paid the bill.)
- **Har du betalt** for billetten? (Have you paid for the ticket?)
- Vi **har allerede betalt**. (We have already paid.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + betale

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal betale / vil betale | I will pay / I'm going to pay |
| du | skal betale / vil betale | you will pay |
| han/hun/det | skal betale / vil betale | he/she/it will pay |
| vi | skal betale / vil betale | we will pay |
| dere | skal betale / vil betale | you will pay (plural) |
| de | skal betale / vil betale | they will pay |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal betale** i morgen. (I'm going to pay tomorrow.)
- **Vil du betale** nå? (Will you pay now?)
- Vi **skal betale** med kort. (We're going to pay with card.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**betal** - pay!

### Eksempler:
- **Betal** regningen! (Pay the bill!)
- **Betal** med kort! (Pay with card!)
- **Betal** ikke for mye. (Don't pay too much.)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **betaler ikke** kontant. (I don't pay cash.)
- Vi **betalte ikke** for mye. (We didn't pay too much.)
- Hun **har ikke betalt** ennå. (She hasn't paid yet.)

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **betale med kort** - to pay with card
  - Jeg **betaler med kort**. (I pay with card.)
- **betale kontant** - to pay cash
  - **Betaler du kontant**? (Do you pay cash?)
- **betale regningen** - to pay the bill
  - Kan jeg **betale regningen**? (Can I pay the bill?)
- **betale for** - to pay for
  - Jeg **betaler for** kaffen. (I pay for the coffee.)
- **betale tilbake** - to pay back
  - Jeg **betaler deg tilbake** i morgen. (I'll pay you back tomorrow.)
- **betale med Vipps** - to pay with Vipps (Norwegian mobile payment app)
  - Kan jeg **betale med Vipps**? (Can I pay with Vipps?)

## Common Uses

**Payment methods:**
- Jeg **betaler** med kort. (I pay with card.)
- Vi **betaler** kontant. (We pay cash.)
- Hun **betaler** med Vipps. (She pays with Vipps.)
- **Betaler du** med kort eller kontant? (Do you pay with card or cash?)

**What to pay:**
- Jeg må **betale** regningen. (I need to pay the bill.)
- Vi **betaler** for maten. (We pay for the food.)
- **Betal** billetten! (Pay for the ticket!)

**How much to pay:**
- Jeg **betalte** hundre kroner. (I paid 100 crowns.)
- Hvor mye **betalte du**? (How much did you pay?)
- Vi **betalte** tusen kroner. (We paid 1000 crowns.)

---

## Prepositions with "betale"

- **betale med** - to pay with (method)
  - Jeg **betaler med** kort. (I pay with card.)
- **betale for** - to pay for (something)
  - Jeg **betaler for** kaffen. (I pay for the coffee.)
- **betale til** - to pay to (person/organization)
  - Vi **betaler til** staten. (We pay to the state.)
- **betale tilbake** - to pay back
  - Jeg **betaler deg tilbake**. (I'll pay you back.)

---

## Common Questions and Answers

**Question:**
- **Kan jeg betale med kort?** (Can I pay with card?)
- **Betaler du kontant?** (Are you paying cash?)
- **Hvor mye betalte du?** (How much did you pay?)
- **Har du betalt?** (Have you paid?)

**Answer:**
- Ja, du kan **betale** med kort. (Yes, you can pay with card.)
- Nei, jeg **betaler** med kort. (No, I pay with card.)
- Jeg **betalte** hundre kroner. (I paid 100 crowns.)
- Ja, jeg **har betalt**. (Yes, I have paid.)

## Payment Culture in Norway

**Most common:**
- **Jeg betaler med kort.** (I pay with card.) - Most common in Norway!

**Less common:**
- **Jeg betaler kontant.** (I pay cash.) - Becoming rare

**Very popular:**
- **Jeg betaler med Vipps.** (I pay with Vipps.) - Very popular mobile payment app

**Important phrase:**
- **Kan jeg betale med kort?** (Can I pay with card?) - Almost always "yes"!

## Common Phrases

- Jeg **betaler** med kort. (I pay with card.)
- **Kan jeg betale** med kort? (Can I pay with card?)
- Jeg må **betale** regningen. (I must pay the bill.)
- **Betaler du** eller skal jeg? (Are you paying or should I?)
- Vi **betaler** sammen. (We pay together / We split the bill.)
- Jeg **betaler** for deg. (I'll pay for you / It's my treat.)
- **Betal** her! (Pay here!)
- Hvor mye skal jeg **betale**? (How much should I pay?)

## Splitting the Bill

**Common ways to split:**
- Vi **deler regningen**. (We split the bill.)
- Vi **betaler** hver for oss. (We each pay for ourselves.)
- Vi **betaler** sammen. (We pay together / split.)
- Jeg **betaler** min del. (I pay my part.)

---

## Treating Someone

- Jeg **betaler** for deg. (I'll pay for you / My treat.)
- Jeg **betaler** denne gangen. (I'll pay this time.)
- La meg **betale**! (Let me pay!)
- Det er min tur å **betale**. (It's my turn to pay.)

---

## Payment Scenarios

**At a restaurant:**
- Kan jeg **betale** regningen? (Can I pay the bill?)
- Vi vil gjerne **betale**. (We'd like to pay.)
- **Betaler** dere sammen eller hver for seg? (Are you paying together or separately?)

**At a store:**
- Hvor **betaler** jeg? (Where do I pay?)
- Jeg skal **betale** her. (I'll pay here.)
- **Kan jeg betale** med kort? (Can I pay with card?)

**Paying back:**
- Jeg **betaler deg tilbake** i morgen. (I'll pay you back tomorrow.)
- Når kan du **betale** meg tilbake? (When can you pay me back?)
