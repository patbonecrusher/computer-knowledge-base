# å vite (to know)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å vite

**Meaning**: To know facts, information, or that something is true

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | vet | I know |
| du | vet | you know |
| han/hun/det | vet | he/she/it knows |
| vi | vet | we know |
| dere | vet | you know (plural) |
| de | vet | they know |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **vet** det. (I know that / I know it.)
- **Vet du** hvor han bor? (Do you know where he lives?)
- Hun **vet** svaret. (She knows the answer.)
- Vi **vet** ikke. (We don't know.)
- De **vet** alt. (They know everything.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | visste | I knew |
| du | visste | you knew |
| han/hun/det | visste | he/she/it knew |
| vi | visste | we knew |
| dere | visste | you knew (plural) |
| de | visste | they knew |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **visste** det. (I knew it.)
- **Visste du** det? (Did you know that?)
- Vi **visste** ikke. (We didn't know.)
- Hun **visste** svaret. (She knew the answer.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + visst

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har visst | I have known |
| du | har visst | you have known |
| han/hun/det | har visst | he/she/it has known |
| vi | har visst | we have known |
| dere | har visst | you have known (plural) |
| de | har visst | they have known |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har alltid visst** det. (I have always known it.)
- **Har du visst** om dette? (Have you known about this?)
- Vi **har visst** det lenge. (We have known it for a long time.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + vite

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal vite / vil vite | I will know / I'm going to know |
| du | skal vite / vil vite | you will know |
| han/hun/det | skal vite / vil vite | he/she/it will know |
| vi | skal vite / vil vite | we will know |
| dere | skal vite / vil vite | you will know (plural) |
| de | skal vite / vil vite | they will know |

### Eksempler:
- Du **vil vite** det snart. (You will know it soon.)
- Vi **skal vite** svaret i morgen. (We're going to know the answer tomorrow.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**vit** - know! (rarely used as command)

### Eksempler:
- **Vit** at jeg hjelper deg. (Know that I'll help you.)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **vet ikke**. (I don't know.)
- Vi **visste ikke**. (We didn't know.)
- Hun **har ikke visst** om det. (She hasn't known about it.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Jeg vet** - I know
- **Jeg vet ikke** - I don't know
- **Vet du...?** - Do you know...?
- **Vet du hva?** - You know what?
- **Jeg vet det** - I know (that)
- **Vi vet** - We know
- **Så vidt jeg vet** - As far as I know
- **Gud vet** - God knows
- **Hvem vet?** - Who knows?

---

## Common Uses

**Knowing facts:**
- **Vet du** svaret? (Do you know the answer?)
- **Vet du** hvor han bor? (Do you know where he lives?)
- **Vet du** hva klokka er? (Do you know what time it is?)

**Not knowing:**
- **Jeg vet ikke**. (I don't know.)
- **Vi vet ikke** ennå. (We don't know yet.)
- **Hvem vet?** (Who knows?)

**Being aware:**
- **Vet du om** dette? (Do you know about this?)
- **Jeg vet** at han kommer. (I know that he's coming.)

---

## Important: vite vs kjenne

**å vite** = to know (facts, information, that something is true)
- Jeg **vet** hvor hun bor. (I know where she lives.) - fact
- **Vet du** svaret? (Do you know the answer?) - information

**å kjenne** = to know (people, places, be familiar with)
- Jeg **kjenner** henne. (I know her.) - person
- Jeg **kjenner** Oslo godt. (I know Oslo well.) - place/familiarity

---

## Special Note: Conjugation

**å vite** is highly irregular:
- Infinitive: vite
- Present: **vet** (not "viter"!)
- Past: **visste** (irregular!)
- Past participle: **visst** (irregular!)

Pattern: vite → vet → visste → visst

---

## Common Questions with "vet"

**Vet du...?** - Do you know...?
- **Vet du** hva klokka er? (Do you know what time it is?)
- **Vet du** hvor han er? (Do you know where he is?)

**Visste du...?** - Did you know...?
- **Visste du** det? (Did you know that?)
- **Visste du** at hun kom? (Did you know that she came?)

---

## Common Responses

**Ja, jeg vet** - Yes, I know

**Nei, jeg vet ikke** - No, I don't know

**Jeg visste ikke det** - I didn't know that

**Så vidt jeg vet** - As far as I know

**Jeg vet ikke ennå** - I don't know yet
