# å pakke (to pack)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å pakke

**Meaning**: To pack (luggage, boxes, etc.)

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | pakker | I pack |
| du | pakker | you pack |
| han/hun/det | pakker | he/she/it packs |
| vi | pakker | we pack |
| dere | pakker | you pack (plural) |
| de | pakker | they pack |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **pakker** kofferten min. (I pack my suitcase.)
- **Pakker du** allerede? (Are you packing already?)
- Hun **pakker** sekken. (She packs the backpack.)
- Vi **pakker** i dag. (We're packing today.)
- De **pakker** før ferien. (They're packing before vacation.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**pakk** - pack!

### Eksempler:
- **Pakk** kofferten! (Pack the suitcase!)
- **Pakk** varme klær! (Pack warm clothes!)
- **Pakk** ferdig! (Finish packing!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **pakker ikke** i dag. (I'm not packing today.)
- Vi **pakket ikke** ferdig. (We didn't finish packing.)
- Hun **har ikke pakket** ennå. (She hasn't packed yet.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **pakke kofferten** - to pack the suitcase
  - Jeg må **pakke kofferten**. (I must pack the suitcase.)
- **pakke sekken** - to pack the backpack
  - Hun **pakker** sekken sin. (She's packing her backpack.)
- **pakke ferdig** - to finish packing
  - Er du **pakket ferdig**? (Have you finished packing?)
- **pakke ut** - to unpack
  - Jeg må **pakke ut** kofferten. (I must unpack the suitcase.)
- **pakke ned** - to pack down / pack away
  - Vi **pakker ned** teltene. (We're packing down the tents.)
- **pakke inn** - to wrap (gifts, packages)
  - Jeg **pakker inn** gaven. (I'm wrapping the gift.)

---

## Common Uses

**Packing for travel:**
- Jeg **pakker** kofferten min. (I'm packing my suitcase.)
- Vi **pakker** før ferien. (We're packing before vacation.)
- **Pakker du** mye? (Do you pack a lot?)
- Hun **pakker** varme klær. (She's packing warm clothes.)

**What to pack:**
- Jeg **pakker** jakke og genser. (I'm packing a jacket and sweater.)
- Du må **pakke** varme klær. (You must pack warm clothes.)
- Vi **pakker** alt vi trenger. (We're packing everything we need.)

**When to pack:**
- Jeg **pakker** i kveld. (I'm packing tonight.)
- Vi må **pakke** før vi reiser. (We must pack before we travel.)
- **Pakk** i morgen! (Pack tomorrow!)

## pakke vs pakke ut vs pakke inn

**å pakke** = to pack (luggage)
- Jeg **pakker** kofferten. (I'm packing the suitcase.)

**å pakke ut** = to unpack
- Jeg **pakker ut** kofferten. (I'm unpacking the suitcase.)

**å pakke inn** = to wrap (gifts)
- Jeg **pakker inn** gaven. (I'm wrapping the gift.)

## Special Expressions

**Pakke ferdig** - Finish packing
- Jeg må **pakke ferdig** i kveld. (I must finish packing tonight.)

**Pakke ut** - Unpack
- Vi **pakker ut** når vi kommer hjem. (We unpack when we get home.)

**Pakke inn** - Wrap up
- Jeg **pakker inn** julegaver. (I'm wrapping Christmas gifts.)

**Pakke ned** - Pack down / Pack away
- Vi **pakker ned** teltet. (We're packing down the tent.)

**Pakke lett** - Pack light
- Jeg prøver å **pakke lett**. (I try to pack light.)
