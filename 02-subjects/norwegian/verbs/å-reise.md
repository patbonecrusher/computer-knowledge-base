# å reise (to travel)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å reise

**Meaning**: To travel or to journey

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | reiser | I travel |
| du | reiser | you travel |
| han/hun/det | reiser | he/she/it travels |
| vi | reiser | we travel |
| dere | reiser | you travel (plural) |
| de | reiser | they travel |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **reiser** til Norge. (I travel to Norway.)
- **Reiser du** mye? (Do you travel a lot?)
- Hun **reiser** til Oslo i morgen. (She's traveling to Oslo tomorrow.)
- Vi **reiser** om sommeren. (We travel in summer.)
- De **reiser** ofte. (They travel often.)

---

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**reis** - travel!

### Eksempler:
- **Reis** trygt! (Travel safely!)
- **Reis** verden rundt! (Travel around the world!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **reiser ikke** mye. (I don't travel much.)
- Vi **reiste ikke** i fjor. (We didn't travel last year.)
- Hun **har ikke reist** til Norge. (She hasn't traveled to Norway.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **reise til** - to travel to (destination)
  - Jeg **reiser til** Norge. (I'm traveling to Norway.)
- **reise fra** - to travel from (origin)
  - Vi **reiser fra** Oslo. (We're traveling from Oslo.)
- **reise med** - to travel with/by (person or transport)
  - Jeg **reiser med** tog. (I'm traveling by train.)
- **reise alene** - to travel alone
  - Hun **reiser** alltid **alene**. (She always travels alone.)
- **reise sammen** - to travel together
  - Vi **reiser sammen**. (We're traveling together.)

---

## Common Uses

**Traveling to places:**
- Jeg **reiser** til Norge. (I'm traveling to Norway.)
- Vi **reiser** til Oslo. (We're traveling to Oslo.)
- De **reiser** til Europa. (They're traveling to Europe.)

**When traveling:**
- Vi **reiser** om sommeren. (We travel in summer.)
- Jeg **reiser** i morgen. (I'm traveling tomorrow.)
- Hun **reiser** neste uke. (She's traveling next week.)

**How to travel:**
- Jeg **reiser** med fly. (I travel by plane.)
- Vi **reiser** med tog. (We travel by train.)
- De **reiser** med bil. (They travel by car.)

---

## Prepositions with "reise"

- **reise til** - travel to
  - Jeg **reiser til** Bergen. (I'm traveling to Bergen.)
- **reise fra** - travel from
  - Vi **reiser fra** Oslo. (We travel from Oslo.)
- **reise med** - travel with/by
  - Hun **reiser med** buss. (She travels by bus.)
- **reise gjennom** - travel through
  - Vi **reiser gjennom** Europa. (We travel through Europe.)
- **reise rundt** - travel around
  - De **reiser rundt** i Norge. (They travel around Norway.)

## Common Questions

**Reiser du mye?** - Do you travel a lot?

**Hvor skal du reise?** - Where are you going to travel?

**Når reiser du?** - When are you traveling?

**Hvordan reiser du?** - How are you traveling?

**Reiser du alene?** - Are you traveling alone?

---

## Special Expressions

**Reise verden rundt** - Travel around the world
- Jeg vil **reise verden rundt**. (I want to travel around the world.)

**Reise på ferie** - Go on vacation
- Vi **reiser på ferie** til Spania. (We're going on vacation to Spain.)

**Reise på forretningsreise** - Go on a business trip
- Han **reiser på forretningsreise**. (He's going on a business trip.)

**God reise!** - Have a good trip!
- **God reise!** Vi sees snart! (Have a good trip! See you soon!)
