# å gjøre (to do / to make)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å gjøre

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | gjør | I do / I make |
| du | gjør | you do / you make |
| han/hun/det | gjør | he/she/it does / makes |
| vi | gjør | we do / we make |
| dere | gjør | you do / you make (plural) |
| de | gjør | they do / they make |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- **Hva gjør du?** (What are you doing? / What do you do?)
- Jeg **gjør** lekser. (I do homework.)
- Hun **gjør** ingenting. (She does nothing.)
- Vi **gjør** det i dag. (We do it today.)
- **Hva gjør du i kveld?** (What are you doing tonight?)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | gjorde | I did / I made |
| du | gjorde | you did / you made |
| han/hun/det | gjorde | he/she/it did / made |
| vi | gjorde | we did / we made |
| dere | gjorde | you did / you made (plural) |
| de | gjorde | they did / they made |

### Eksempler:
- **Hva gjorde du i går?** (What did you do yesterday?)
- Jeg **gjorde** leksene mine. (I did my homework.)
- Vi **gjorde** det. (We did it.)
- De **gjorde** ingenting. (They did nothing.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + gjort

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har gjort | I have done / I have made |
| du | har gjort | you have done / you have made |
| han/hun/det | har gjort | he/she/it has done / has made |
| vi | har gjort | we have done / we have made |
| dere | har gjort | you have done / you have made (plural) |
| de | har gjort | they have done / they have made |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har gjort** leksene. (I have done the homework.)
- **Hva har du gjort?** (What have you done?)
- Vi **har gjort** en feil. (We have made a mistake.)
- Hun **har gjort** det før. (She has done it before.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + gjøre

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal gjøre / vil gjøre | I will do / I'm going to do |
| du | skal gjøre / vil gjøre | you will do |
| han/hun/det | skal gjøre / vil gjøre | he/she/it will do |
| vi | skal gjøre / vil gjøre | we will do |
| dere | skal gjøre / vil gjøre | you will do (plural) |
| de | skal gjøre / vil gjøre | they will do |

### Eksempler:
- **Hva skal du gjøre i morgen?** (What are you going to do tomorrow?)
- Jeg **skal gjøre** lekser. (I'm going to do homework.)
- Vi **vil gjøre** det senere. (We will do it later.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**gjør** - do! / make!

### Eksempler:
- **Gjør** det nå! (Do it now!)
- **Gjør** leksene dine! (Do your homework!)
- **Gjør** det bra! (Do it well!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **gjør ikke** det. (I don't do that.)
- Vi **gjorde ikke** lekser. (We didn't do homework.)
- Hun **har ikke gjort** det. (She has not done it.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Hva gjør du?** - What do you do? / What are you doing?
- **Hva gjør du i dag?** - What are you doing today?
- **Hva skal vi gjøre?** - What should we do?
- **Det gjør ingenting** - It doesn't matter / That's okay (literally: It does nothing)
- **Det gjør vondt** - It hurts (literally: It does pain)
- **Det gjør meg glad** - That makes me happy
- **Gjør det selv** - Do it yourself

---

## Special Note: Common Phrases

**Hva gjør du?** can mean:
1. What are you doing? (right now)
2. What do you do? (for work/profession)

Context determines the meaning!
