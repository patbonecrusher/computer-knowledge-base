# å mene (to mean / to think)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å mene

**Meaning**: This verb has two main meanings:
1. to mean (what something means)
2. to think / to have an opinion

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | mener | I mean / I think |
| du | mener | you mean / you think |
| han/hun/det | mener | he/she/it means / thinks |
| vi | mener | we mean / we think |
| dere | mener | you mean / you think (plural) |
| de | mener | they mean / they think |

### Eksempler (Examples):

**Meaning (what something means):**
- **Hva mener du?** (What do you mean?)
- Jeg **mener** ikke det. (I don't mean that.)
- Hva **mener** dette ordet? (What does this word mean?)

**Opinion (to think):**
- Jeg **mener** at du har rett. (I think that you're right.)
- Hva **mener du** om dette? (What do you think about this?)
- Vi **mener** at det er viktig. (We think that it's important.)

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**men** - mean! (rarely used)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **mener ikke** det. (I don't mean that / I don't think so.)
- Vi **mente ikke** å såre deg. (We didn't mean to hurt you.)
- Hun **har ikke ment** det sånn. (She hasn't meant it like that.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

**Asking for meaning:**
- **Hva mener du?** - What do you mean?
- **Hva mener dette?** - What does this mean?
- **Jeg forstår ikke hva du mener** - I don't understand what you mean

**Asking for opinion:**
- **Hva mener du om...?** - What do you think about...?
- **Jeg mener at...** - I think that...
- **Hva mener du vi bør gjøre?** - What do you think we should do?

**Clarifying:**
- **Jeg mener ikke det** - I don't mean that
- **Jeg mener** - I mean (clarification)
- **Det vil si** - That is to say / I mean

---

## Two Main Uses

### 1. What something means

**Hva mener...?** = What does ... mean?
- **Hva mener** dette ordet? (What does this word mean?)
- **Hva mener** det? (What does that mean?)

### 2. Opinion/Thinking

**mene at** = think that / believe that
- Jeg **mener at** det er viktig. (I think that it's important.)
- Hva **mener du at** vi bør gjøre? (What do you think we should do?)

**mene om** = think about (opinion about something)
- Hva **mener du om** filmen? (What do you think about the movie?)

---

## Related Verbs

**å mene** = to mean / to think (opinion)
- **Hva mener du?** (What do you mean? / What do you think?)

**å tro** = to think / to believe (assumption/belief)
- Jeg **tror** det er sant. (I think it's true.)

**å tenke** = to think (mental process)
- Jeg **tenker** på deg. (I'm thinking of you.)

**å synes** = to think / to find (subjective opinion)
- Jeg **synes** det er fint. (I think it's nice / I find it nice.)

---

## Common Phrases

**Jeg mener det** - I mean it
- Jeg **mener det** virkelig. (I really mean it.)

**Hva mener du med det?** - What do you mean by that?

**Som jeg mener** - As I think / In my opinion

**Det er hva jeg mener** - That's what I mean
