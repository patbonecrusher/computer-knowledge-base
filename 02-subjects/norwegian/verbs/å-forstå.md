# å forstå (to understand)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å forstå

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | forstår | I understand |
| du | forstår | you understand |
| han/hun/det | forstår | he/she/it understands |
| vi | forstår | we understand |
| dere | forstår | you understand (plural) |
| de | forstår | they understand |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **forstår** norsk. (I understand Norwegian.)
- **Forstår du** meg? (Do you understand me?)
- Hun **forstår** ikke. (She doesn't understand.)
- Vi **forstår** alt. (We understand everything.)
- De **forstår** godt. (They understand well.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | forsto / forstod | I understood |
| du | forsto / forstod | you understood |
| han/hun/det | forsto / forstod | he/she/it understood |
| vi | forsto / forstod | we understood |
| dere | forsto / forstod | you understood (plural) |
| de | forsto / forstod | they understood |

**Note**: Both "forsto" and "forstod" are correct. "Forsto" is more common.

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **forsto** deg. (I understood you.)
- **Forsto du** det? (Did you understand it?)
- Vi **forsto** ikke. (We didn't understand.)
- Hun **forsto** alt. (She understood everything.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + forstått

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har forstått | I have understood |
| du | har forstått | you have understood |
| han/hun/det | har forstått | he/she/it has understood |
| vi | har forstått | we have understood |
| dere | har forstått | you have understood (plural) |
| de | har forstått | they have understood |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har forstått** det nå. (I have understood it now / I understand it now.)
- **Har du forstått**? (Have you understood? / Do you understand?)
- Vi **har alltid forstått** deg. (We have always understood you.)
- Hun **har ikke forstått** ennå. (She hasn't understood yet.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + forstå

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal forstå / vil forstå | I will understand / I'm going to understand |
| du | skal forstå / vil forstå | you will understand |
| han/hun/det | skal forstå / vil forstå | he/she/it will understand |
| vi | skal forstå / vil forstå | we will understand |
| dere | skal forstå / vil forstå | you will understand (plural) |
| de | skal forstå / vil forstå | they will understand |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal forstå** det snart. (I'm going to understand it soon.)
- Du **vil forstå** senere. (You will understand later.)
- Vi **kommer til å forstå**. (We're going to understand.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**forstå** - understand! (rarely used as command)

### Eksempler:
- **Forstå** dette! (Understand this!)
- Du må **forstå**! (You must understand!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **forstår ikke**. (I don't understand.)
- Vi **forsto ikke**. (We didn't understand.)
- Hun **har ikke forstått**. (She hasn't understood.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Forstår du?** - Do you understand?
- **Jeg forstår** - I understand
- **Jeg forstår ikke** - I don't understand
- **Jeg forstår deg** - I understand you
- **Jeg forstår hva du mener** - I understand what you mean
- **Jeg begynner å forstå** - I'm beginning to understand
- **Det er lett å forstå** - It's easy to understand
- **Det er vanskelig å forstå** - It's difficult to understand
- **Kan du forstå norsk?** - Can you understand Norwegian?

## Common Questions

**Forstår du?** - Do you understand?

**Forstår du meg?** - Do you understand me?

**Forstår du norsk?** - Do you understand Norwegian?

**Kan du forstå hva jeg sier?** - Can you understand what I'm saying?

**Har du forstått?** - Have you understood? / Do you understand?

## Checking Understanding

**Teacher/Speaker:**
- **Forstår dere?** (Do you [plural] understand?)
- **Har dere forstått?** (Have you understood?)

**Learner:**
- **Jeg forstår ikke.** (I don't understand.)
- **Kan du si det en gang til?** (Can you say that one more time?)
- **Jeg begynner å forstå.** (I'm beginning to understand.)

---

## Degrees of Understanding

**Jeg forstår godt** - I understand well

**Jeg forstår litt** - I understand a little

**Jeg forstår ikke** - I don't understand

**Jeg forstår ikke noe** - I don't understand anything

**Jeg forstår alt** - I understand everything

**Jeg forstår nesten alt** - I understand almost everything

---

## Special Note: Conjugation

**å forstå** is irregular:
- Present: forstår (note the **å** at the end!)
- Past: **forsto/forstod** (irregular!)
- Past participle: **forstått** (irregular!)

**Important**: The present tense ends in **-år** (not -er like most verbs):
- forstår (NOT forster)

---

## Related Expressions

**Jeg skjønner** = I understand (synonym, more informal)
- **Jeg skjønner** means the same as **Jeg forstår**
- Both are common!

**å begripe** = to comprehend (more formal)

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Jeg forster ikke. (Wrong!)
✅ Jeg forstår ikke. (Correct!)

❌ Jeg forstår deg ikke. (Word order!)
✅ Jeg forstår ikke deg. / Jeg forstår deg ikke. (Both correct, but "ikke" usually comes after the verb)

Most natural: **Jeg forstår deg ikke.**

---

## Useful Phrases for Language Learning

**Jeg vil lære å forstå norsk** - I want to learn to understand Norwegian

**Jeg begynner å forstå** - I'm beginning to understand

**Kan du snakke saktere? Jeg forstår ikke.** - Can you speak slower? I don't understand.

**Nå forstår jeg!** - Now I understand!

**Jeg forstår mer og mer** - I understand more and more
