# å finne (to find)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å finne

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | finner | I find |
| du | finner | you find |
| han/hun/det | finner | he/she/it finds |
| vi | finner | we find |
| dere | finner | you find (plural) |
| de | finner | they find |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **finner** bilen min. (I find my car.)
- **Finner du** det? (Do you find it? / Can you find it?)
- Hun **finner** ikke veien. (She can't find the way.)
- Vi **finner** huset. (We find the house.)
- De **finner** alltid noe. (They always find something.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | fant | I found |
| du | fant | you found |
| han/hun/det | fant | he/she/it found |
| vi | fant | we found |
| dere | fant | you found (plural) |
| de | fant | they found |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **fant** bilen min. (I found my car.)
- **Fant du** nøklene? (Did you find the keys?)
- Vi **fant** veien. (We found the way.)
- Han **fant** ikke huset. (He didn't find the house.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + funnet

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har funnet | I have found |
| du | har funnet | you have found |
| han/hun/det | har funnet | he/she/it has found |
| vi | har funnet | we have found |
| dere | har funnet | you have found (plural) |
| de | har funnet | they have found |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har funnet** bilen min. (I have found my car.)
- **Har du funnet** det? (Have you found it?)
- Vi **har funnet** en løsning. (We have found a solution.)
- Hun **har ikke funnet** veien. (She hasn't found the way.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + finne

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal finne / vil finne | I will find / I'm going to find |
| du | skal finne / vil finne | you will find |
| han/hun/det | skal finne / vil finne | he/she/it will find |
| vi | skal finne / vil finne | we will find |
| dere | skal finne / vil finne | you will find (plural) |
| de | skal finne / vil finne | they will find |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal finne** det. (I'm going to find it.)
- **Vil du finne** veien? (Will you find the way?)
- Vi **må finne** en løsning. (We must find a solution.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**finn** - find!

### Eksempler:
- **Finn** bilen! (Find the car!)
- **Finn** veien! (Find the way!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **finner ikke** det. (I can't find it / I don't find it.)
- Vi **fant ikke** huset. (We didn't find the house.)
- Hun **har ikke funnet** nøklene. (She hasn't found the keys.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Kan du finne det?** - Can you find it?
- **Jeg finner det ikke** - I can't find it
- **finne veien** - to find the way
- **finne ut** - to find out / to figure out
  - Jeg må **finne ut** av dette. (I need to find out about this / figure this out.)
- **finne på** - to come up with / to make up
  - Hva skal vi **finne på** i kveld? (What shall we do tonight?)

## Special Expressions

**finne ut** = find out / figure out
- Jeg må **finne ut** hva som skjer. (I need to find out what's happening.)

**finne på** = come up with / invent / make up
- Hva skal vi **finne på**? (What should we do? / What shall we come up with?)

**finne seg i** = put up with / accept
- Jeg kan ikke **finne meg i** dette. (I can't put up with this.)

---

## Special Note: Conjugation

**å finne** is irregular:
- Present: finner
- Past: **fant** (irregular!)
- Past participle: **funnet** (irregular!)

Pattern: finne → fant → funnet
