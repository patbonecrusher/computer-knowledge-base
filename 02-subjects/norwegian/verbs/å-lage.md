# å lage (to make / to cook)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å lage

**Meaning**: To make or to cook food/things

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | lager | I make |
| du | lager | you make |
| han/hun/det | lager | he/she/it makes |
| vi | lager | we make |
| dere | lager | you make (plural) |
| de | lager | they make |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **lager** middag. (I'm making dinner.)
- **Lager du** mat i dag? (Are you making food today?)
- Hun **lager** pizza til oss. (She's making pizza for us.)
- Vi **lager** ofte pasta. (We often make pasta.)
- De **lager** ikke mat hjemme. (They don't make food at home.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | laget | I made |
| du | laget | you made |
| han/hun/det | laget | he/she/it made |
| vi | laget | we made |
| dere | laget | you made (plural) |
| de | laget | they made |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **laget** middag i går. (I made dinner yesterday.)
- **Laget du** maten selv? (Did you make the food yourself?)
- Vi **laget** pizza sammen. (We made pizza together.)
- Hun **laget** en deilig suppe. (She made a delicious soup.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + laget

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har laget | I have made |
| du | har laget | you have made |
| han/hun/det | har laget | he/she/it has made |
| vi | har laget | we have made |
| dere | har laget | you have made (plural) |
| de | har laget | they have made |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har laget** mat hele dagen. (I have made food all day.)
- **Har du laget** middag? (Have you made dinner?)
- Vi **har alltid laget** vår egen mat. (We have always made our own food.)

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + lage

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal lage / vil lage | I will make / I'm going to make |
| du | skal lage / vil lage | you will make |
| han/hun/det | skal lage / vil lage | he/she/it will make |
| vi | skal lage / vil lage | we will make |
| dere | skal lage / vil lage | you will make (plural) |
| de | skal lage / vil lage | they will make |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal lage** middag i kveld. (I'm going to make dinner tonight.)
- **Vil du lage** mat sammen med meg? (Will you make food with me?)
- Vi **skal lage** pizza på fredag. (We're going to make pizza on Friday.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**lag** - make!

### Eksempler:
- **Lag** mat til oss! (Make food for us!)
- **Lag** en god middag. (Make a good dinner.)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **lager ikke** mat i dag. (I'm not making food today.)
- Vi **laget ikke** middag i går. (We didn't make dinner yesterday.)
- Hun **har ikke laget** noe ennå. (She hasn't made anything yet.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **lage mat** - to cook / to make food
  - Jeg **lager mat** hver dag. (I cook every day.)
- **lage middag** - to make dinner
  - Skal vi **lage middag** sammen? (Should we make dinner together?)
- **lage fra bunnen av** - to make from scratch
  - Hun **lager** alltid mat **fra bunnen av**. (She always makes food from scratch.)
- **lage til** - to make for
  - Jeg **lager mat til** familien. (I make food for the family.)

---

## Common Uses

**Making food:**
- **Lager du** mat i dag? (Are you making food today?)
- Jeg **lager** ofte pasta til middag. (I often make pasta for dinner.)
- Vi **lager** pizza hjemme. (We make pizza at home.)

**What to make:**
- Hva skal vi **lage** til middag? (What should we make for dinner?)
- Jeg vil **lage** noe enkelt. (I want to make something simple.)

**Making from scratch:**
- Hun **lager** brød selv. (She makes bread herself.)
- Vi **lager** alt fra bunnen av. (We make everything from scratch.)

---

## Special Note

**å lage** vs **å gjøre**:

**å lage** = to make (physical creation, especially food)
- Jeg **lager** mat. (I make food / I cook.)
- Hun **lager** en kake. (She makes a cake.)

**å gjøre** = to do (actions, activities)
- Hva **gjør** du? (What are you doing?)
- Jeg **gjør** lekser. (I do homework.)
