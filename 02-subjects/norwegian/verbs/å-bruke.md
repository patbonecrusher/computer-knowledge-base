# å bruke (to use / to wear)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å bruke

**Meaning**: To use or to wear (clothes)

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | bruker | I use / I wear |
| du | bruker | you use / you wear |
| han/hun/det | bruker | he/she/it uses / wears |
| vi | bruker | we use / we wear |
| dere | bruker | you use / you wear (plural) |
| de | bruker | they use / they wear |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **bruker** telefonen min. (I use my phone.)
- **Bruker du** jakke? (Do you wear a jacket?)
- Hun **bruker** genser. (She wears a sweater.)
- Vi **bruker** bussen. (We use the bus.)
- De **bruker** skjerf om vinteren. (They wear scarves in winter.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | brukte | I used / I wore |
| du | brukte | you used / you wore |
| han/hun/det | brukte | he/she/it used / wore |
| vi | brukte | we used / we wore |
| dere | brukte | you used / you wore (plural) |
| de | brukte | they used / they wore |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **brukte** bilen i går. (I used the car yesterday.)
- **Brukte du** jakke? (Did you wear a jacket?)
- Vi **brukte** tid på det. (We used time on that.)
- Hun **brukte** lue. (She wore a hat.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + brukt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har brukt | I have used / I have worn |
| du | har brukt | you have used / you have worn |
| han/hun/det | har brukt | he/she/it has used / has worn |
| vi | har brukt | we have used / we have worn |
| dere | har brukt | you have used / you have worn (plural) |
| de | har brukt | they have used / they have worn |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har brukt** denne jakken før. (I have worn this jacket before.)
- **Har du brukt** dette? (Have you used this?)
- Vi **har brukt** mye tid. (We have used a lot of time.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + bruke

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal bruke / vil bruke | I will use / I will wear |
| du | skal bruke / vil bruke | you will use / you will wear |
| han/hun/det | skal bruke / vil bruke | he/she/it will use / will wear |
| vi | skal bruke / vil bruke | we will use / we will wear |
| dere | skal bruke / vil bruke | you will use / you will wear (plural) |
| de | skal bruke / vil bruke | they will use / they will wear |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal bruke** jakke i dag. (I'm going to wear a jacket today.)
- **Vil du bruke** dette? (Will you use this?)
- Vi **skal bruke** bilen. (We're going to use the car.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**bruk** - use! / wear!

### Eksempler:
- **Bruk** jakke! (Wear a jacket!)
- **Bruk** skjerf når det er kaldt! (Wear a scarf when it's cold!)
- **Bruk** dette! (Use this!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **bruker ikke** telefonen min. (I don't use my phone.)
- Vi **brukte ikke** bil. (We didn't use the car.)
- Hun **har ikke brukt** det. (She hasn't used it.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **bruke tid** - to use/spend time
  - Jeg **bruker** mye tid på det. (I spend a lot of time on it.)
- **bruke penger** - to spend money
  - Hun **bruker** penger på klær. (She spends money on clothes.)
- **bruke + clothing** - to wear
  - Jeg **bruker** jakke. (I wear a jacket.)
- **bruke til** - to use for
  - Hva **bruker** du dette til? (What do you use this for?)

---

## Two Main Uses

### 1. Using something (tool, item, vehicle)

- Jeg **bruker** telefonen min. (I use my phone.)
- Vi **bruker** bussen. (We use the bus.)
- **Bruker du** datamaskinen? (Do you use the computer?)
- Hun **bruker** bilen. (She uses the car.)

### 2. Wearing clothes

Very common for clothing!

- Jeg **bruker** jakke. (I wear a jacket.)
- **Bruker du** skjerf? (Do you wear a scarf?)
- Hun **bruker** genser om vinteren. (She wears a sweater in winter.)
- Vi **bruker** lue når det er kaldt. (We wear hats when it's cold.)

---

## Common Clothing with "bruke"

- **bruke jakke** - wear a jacket
- **bruke genser** - wear a sweater
- **bruke skjerf** - wear a scarf
- **bruke lue** - wear a hat
- **bruke hansker** - wear gloves
- **bruke sko** - wear shoes
- **bruke klær** - wear clothes

---

## Common Uses

**Wearing clothes:**
- Jeg **bruker** varme klær om vinteren. (I wear warm clothes in winter.)
- Du må **bruke** jakke i dag. (You must wear a jacket today.)
- Hun **bruker** alltid lue. (She always wears a hat.)

**Using items:**
- Jeg **bruker** telefonen min hver dag. (I use my phone every day.)
- Vi **bruker** bussen til jobb. (We use the bus to work.)
- **Bruker du** dette? (Do you use this?)

**Spending time/money:**
- Jeg **bruker** mye tid på dette. (I spend a lot of time on this.)
- Hun **bruker** penger på mat. (She spends money on food.)
- Vi **bruker** tid sammen. (We spend time together.)

---

## Common Questions

**Hva bruker du?** - What are you using? / What are you wearing?

**Bruker du jakke?** - Are you wearing a jacket?

**Bruker du dette?** - Do you use this?

**Hva bruker du dette til?** - What do you use this for?

**Hvor mye bruker du?** - How much do you spend/use?

---

## Special Expressions

**Bruke tid på** - Spend time on
- Jeg **bruker** mye tid **på** norsk. (I spend a lot of time on Norwegian.)

**Bruke penger på** - Spend money on
- Hun **bruker** penger **på** klær. (She spends money on clothes.)

**Bruke til** - Use for
- Hva **bruker** du dette **til**? (What do you use this for?)

**Bruke opp** - Use up
- Vi **brukte opp** maten. (We used up the food.)

---

## Time Expressions with "bruke"

- **bruke alltid** - always use/wear
- **bruke ofte** - often use/wear
- **bruke sjelden** - rarely use/wear
- **bruke aldri** - never use/wear
- **bruke hver dag** - use/wear every day

---

## Important Note

**å bruke** is VERY common in Norwegian for wearing clothes!

In English, we say "I wear a jacket"
In Norwegian, we say "Jeg **bruker** jakke" (literally "I use a jacket")

This is one of the most important differences to remember!
