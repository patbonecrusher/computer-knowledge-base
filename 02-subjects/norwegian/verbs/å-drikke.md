# å drikke (to drink)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å drikke

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | drikker | I drink |
| du | drikker | you drink |
| han/hun/det | drikker | he/she/it drinks |
| vi | drikker | we drink |
| dere | drikker | you drink (plural) |
| de | drikker | they drink |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **drikker** kaffe. (I drink coffee / I'm drinking coffee.)
- **Hva drikker du?** (What are you drinking? / What do you drink?)
- Hun **drikker** te. (She drinks tea.)
- Vi **drikker** vann. (We drink water.)
- De **drikker** ofte melk. (They often drink milk.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | drakk | I drank |
| du | drakk | you drank |
| han/hun/det | drakk | he/she/it drank |
| vi | drakk | we drank |
| dere | drakk | you drank (plural) |
| de | drakk | they drank |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **drakk** kaffe i går. (I drank coffee yesterday.)
- **Hva drakk du?** (What did you drink?)
- Vi **drakk** te sammen. (We drank tea together.)
- Hun **drakk** ikke vann. (She didn't drink water.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + drukket

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har drukket | I have drunk |
| du | har drukket | you have drunk |
| han/hun/det | har drukket | he/she/it has drunk |
| vi | har drukket | we have drunk |
| dere | har drukket | you have drunk (plural) |
| de | har drukket | they have drunk |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har drukket** for mye kaffe. (I have drunk too much coffee.)
- **Har du drukket** nok vann? (Have you drunk enough water?)
- Vi **har drukket** all teen. (We have drunk all the tea.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + drikke

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal drikke / vil drikke | I will drink / I'm going to drink |
| du | skal drikke / vil drikke | you will drink |
| han/hun/det | skal drikke / vil drikke | he/she/it will drink |
| vi | skal drikke / vil drikke | we will drink |
| dere | skal drikke / vil drikke | you will drink (plural) |
| de | skal drikke / vil drikke | they will drink |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal drikke** kaffe. (I'm going to drink coffee.)
- **Vil du drikke** noe? (Do you want to drink something?)
- Vi **vil drikke** te. (We want to drink tea.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**drikk** - drink!

### Eksempler:
- **Drikk** vann! (Drink water!)
- **Drikk** opp! (Drink up!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **drikker ikke** kaffe. (I don't drink coffee.)
- Vi **drakk ikke** te. (We didn't drink tea.)
- Hun **har ikke drukket** nok. (She hasn't drunk enough.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Vil du ha noe å drikke?** - Do you want something to drink?
- **Hva vil du drikke?** - What do you want to drink?
- **drikke kaffe** - to drink coffee
- **drikke te** - to drink tea
- **drikke vann** - to drink water
- **drikke melk** - to drink milk
- **drikke øl** - to drink beer
- **drikke vin** - to drink wine
- **drikke opp** - to drink up / finish drinking
- **Skål!** - Cheers! (when drinking/toasting)

---

## Common Beverages

**kaffe** = coffee
- Jeg **drikker kaffe** hver morgen. (I drink coffee every morning.)

**te** = tea
- Hun **drikker te**. (She drinks tea.)

**vann** = water
- Du må **drikke vann**. (You must drink water.)

**melk** = milk
- Barn **drikker melk**. (Children drink milk.)

**juice/saft** = juice
- Vi **drikker juice**. (We drink juice.)

**øl** = beer
- De **drikker øl**. (They drink beer.)

**vin** = wine
- Vi **drikker vin** til middag. (We drink wine with dinner.)

---

## Common Questions

**Hva drikker du?** - What do you drink? / What are you drinking?

**Vil du drikke kaffe eller te?** - Do you want to drink coffee or tea?

**Har du drukket nok vann?** - Have you drunk enough water?

**Drikker du kaffe?** - Do you drink coffee?

**Vil du ha noe å drikke?** - Do you want something to drink?

---

## Offering Drinks

**Vil du ha...?** - Do you want...?
- **Vil du ha** kaffe? (Do you want coffee?)
- **Vil du ha** te? (Do you want tea?)
- **Vil du ha** noe å drikke? (Do you want something to drink?)

**Skal vi drikke...?** - Shall we drink...?
- **Skal vi drikke** kaffe? (Shall we drink coffee?)

---

## Special Note: Conjugation

**å drikke** is irregular in past tense:
- Present: drikker
- Past: **drakk** (irregular!)
- Past participle: **drukket** (irregular!)

Compare to regular verbs like "å snakke":
- snakker → snakket → snakket (all regular)
