# å kle (to dress / to clothe)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å kle

**Meaning**: To dress or to clothe (usually reflexive: å kle seg = to get dressed)

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | kler | I dress |
| du | kler | you dress |
| han/hun/det | kler | he/she/it dresses |
| vi | kler | we dress |
| dere | kler | you dress (plural) |
| de | kler | they dress |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **kler** meg. (I get dressed.)
- **Kler du** deg varmt? (Do you dress warmly?)
- Hun **kler** seg pent. (She dresses nicely.)
- Vi **kler** oss fort. (We get dressed quickly.)
- De **kler** seg for festen. (They're getting dressed for the party.)

---

| Person | Norsk | English |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **kledde** meg varmt. (I dressed warmly.)

**Pattern**: har + kledd

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|

| du | har kledd | you have dressed |
| han/hun/det | har kledd | he/she/it has dressed |
| vi | har kledd | we have dressed |
| dere | har kledd | you have dressed (plural) |
| de | har kledd | they have dressed |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har kledd** meg allerede. (I have already gotten dressed.)
- **Har du kledd** deg? (Have you gotten dressed?)
- Vi **har kledd** oss varmt. (We have dressed warmly.)

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + kle

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal kle / vil kle | I will dress / I'm going to get dressed |
| du | skal kle / vil kle | you will dress |
| han/hun/det | skal kle / vil kle | he/she/it will dress |
| vi | skal kle / vil kle | we will dress |
| dere | skal kle / vil kle | you will dress (plural) |
| de | skal kle / vil kle | they will dress |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal kle** meg nå. (I'm going to get dressed now.)
- **Vil du kle** deg varmt? (Will you dress warmly?)
- Vi **skal kle** oss pent. (We're going to dress nicely.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**kle** - dress!

### Eksempler:
- **Kle** deg! (Get dressed!)
- **Kle** deg varmt! (Dress warmly!)
- **Kle** deg fort! (Get dressed quickly!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **kler** meg **ikke** pent. (I don't dress nicely.)
- Vi **kledde** oss **ikke** varmt nok. (We didn't dress warmly enough.)
- Hun **har ikke kledd** seg ennå. (She hasn't gotten dressed yet.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **kle seg** - to get dressed (reflexive)
  - Jeg må **kle meg**. (I must get dressed.)
- **kle seg varmt** - to dress warmly
  - **Kle deg varmt**! (Dress warmly!)
- **kle seg pent** - to dress nicely
  - Hun **kler seg** alltid pent. (She always dresses nicely.)
- **kle seg om** - to change clothes
  - Jeg må **kle meg om**. (I must change clothes.)
- **kle av seg** - to undress / take off clothes
  - Jeg **kler av** meg. (I'm getting undressed.)
- **kle på seg** - to put on clothes / get dressed
  - Jeg **kler på** meg jakken. (I'm putting on my jacket.)

---

## Important: Reflexive Pattern

**å kle seg** is almost ALWAYS used reflexively!

Must conjugate "seg" according to person:
- Jeg kler **meg**. (I get dressed.)
- Du kler **deg**. (You get dressed.)
- Han/hun kler **seg**. (He/she gets dressed.)
- Vi kler **oss**. (We get dressed.)
- Dere kler **dere**. (You get dressed.)
- De kler **seg**. (They get dressed.)

---

## Common Uses

### 1. Getting dressed (most common)

- Jeg må **kle meg**. (I must get dressed.)
- Hun **kler seg** fort. (She gets dressed quickly.)
- **Kle deg**! (Get dressed!)
- Vi **kler oss** om morgenen. (We get dressed in the morning.)

### 2. Dressing a certain way

- Jeg **kler meg** varmt om vinteren. (I dress warmly in winter.)
- Hun **kler seg** alltid pent. (She always dresses nicely.)
- Vi **kler oss** komfortabelt. (We dress comfortably.)

### 3. Changing clothes

- Jeg må **kle meg om**. (I must change clothes.)
- Hun **kledde seg om** etter jobb. (She changed clothes after work.)

---

## kle seg vs kle av seg vs kle på seg

**å kle seg** = to get dressed (general)
- Jeg **kler meg**. (I'm getting dressed.)

**å kle av seg** = to undress / take off clothes
- Jeg **kler av meg**. (I'm getting undressed.)
- Hun **kledde av seg** jakken. (She took off her jacket.)

**å kle på seg** = to put on clothes / get dressed
- Jeg **kler på meg** jakken. (I'm putting on my jacket.)
- **Kle på deg** genseren! (Put on your sweater!)

---

## Common Adverbs with "kle seg"

**How you dress:**
- **kle seg varmt** - dress warmly
- **kle seg kaldt** - dress lightly (for cold)
- **kle seg pent** - dress nicely
- **kle seg fint** - dress nicely
- **kle seg komfortabelt** - dress comfortably
- **kle seg enkelt** - dress simply
- **kle seg godt** - dress well

**When you dress:**
- **kle seg fort** - get dressed quickly
- **kle seg sakte** - get dressed slowly
- **kle seg om morgenen** - get dressed in the morning

---

## Common Questions

**Har du kledd deg?** - Have you gotten dressed?

**Kler du deg varmt?** - Do you dress warmly?

**Hvordan kler du deg?** - How do you dress?

**Skal du kle deg om?** - Are you going to change clothes?

---

## Special Expressions

**Kle seg varmt** - Dress warmly
- Om vinteren må du **kle deg varmt**. (In winter you must dress warmly.)

**Kle seg om** - Change clothes
- Jeg må **kle meg om** før festen. (I must change clothes before the party.)

**Kle av seg** - Undress / Take off clothes
- Han **kledde av seg** jakken. (He took off his jacket.)

**Kle på seg** - Put on clothes
- **Kle på deg** varme klær! (Put on warm clothes!)

**Det kler deg!** - It suits you! / It looks good on you!
- Den jakken **kler deg**! (That jacket looks good on you!)

---

## Weather-Related Uses

Very common with weather and seasons!

- Om vinteren **kler jeg meg** varmt. (In winter I dress warmly.)
- Når det er kaldt må du **kle deg** godt. (When it's cold you must dress well.)
- Hun **kler seg** alltid riktig for været. (She always dresses right for the weather.)

---

## Time Expressions with "kle seg"

- **kle seg om morgenen** - get dressed in the morning
- **kle seg om kvelden** - get dressed in the evening
- **kle seg fort** - get dressed quickly
- **kle seg nå** - get dressed now
- **kle seg alltid** - always dress (a certain way)
