# å hate (to hate)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å hate

**Meaning**: To hate (strong dislike)

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | hater | I hate |
| du | hater | you hate |
| han/hun/det | hater | he/she/it hates |
| vi | hater | we hate |
| dere | hater | you hate (plural) |
| de | hater | they hate |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **hater** ikke noen. (I don't hate anyone.)
- **Hater du** fisk? (Do you hate fish?)
- Hun **hater** å vente. (She hates to wait / waiting.)
- Vi **hater** regn. (We hate rain.)
- De **hater** tidlig morgen. (They hate early morning.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | hatet | I hated |
| du | hatet | you hated |
| han/hun/det | hatet | he/she/it hated |
| vi | hatet | we hated |
| dere | hatet | you hated (plural) |
| de | hatet | they hated |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **hatet** den filmen. (I hated that movie.)
- **Hatet du** det? (Did you hate it?)
- Vi **hatet** været. (We hated the weather.)
- Hun **hatet** å gå til skolen. (She hated going to school.)

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + hatet

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har hatet | I have hated |
| du | har hatet | you have hated |
| han/hun/det | har hatet | he/she/it has hated |
| vi | har hatet | we have hated |
| dere | har hatet | you have hated (plural) |
| de | har hatet | they have hated |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har alltid hatet** å løpe. (I have always hated running.)
- **Har du hatet** det lenge? (Have you hated it for long?)
- Vi **har hatet** den politikken. (We have hated that policy.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**hat** - hate! (rarely used)

### Eksempler:
- **Hat** ikke! (Don't hate!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **hater ikke** noen. (I don't hate anyone.)
- Vi **hatet ikke** det. (We didn't hate it.)
- Hun **har ikke hatet** deg. (She hasn't hated you.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **hate å** + verb - to hate to (do something)
  - Jeg **hater å** vente. (I hate to wait / I hate waiting.)
- **hate når** - to hate when
  - Jeg **hater** når det regner. (I hate when it rains.)
- **hate det** - to hate it/that
  - Jeg **hater det**! (I hate it/that!)

---

## Common Uses

**Hating activities:**
- Jeg **hater** å løpe. (I hate to run / I hate running.)
- Hun **hater** å vente. (She hates waiting.)
- Vi **hater** å være sent ute. (We hate being late.)

**Hating food:**
- **Hater du** fisk? (Do you hate fish?)
- Jeg **hater** ikke noe mat. (I don't hate any food.)
- De **hater** ost. (They hate cheese.)

**Hating situations:**
- Jeg **hater** regn. (I hate rain.)
- Vi **hater** trafikk. (We hate traffic.)
- Hun **hater** å være alene. (She hates being alone.)

---

## Important: hate vs ikke like

**å hate** = to hate (very strong dislike)
- Jeg **hater** dette! (I hate this!) - very strong

**ikke like** = to not like (dislike, milder)
- Jeg **liker ikke** dette. (I don't like this.) - milder

Norwegians often use **"liker ikke"** instead of **"hater"** to be less extreme:
- Jeg **liker ikke** fisk. (I don't like fish.) - more common
- Jeg **hater** fisk. (I hate fish.) - stronger

---

## Pattern: hate å + infinitive

**hate å** = to hate to / to hate (doing something)

Always use **å** before the next verb:
- Jeg **hater å** vente. ✅ (I hate waiting.)
- Jeg **hater** vente. ❌

## Special Note

In Norwegian culture, saying "jeg hater" is considered quite strong and direct. Norwegians often prefer softer expressions:

**Instead of "jeg hater":**
- **Jeg liker ikke** - I don't like (more common)
- **Jeg er ikke glad i** - I'm not fond of (softer)
- **Jeg er ikke så glad i** - I'm not so fond of (even softer)

**Example:**
- Jeg **hater** fisk. (I hate fish.) - very strong
- Jeg **liker ikke** fisk. (I don't like fish.) - more common
- Jeg er **ikke så glad i** fisk. (I'm not so fond of fish.) - softer

---

## Common Questions

**Hater du...?** - Do you hate...?

**Hva hater du mest?** - What do you hate most?

**Hvorfor hater du det?** - Why do you hate it?

---

## Milder Alternatives

If you want to express dislike without using the strong word "hate":

1. **Jeg liker ikke...** (I don't like...)
2. **Jeg er ikke glad i...** (I'm not fond of...)
3. **Jeg er ikke så glad i...** (I'm not so fond of...)
4. **Jeg er ikke fan av...** (I'm not a fan of...)

These are all softer and more commonly used in everyday Norwegian!
