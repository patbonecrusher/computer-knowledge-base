# å like (to like)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å like

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | liker | I like |
| du | liker | you like |
| han/hun/det | liker | he/she/it likes |
| vi | liker | we like |
| dere | liker | you like (plural) |
| de | liker | they like |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **liker** norsk. (I like Norwegian.)
- **Hva liker du?** (What do you like?)
- Hun **liker** Norge. (She likes Norway.)
- Vi **liker** å snakke norsk. (We like to speak Norwegian.)
- De **liker** skolen. (They like school.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | likte | I liked |
| du | likte | you liked |
| han/hun/det | likte | he/she/it liked |
| vi | likte | we liked |
| dere | likte | you liked (plural) |
| de | likte | they liked |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **likte** filmen. (I liked the movie.)
- Vi **likte** maten. (We liked the food.)
- Hun **likte** ikke det. (She didn't like it.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + likt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har likt | I have liked |
| du | har likt | you have liked |
| han/hun/det | har likt | he/she/it has liked |
| vi | har likt | we have liked |
| dere | har likt | you have liked (plural) |
| de | har likt | they have liked |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har likt** Norge lenge. (I have liked Norway for a long time.)
- Vi **har likt** å lære norsk. (We have liked learning Norwegian.)
- Hun **har alltid likt** musikk. (She has always liked music.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + like

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal like / vil like | I will like / I'm going to like |
| du | skal like / vil like | you will like |
| han/hun/det | skal like / vil like | he/she/it will like |
| vi | skal like / vil like | we will like |
| dere | skal like / vil like | you will like (plural) |
| de | skal like / vil like | they will like |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg tror du **vil like** det. (I think you will like it.)
- De **skal like** Norge. (They are going to like Norway.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**lik** - like! (rarely used)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **liker ikke** kaffe. (I don't like coffee.)
- Vi **likte ikke** filmen. (We didn't like the movie.)
- Hun **har ikke likt** skolen. (She has not liked school.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Hva liker du?** - What do you like?
- **Jeg liker å...** - I like to... (+ verb)
  - Jeg liker å lese. (I like to read.)
  - Jeg liker å snakke norsk. (I like to speak Norwegian.)
- **Liker du...?** - Do you like...?
- **Jeg liker det** - I like it
- **Jeg liker deg** - I like you
- **godt likt** - well-liked (popular)

---

## Special Note: Different meanings

**like** can be:
1. **Verb**: å like (to like)
   - Jeg **liker** kaffe. (I like coffee.)

2. **Adverb**: like (just, equally, as)
   - Det er **like** bra. (It's just as good.)

3. **Adjective**: lik (similar, same)
   - Vi er **like**. (We are similar.)

Context determines the meaning!

---

## Common Patterns

**å like å** + [verb] = to like to [do something]
- Jeg **liker å** lese. (I like to read.)
- Hun **liker å** gå. (She likes to walk.)

**å like** + [noun] = to like [something]
- Jeg **liker** Norge. (I like Norway.)
- Vi **liker** norsk. (We like Norwegian.)
