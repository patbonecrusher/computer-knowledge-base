# å gå (to go / to walk)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å gå

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | går | I go / I walk |
| du | går | you go / you walk |
| han/hun/det | går | he/she/it goes / walks |
| vi | går | we go / we walk |
| dere | går | you go / you walk (plural) |
| de | går | they go / they walk |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **går** til skolen. (I go to school / I walk to school.)
- Du **går** hjem. (You go home.)
- Hun **går** på arbeid. (She goes to work.)
- Vi **går** til Oslo. (We are going to Oslo.)
- De **går** på mandag. (They go on Monday.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | gikk | I went / I walked |
| du | gikk | you went / you walked |
| han/hun/det | gikk | he/she/it went / walked |
| vi | gikk | we went / we walked |
| dere | gikk | you went / you walked (plural) |
| de | gikk | they went / they walked |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **gikk** til skolen i går. (I went to school yesterday.)
- Vi **gikk** hjem. (We went home.)
- Han **gikk** til Oslo. (He went to Oslo.)
- De **gikk** på tirsdag. (They went on Tuesday.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + gått

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har gått | I have gone / I have walked |
| du | har gått | you have gone / you have walked |
| han/hun/det | har gått | he/she/it has gone / has walked |
| vi | har gått | we have gone / we have walked |
| dere | har gått | you have gone / you have walked (plural) |
| de | har gått | they have gone / they have walked |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har gått** til skolen. (I have walked to school.)
- Hun **har gått** hjem. (She has gone home.)
- Vi **har gått** langt. (We have walked far.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + gå

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal gå / vil gå | I will go / I'm going to walk |
| du | skal gå / vil gå | you will go |
| han/hun/det | skal gå / vil gå | he/she/it will go |
| vi | skal gå / vil gå | we will go |
| dere | skal gå / vil gå | you will go (plural) |
| de | skal gå / vil gå | they will go |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal gå** til skolen i morgen. (I will go to school tomorrow.)
- Vi **skal gå** hjem nå. (We are going to go home now.)
- De **vil gå** til Oslo. (They want to go to Oslo.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**gå** - go! / walk!

### Eksempler:
- **Gå** nå! (Go now!)
- **Gå** hjem! (Go home!)
- **Gå** til skolen! (Go to school!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **går ikke** til skolen. (I don't go to school.)
- Vi **gikk ikke** hjem. (We didn't go home.)
- Hun **har ikke gått** til Oslo. (She has not gone to Oslo.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Hvordan går det?** - How's it going? (literally: How goes it?)
- **Det går bra** - It's going well
- **Går du til...?** - Are you going to...?
- **Jeg går til...** - I'm going to...
- **Det går an** - It's possible (literally: It goes on)
- **Det går ikke** - It doesn't work / That won't work
- **Går det bra?** - Is it going well? / Are you okay?

---

## Special Note: "gå" vs "dra"

**å gå** = to go/walk (usually on foot, or general movement)
**å dra** = to go/leave (often by vehicle, or leaving/departing)

- Jeg **går** til butikken. (I walk to the store.)
- Jeg **drar** til Oslo. (I'm going/traveling to Oslo.)
