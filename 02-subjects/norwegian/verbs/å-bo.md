# å bo (to live / to reside)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å bo

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | bor | I live |
| du | bor | you live |
| han/hun/det | bor | he/she/it lives |
| vi | bor | we live |
| dere | bor | you live (plural) |
| de | bor | they live |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **bor** i Oslo. (I live in Oslo.)
- **Hvor bor du?** (Where do you live?)
- Hun **bor** i Bergen. (She lives in Bergen.)
- Vi **bor** i Norge. (We live in Norway.)
- De **bor** her. (They live here.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | bodde | I lived |
| du | bodde | you lived |
| han/hun/det | bodde | he/she/it lived |
| vi | bodde | we lived |
| dere | bodde | you lived (plural) |
| de | bodde | they lived |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **bodde** i Oslo før. (I lived in Oslo before.)
- Vi **bodde** i Canada. (We lived in Canada.)
- Han **bodde** her i to år. (He lived here for two years.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + bodd

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har bodd | I have lived |
| du | har bodd | you have lived |
| han/hun/det | har bodd | he/she/it has lived |
| vi | har bodd | we have lived |
| dere | har bodd | you have lived (plural) |
| de | har bodd | they have lived |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har bodd** i Norge i fem år. (I have lived in Norway for five years.)
- Hun **har bodd** mange steder. (She has lived in many places.)
- Vi **har bodd** her lenge. (We have lived here for a long time.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + bo

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal bo / vil bo | I will live / I'm going to live |
| du | skal bo / vil bo | you will live |
| han/hun/det | skal bo / vil bo | he/she/it will live |
| vi | skal bo / vil bo | we will live |
| dere | skal bo / vil bo | you will live (plural) |
| de | skal bo / vil bo | they will live |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal bo** i Oslo. (I'm going to live in Oslo.)
- Hvor **vil du bo**? (Where do you want to live?)
- Vi **skal bo** i Norge. (We will live in Norway.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**bo** - live!

### Eksempler:
- **Bo** her! (Live here!)
- **Bo** i Norge! (Live in Norway!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **bor ikke** i Oslo. (I don't live in Oslo.)
- Vi **bodde ikke** der. (We didn't live there.)
- Hun **har ikke bodd** her. (She has not lived here.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Hvor bor du?** - Where do you live?
- **Jeg bor i...** - I live in...
- **Bor du alene?** - Do you live alone?
- **Bor du sammen med...?** - Do you live together with...?
- **Jeg bor hjemme** - I live at home (with parents)

---

## Special Note: "bo" vs "leve"

**å bo** = to live/reside (where you live)
- Jeg **bor** i Oslo. (I live in Oslo.)

**å leve** = to live (to be alive, way of life)
- Jeg **lever** et godt liv. (I live a good life.)
- Vi **lever** i Norge. (We are living in Norway.)

**When talking about location, use "bo"!**

---

## Common Prepositions with "bo"

- **bo i** [city/country] - live in
  - Jeg bor **i** Oslo. (I live in Oslo.)

- **bo på** [street/island] - live on
  - Jeg bor **på** Storgata. (I live on Storgata.)

- **bo hos** [person] - live with/at someone's place
  - Jeg bor **hos** venner. (I live with friends.)
