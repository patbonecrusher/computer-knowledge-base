# å si (to say)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å si

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | sier | I say |
| du | sier | you say |
| han/hun/det | sier | he/she/it says |
| vi | sier | we say |
| dere | sier | you say (plural) |
| de | sier | they say |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **sier** ingenting. (I say nothing.)
- **Hva sier du?** (What are you saying? / What do you say?)
- Hun **sier** at hun kommer. (She says that she's coming.)
- Vi **sier** takk. (We say thank you.)
- De **sier** alltid ja. (They always say yes.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | sa | I said |
| du | sa | you said |
| han/hun/det | sa | he/she/it said |
| vi | sa | we said |
| dere | sa | you said (plural) |
| de | sa | they said |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **sa** ingenting. (I said nothing.)
- **Hva sa du?** (What did you say?)
- Hun **sa** at hun ville komme. (She said that she would come.)
- Vi **sa** takk. (We said thank you.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + sagt

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har sagt | I have said |
| du | har sagt | you have said |
| han/hun/det | har sagt | he/she/it has said |
| vi | har sagt | we have said |
| dere | har sagt | you have said (plural) |
| de | har sagt | they have said |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har sagt** det mange ganger. (I have said it many times.)
- **Hva har du sagt?** (What have you said?)
- Vi **har sagt** farvel. (We have said goodbye.)
- Hun **har ikke sagt** noe. (She hasn't said anything.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + si

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal si / vil si | I will say / I'm going to say |
| du | skal si / vil si | you will say |
| han/hun/det | skal si / vil si | he/she/it will say |
| vi | skal si / vil si | we will say |
| dere | skal si / vil si | you will say (plural) |
| de | skal si / vil si | they will say |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal si** det. (I'm going to say it.)
- **Hva vil du si?** (What do you want to say?)
- Vi **må si** noe. (We must say something.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**si** - say!

### Eksempler:
- **Si** noe! (Say something!)
- **Si** det! (Say it!)
- **Si** takk! (Say thank you!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **sier ikke** noe. (I don't say anything / I'm not saying anything.)
- Vi **sa ikke** det. (We didn't say that.)
- Hun **har ikke sagt** farvel. (She hasn't said goodbye.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **Hva sier du?** - What are you saying? / What do you say?
- **si takk** - to say thank you
- **si ja** - to say yes
- **si nei** - to say no
- **si farvel** - to say goodbye
- **si hei** - to say hi
- **si fra** - to let know / to speak up
  - **Si fra** hvis du trenger hjelp. (Let me know if you need help.)
- **si i fra** - to speak up / to say something
- **si opp** - to quit (a job)
  - Jeg skal **si opp** jobben min. (I'm going to quit my job.)

---

## Common Phrases

**What to say:**
- **si ingenting** - say nothing
- **si noe** - say something
- **si sannheten** - tell the truth / say the truth
- **si ifra** - let know / speak up

**When greeting:**
- **si hei** - say hi
- **si ha det** - say goodbye

**Opinions:**
- **Hva sier du om...?** - What do you say about...?
- **Jeg sier ikke noe** - I'm not saying anything

---

## Special Expressions

**si fra** / **si ifra** = let know / speak up
- **Si fra** hvis du trenger noe. (Let me know if you need anything.)

**si opp** = quit (a job)
- Hun skal **si opp** jobben. (She's going to quit the job.)

**det vil si** = that is to say / i.e.
- Jeg kommer i morgen, **det vil si** på mandag. (I'm coming tomorrow, that is to say on Monday.)

**som sagt** = as I said / as mentioned
- **Som sagt**, jeg kommer i morgen. (As I said, I'm coming tomorrow.)

---

## Special Note: Conjugation

**å si** is irregular and very short!:
- Present: sier
- Past: **sa** (irregular!)
- Past participle: **sagt** (irregular!)

Pattern: si → sa → sagt

---

## Related Verbs

**å si** = to say (specific words)
- **Hva sier du?** (What do you say?)

**å snakke** = to speak / to talk
- Jeg **snakker** norsk. (I speak Norwegian.)

**å fortelle** = to tell (a story/information)
- Jeg **forteller** en historie. (I'm telling a story.)
