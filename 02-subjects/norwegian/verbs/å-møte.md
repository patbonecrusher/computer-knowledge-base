# å møte (to meet)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å møte

**Meaning**: To meet (someone)

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | møter | I meet |
| du | møter | you meet |
| han/hun/det | møter | he/she/it meets |
| vi | møter | we meet |
| dere | møter | you meet (plural) |
| de | møter | they meet |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **møter** venner i dag. (I meet friends today.)
- **Møter du** han i morgen? (Are you meeting him tomorrow?)
- Hun **møter** meg på kafé. (She's meeting me at the café.)
- Vi **møter** ofte. (We meet often.)
- De **møter** familien sin. (They're meeting their family.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**møt** - meet!

### Eksempler:
- **Møt** meg her! (Meet me here!)
- **Møt** opp klokka åtte! (Show up at eight o'clock!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **møter ikke** venner i dag. (I'm not meeting friends today.)
- Vi **møtte ikke** noen. (We didn't meet anyone.)
- Han **har ikke møtt** henne. (He hasn't met her.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **møte noen** - to meet someone
  - Jeg **møter** venner. (I'm meeting friends.)
- **møte på** - to meet at (a place)
  - Vi **møter på** kafé. (We're meeting at the café.)
- **møte opp** - to show up / to arrive
  - Du må **møte opp** klokka åtte. (You must show up at eight o'clock.)
- **møtes** - to meet (each other) - reflexive
  - Vi **møtes** i morgen. (We'll meet tomorrow.)
- **møte igjen** - to meet again
  - Vi **møtes igjen** snart! (We'll meet again soon!)

---

## Common Uses

**Meeting people:**
- Jeg **møter** venner i dag. (I'm meeting friends today.)
- Vi **møter** familien. (We're meeting the family.)
- Skal du **møte** noen? (Are you meeting someone?)
- Hun **møter** ham på kafé. (She's meeting him at the café.)

**Meeting at a place:**
- Vi **møtes på** stasjonen. (We're meeting at the station.)
- **Møt** meg på kafé! (Meet me at the café!)
- Hvor skal vi **møtes**? (Where shall we meet?)

**Meeting at a time:**
- Vi **møtes** klokka tre. (We're meeting at three o'clock.)
- Jeg **møter** deg om en time. (I'll meet you in an hour.)
- **Møt** meg klokka åtte! (Meet me at eight o'clock!)

---

## Important Pattern: møte vs møtes

**møte** = to meet (someone specific)
- Jeg **møter** henne. (I'm meeting her.)
- Du **møter** han. (You're meeting him.)

**møtes** = to meet (each other) - reciprocal/reflexive
- Vi **møtes** i morgen. (We'll meet tomorrow.)
- Skal vi **møtes**? (Shall we meet?)
- De **møtes** på kafé. (They're meeting at the café.)

**møtes** is often used when two or more people are meeting each other!

---

## Common Questions

**Hvem møter du?** - Who are you meeting?

**Når skal vi møtes?** - When shall we meet?

**Hvor skal vi møtes?** - Where shall we meet?

**Har du møtt henne før?** - Have you met her before?

**Møter du noen i dag?** - Are you meeting anyone today?

---

## Special Expressions

**Møte opp** - Show up / Arrive
- Du må **møte opp** til tiden. (You must show up on time.)
- Han **møtte ikke opp**. (He didn't show up.)

**Møtes igjen** - Meet again / See you again
- Vi **møtes igjen** snart! (We'll meet again soon!)
- **Møtes igjen**! (See you again!)

**Møte på** - Meet at / Encounter
- Vi **møtes på** stasjonen. (We're meeting at the station.)
- Jeg **møtte på** problemer. (I encountered problems.)

**Hyggelig å møte deg!** - Nice to meet you!
- **Hyggelig å møte deg!** (Nice to meet you!)
