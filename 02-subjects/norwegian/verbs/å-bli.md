# å bli (to become / to get / to stay)

**Type**: Irregular verb
**Infinitive**: å bli

**Meanings**:
1. To become, to get, or to turn into (change of state)
2. To stay or to remain (in a place)

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | blir | I become / I get / I stay |
| du | blir | you become / you get / you stay |
| han/hun/det | blir | he/she/it becomes / gets / stays |
| vi | blir | we become / we get / we stay |
| dere | blir | you become / you get / you stay (plural) |
| de | blir | they become / they get / they stay |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Det **blir** kaldt. (It's getting cold.) - *becoming/getting*
- Jeg **blir** glad. (I become/get happy.) - *becoming/getting*
- **Blir du** lærer? (Are you becoming a teacher?) - *becoming*
- Hun **blir** varm. (She's getting warm.) - *getting*
- Vi **blir** hjemme. (We're staying home.) - *staying*
- Jeg **blir** i sengen i dag. (I'm staying in bed today.) - *staying*
- **Blir du** her? (Are you staying here?) - *staying*
- Været **blir** bedre. (The weather is getting better.) - *getting*

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**bli** - become! / stay!

### Eksempler:
- **Bli** her! (Stay here!)
- **Bli** med meg! (Come with me! / Join me!)
- **Bli** ikke kald! (Don't get cold!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Det **blir ikke** kaldt. (It's not getting cold.)
- Jeg **ble ikke** lærer. (I didn't become a teacher.)
- Hun **har ikke blitt** syk. (She hasn't gotten sick.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **bli + adjective** - to become/get + adjective
  - Det **blir** kaldt. (It's getting cold.)
- **bli + noun** - to become + noun (profession/role)
  - Jeg **blir** lærer. (I'm becoming a teacher.)
- **bli med** - to come with / to join
  - **Bli med** meg! (Come with me!)
- **bli hjemme** - to stay home
  - Vi **blir** hjemme i dag. (We're staying home today.)
- **bli til** - to become (turn into)
  - Vannet **blir til** is. (The water turns into ice.)

---

## Four Main Uses

### 1. Weather changes (very common!)

- Det **blir** kaldt. (It's getting cold.)
- Det **blir** varmere. (It's getting warmer.)
- Været **blir** bedre. (The weather is getting better.)
- Det **blir** mørkt. (It's getting dark.)
- Det **blir** snart vinter. (It will soon be winter.)

### 2. Becoming something (profession, state)

- Jeg **blir** lærer. (I'm becoming a teacher.)
- Hun **blir** lege. (She's becoming a doctor.)
- De **blir** voksne. (They're becoming adults.)
- Hva **vil du bli**? (What do you want to become?)

### 3. Getting/becoming an adjective (change of state)

- Jeg **blir** glad. (I'm getting happy.)
- Du **blir** varm. (You're getting warm.)
- Vi **blir** trøtte. (We're getting tired.)
- Hun **blir** syk. (She's getting sick.)

### 4. Staying/remaining (in a place) - VERY COMMON!

- Jeg **blir** hjemme. (I'm staying home.)
- Vi **blir** i Norge. (We're staying in Norway.)
- **Bli** her! (Stay here!)
- Jeg **blir** i sengen i dag. (I'm staying in bed today.)
- Skal du **bli** lenge? (Are you staying long?)
- Vi **ble** på hotellet. (We stayed at the hotel.)
- Hun **blir** hos venner. (She's staying with friends.)

---

## Important: bli vs være

### When bli = becoming/getting (change):

**å bli** = to become / to get (change)
- Det **blir** kaldt. (It's getting cold.) - changing
- Jeg **blir** glad. (I'm getting happy.) - changing

**å være** = to be (state)
- Det **er** kaldt. (It is cold.) - current state
- Jeg **er** glad. (I am happy.) - current state

The difference is that **bli** implies a change, while **være** describes a current state!

### When bli = staying/remaining:

**å bli** = to stay / to remain (in a place)
- Jeg **blir** hjemme. (I'm staying home.)
- Vi **ble** i Norge. (We stayed in Norway.)

**å være** = to be (location/state)
- Jeg **er** hjemme. (I am home.) - current location
- Vi **var** i Norge. (We were in Norway.) - past location

The difference here is that **bli** emphasizes the decision to stay or remain, while **være** just states the location!

## Special Expressions

### Staying/Remaining Expressions:

**Bli hjemme** - Stay home
- Jeg **blir hjemme** i dag. (I'm staying home today.)
- Vi **ble hjemme** hele helgen. (We stayed home all weekend.)

**Bli i sengen** - Stay in bed
- Jeg **blir i sengen** i dag. (I'm staying in bed today.)
- Hun **ble i sengen** til klokka tolv. (She stayed in bed until twelve o'clock.)

**Bli her** - Stay here
- **Bli her!** (Stay here!)
- Skal du **bli her** lenge? (Are you staying here long?)

**Bli (i + place)** - Stay in/at (a place)
- Vi **blir i** Norge i sommer. (We're staying in Norway this summer.)
- Jeg **ble på** hotellet. (I stayed at the hotel.)
- Hun **blir hos** venner. (She's staying with friends.)

### Other Common Expressions:

**Bli med** - Come with / Join
- **Bli med** på kino! (Come with me to the cinema!)
- Vil du **bli med**? (Do you want to come/join?)

**Bli borte** - Go away / Disappear
- Snøen **blir borte** om våren. (The snow goes away in spring.)

**Bli til** - Turn into / Become
- Is **blir til** vann. (Ice turns into water.)

**Hva blir det?** - What will it be?
- Used when ordering food or asking about outcome

**Bli kvitt** - Get rid of
- Jeg vil **bli kvitt** dette. (I want to get rid of this.)

- **Det blir** kaldere. (It's getting colder.)
- **Det blir** varmere. (It's getting warmer.)
- **Det blir** bedre vær. (The weather is getting better.)

- **Det blir** regn. (It will rain / Rain is coming.)
- **Det blir** snø. (It will snow / Snow is coming.)
- **Det blir** vind. (It will be windy.)
- **Det blir** mørkt. (It's getting dark.)
- **Det blir** lyst. (It's getting light.)

## Summary: Two Main Meanings of "bli"

**å bli** has TWO distinct meanings - both very common!

### Meaning 1: To become / To get (CHANGE)
Used for changes, transformations, weather changes:
- Det **blir** kaldt. (It's **getting** cold.)
- Jeg **blir** lærer. (I'm **becoming** a teacher.)
- Hun **blir** glad. (She's **getting** happy.)

### Meaning 2: To stay / To remain (LOCATION)
Used for staying in a place:
- Jeg **blir** hjemme. (I'm **staying** home.)
- **Bli** her! (**Stay** here!)
- Vi **ble** i sengen. (We **stayed** in bed.)

The same verb form, two different meanings - context tells you which one!

---

## Conjugation Pattern

**å bli** is IRREGULAR:

- Infinitive: **bli**
- Present: **blir** (all persons same)
- Past: **ble** (all persons same)
- Past Participle: **blitt**

Pattern: bli → blir → ble → blitt

This is an important verb to memorize!
