# å lese (to read)

**Type**: Regular verb
**Infinitive**: å lese

---

## Presens (Present Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | leser | I read |
| du | leser | you read |
| han/hun/det | leser | he/she/it reads |
| vi | leser | we read |
| dere | leser | you read (plural) |
| de | leser | they read |

### Eksempler (Examples):
- Jeg **leser** en bok. (I read a book / I'm reading a book.)
- **Hva leser du?** (What are you reading?)
- Hun **leser** norsk. (She's reading Norwegian / She reads Norwegian.)
- Vi **leser** avisen. (We read the newspaper.)
- De **leser** ofte. (They read often.)

---

## Preteritum (Past Tense)

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | leste | I read (past) |
| du | leste | you read (past) |
| han/hun/det | leste | he/she/it read (past) |
| vi | leste | we read (past) |
| dere | leste | you read (past - plural) |
| de | leste | they read (past) |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **leste** boken i går. (I read the book yesterday.)
- **Hva leste du?** (What did you read?)
- Vi **leste** sammen. (We read together.)
- Hun **leste** hele boken. (She read the whole book.)

---

## Presens perfektum (Present Perfect)

**Pattern**: har + lest

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | har lest | I have read |
| du | har lest | you have read |
| han/hun/det | har lest | he/she/it has read |
| vi | har lest | we have read |
| dere | har lest | you have read (plural) |
| de | har lest | they have read |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **har lest** mange bøker. (I have read many books.)
- **Har du lest** denne boken? (Have you read this book?)
- Vi **har lest** hele artikkelen. (We have read the whole article.)
- Hun **har lest** norsk i to år. (She has been reading Norwegian for two years.)

---

## Framtid (Future)

**Pattern**: skal/vil + lese

| Person | Norsk | English |
|--------|-------|---------|
| jeg | skal lese / vil lese | I will read / I'm going to read |
| du | skal lese / vil lese | you will read |
| han/hun/det | skal lese / vil lese | he/she/it will read |
| vi | skal lese / vil lese | we will read |
| dere | skal lese / vil lese | you will read (plural) |
| de | skal lese / vil lese | they will read |

### Eksempler:
- Jeg **skal lese** boken i morgen. (I'm going to read the book tomorrow.)
- **Vil du lese** med meg? (Will you read with me?)
- Vi **må lese** dette. (We must read this.)

---

## Imperativ (Imperative)

**les** - read!

### Eksempler:
- **Les** boken! (Read the book!)
- **Les** høyt! (Read aloud!)
- **Les** dette! (Read this!)

---

## Nektelse (Negation)

Add **ikke** after the verb:

- Jeg **leser ikke** mye. (I don't read much.)
- Vi **leste ikke** boken. (We didn't read the book.)
- Hun **har ikke lest** avisen. (She hasn't read the newspaper.)

---

## Nyttige Uttrykk (Useful Expressions)

- **lese en bok** - to read a book
- **lese avisen** - to read the newspaper
- **lese høyt** - to read aloud
- **lese for** - to read for/to (someone)
  - Jeg **leser for** barna. (I read to the children.)
- **lese om** - to read about
  - Jeg **leser om** Norge. (I'm reading about Norway.)
- **lese opp** - to read out
- **lese gjennom** - to read through

---

## What You Can Read

**bok** = book (en bok)
- Jeg **leser en bok**. (I'm reading a book.)

**avis** = newspaper (en avis)
- Vi **leser avisen**. (We read the newspaper.)

**artikkel** = article (en artikkel)
- Hun **leser en artikkel**. (She's reading an article.)

**brev** = letter (et brev)
- Jeg **leser et brev**. (I'm reading a letter.)

**tekst** = text (en tekst)
- Du må **lese teksten**. (You must read the text.)

**melding** = message (en melding)
- Jeg **leser meldinger**. (I'm reading messages.)

---

## Common Questions

**Hva leser du?** - What are you reading?

**Har du lest denne boken?** - Have you read this book?

**Leser du mye?** - Do you read a lot?

**Liker du å lese?** - Do you like to read?

**Kan du lese norsk?** - Can you read Norwegian?

---

## Prepositions with "lese"

**lese om** = read about
- Jeg **leser om** Norge. (I'm reading about Norway.)

**lese for** = read to/for (someone)
- Jeg **leser for** barna. (I read to the children.)

**lese i** = read in (a book/paper)
- Jeg **leser i** avisen. (I'm reading in the newspaper.)

**lese på** = read in (a language)
- Jeg kan **lese på** norsk. (I can read in Norwegian.)

---

## Related Expressions

**Jeg liker å lese** - I like to read

**Du bør lese mer** - You should read more

**Kan du lese dette?** - Can you read this?

**Les høyt!** - Read aloud!

**God lesing!** - Happy reading! / Enjoy your reading!

---

## Special Note: "lese" for Studying

**å lese** can also mean "to study" (especially at university level):

- Jeg **leser** norsk ved universitetet. (I study Norwegian at the university.)
- Hun **leser** medisin. (She studies medicine.)
- Hva **leser** du? (What do you study? / What are you studying?)

**Context** determines if it means "read" or "study"!
