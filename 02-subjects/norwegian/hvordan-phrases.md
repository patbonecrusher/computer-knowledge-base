# Hvordan Phrases: Common Norwegian Greetings

Understanding the subtle differences between similar "how are you" phrases in Norwegian.

---

## Hvordan har du det?

**Literal translation**: "How do you have it?"

### Characteristics:
- More **personal** - focuses on YOU as a person
- Asks about your personal state/well-being
- Slightly more **formal/traditional**
- Traditional greeting with genuine concern

### Common responses:
- **Bra, takk** (Fine, thanks)
- **Jeg har det bra** (I'm doing well)
- **Veldig bra, takk** (Very well, thanks)

### When to use:
- When you haven't seen someone in a while
- Showing genuine concern for someone's well-being
- More traditional or formal settings
- With older generations

### Example dialogues:
```
A: Hei! Hvordan har du det?
B: Bra, takk! Og du?
A: Jeg har det også bra.
```

---

## Hvordan går det?

**Literal translation**: "How goes it?"

### Characteristics:
- More **situational** - focuses on how things are going
- Asks about your circumstances/how life is treating you
- Slightly more **casual/modern**
- Very common in everyday speech
- Can be used more broadly (about situations, not just people)

### Common responses:
- **Det går bra** (It's going well)
- **Bra** (Good)
- **Bare bra!** (Just fine!)
- **Det går greit** (It's going okay)

### When to use:
- Casual greeting with friends/colleagues
- Everyday conversation
- Can refer to specific situations
- Modern, conversational Norwegian

### Example dialogues:
```
A: Hei! Hvordan går det?
B: Det går bra, takk!
A: Fint!
```

### Asking about specific situations:
- **Hvordan går det med jobben?** - How's it going with work?
- **Hvordan går det med studiene?** - How's it going with studies?
- **Hvordan går det med prosjektet?** - How's it going with the project?

---

## Quick Comparison

| Aspect | Hvordan har du det? | Hvordan går det? |
|--------|---------------------|------------------|
| **Focus** | YOU (the person) | IT (the situation) |
| **Tone** | Slightly more formal | Slightly more casual |
| **Usage** | Traditional | Modern/Common |
| **Translation** | How are you? | How's it going? |
| **Response** | Bra, takk | Det går bra |

---

## Other Related Phrases

### Hvordan går det med deg?
"How's it going with you?"
- Combines both approaches
- Very friendly and personal
- Common in casual conversation

### Har du det bra?
"Do you have it good?" / "Are you doing well?"
- Direct question about well-being
- Shows genuine concern
- Expects yes/no answer
- Response: **Ja, jeg har det bra** (Yes, I'm doing well)

### Går det bra?
"Is it going well?"
- Quick check-in
- Very casual
- Can be used when you sense something might be wrong
- Response: **Ja, det går bra** (Yes, it's going well)

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Both are correct and interchangeable** in most situations
2. **"Hvordan går det?"** is probably more common in everyday casual conversation
3. **"Hvordan har du det?"** shows a bit more personal concern
4. You can respond to either one with **"Bra, takk!"** - it works for both
5. When in doubt, use **"Hvordan går det?"** - it's the safe, modern choice

---

## Practice Responses

### Positive responses:
- **Bra, takk!** - Good, thanks!
- **Veldig bra!** - Very good!
- **Det går kjempebra!** - It's going really well! (informal)
- **Bare bra!** - Just fine!
- **Fint, takk!** - Fine, thanks!

### Neutral responses:
- **Det går greit** - It's going okay
- **Sånn passe** - So-so
- **Det går** - It's going (implies "okay, not great")

### Not-so-good responses:
- **Ikke så bra** - Not so good
- **Det har vært bedre** - It's been better
- **Litt sliten** - A bit tired
